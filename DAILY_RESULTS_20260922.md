## SZ-20260922-001 — uniform sharp inverse and compound bounds

Let \(p\equiv1\pmod {12}\) be prime and let
\(W=(p;a,Y,Z)\), \(a<Y<Z\), be any positive integral Erdős–Straus witness in
the original exterior or middle coordinate domain. Put

\[
\ell=(p,a,Y,Z),\qquad S=p+a+Y+Z,\qquad
H_W(X)=-S^{-1}\prod_{j=0}^3(X-\ell_j),
\]

write \(d_j=H_W'(\ell_j)\), and retain a square root
\(\xi_j^2d_j=1\) for each labelled root. The literal receiver is

\[
Oe_j=\xi_j
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\-id_j\\-S^{-1}d_j^2+2\ell_jd_j\\
i(7\ell_j^2d_j-13d_j^2)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

With the actual witness residual \(R=4a-p\), \(w=p/R\), and

\[
\gamma=\frac{80}{6279^4},
\]

every such witness satisfies

\[
\|O^{-1}\|_2<\frac{360}{\gamma}p\sqrt w
\le\frac{120\sqrt3}{\gamma}p^{3/2},
\]

\[
\|\wedge^2O^{-1}\|_2<\frac{180}{\gamma},\qquad
\|\wedge^3O^{-1}\|_2<\frac{41}{4\gamma p^2},\qquad
|\det O^{-1}|<\frac1{4\gamma p^2S^3}.
\]

If \(s_1\ge s_2\ge s_3\ge s_4>0\) are the singular values of \(O\), then

\[
s_3>\sqrt{\gamma/180}.
\]

Consequently

\[
O^{-1}=u\varphi+B,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(u\varphi)\le1,\qquad
\|B\|_2<\sqrt{180/\gamma}.
\]

Thus no more than one inverse singular direction can become unbounded. The
line \(u\varphi\) may vary with the witness.

- **Proof:** [Theorem 1.1, coefficient margins, cofactor bounds and compound estimates](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-foundation/blob/e713c342fc16e361e104de3322790ca0cee7281d/research/incoming/es-turn07-sharp-receiver-growth-20260921/core.tex#L75-L443).

## SZ-20260922-002 — exact receiver defect and two exponent-sharp prime families

Extend the same typed receiver along the inclusion of the integral-witness
locus into the ambient space of four distinct positive labelled roots
\(\ell=(p,a,Y,Z)\).  This ambient calculation does not impose the reciprocal
identity.  Put \(S=p+a+Y+Z\), let \(e_2\) be the second elementary symmetric
sum of the four roots, and define

\[
P_2(x)=x^2+\frac{19S}{7}x+\frac{e_2}{2}-\frac{10S^2}{7},
\qquad
\mathfrak h=\sum_j\frac{P_2(\ell_j)}{d_j^2}.
\]

With \(R_1,R_2,R_3\) and
\(\mathfrak r_\nu=\sum_jR_\nu(\ell_j)/d_j^2\) as defined in ES3, the
exceptional locus is exactly

\[
\mathfrak h=0.
\]

On its complement, the Lagrange interpolation calculation gives the complete
rational inverse. On \(\mathfrak h=0\), the last three receiver rows still
have rank three. Therefore the receiver has rank exactly three, with kernel
generated by

\[
\left(\xi_j^{-1}\frac{P_2(\ell_j)}{d_j^2}\right)_{j=0}^3,
\]

and image equal to the hyperplane

\[
\left\{b\in\mathbb C^4:
\frac{b_0}{A}-\mathfrak r_1b_1-\mathfrak r_2b_2-
\mathfrak r_3b_3=0\right\},
\qquad A=-S^{-1}.
\]

The obstruction is therefore itself an exact rank-three object, not a
discarded failure locus.  The global sign theorem proves that this ambient
divisor does not meet the positive integral witness locus of SZ-20260922-001.

For every prime \(p\equiv13\pmod {24}\), the integral family

\[
a=\frac{p+3}{4},\qquad
Y=\frac{pa+2}{3},\qquad
Z=\frac{pa(pa+2)}6
\]

has

\[
s_1\sim\frac5{221184}p^{12},\quad
s_2\sim\frac1{72}p^4,\quad
s_3\sim\frac1{2\sqrt2}p^{3/2},\quad
s_4\sim4\sqrt2\,p^{-3/2},
\]

and hence

\[
p^{-3/2}\|O^{-1}\|_2\to\frac1{4\sqrt2},\qquad
\|\wedge^2O^{-1}\|_2\to\frac12,\qquad
p^4\|\wedge^3O^{-1}\|_2\to36.
\]

For the following branch-dependent vector formulas, choose positive real
square roots for \(d_a,d_Y>0\) and positive-imaginary square roots for
\(d_p,d_Z<0\), in root order \((p,a,Y,Z)\).  Then its normalized inverse and
heat operator converge to

\[
p^{-3/2}O^{-1}\longrightarrow
\frac18(-i,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}e_0^*,\qquad
e^{-tp^3O^*O}\longrightarrow e^{-32t}vv^*,\quad
v=\frac{(-i,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}}{\sqrt2},\quad t>0.
\]

Other retained square-root choices act by diagonal signs on these vectors;
the singular values and compound norms are unchanged.

The second family

\[
a=\frac{3p+1}{8},\qquad Y=2a,\qquad Z=2pa
\]

has

\[
s_1\sim\frac{135}{16}p^6,\quad
s_2\sim\frac{\sqrt{991}}{32}p^2,\quad
s_3\sim\frac{5\sqrt3}{\sqrt{991}}p,\quad
s_4\sim\frac{38}{5\sqrt3}p^{-1},
\]

so

\[
p^{-1}\|O^{-1}\|_2\to\frac{5\sqrt3}{38},\qquad
\|\wedge^2O^{-1}\|_2\to\frac{\sqrt{991}}{38},\qquad
p^2\|\wedge^3O^{-1}\|_2\to\frac{16}{19}.
\]

Together the two actual prime families attain the exponents in
SZ-20260922-001.

## SZ-20260922-004 — rational trace normalization and complete primitive-ray deletion

Fix a prime \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), \(p/4<a<p/2\), \(R=4a-p\), and an original
word \(u\mid a^2\). Put

\[
g=\gcd(a,u),\qquad (h,r,s)=\left(\frac{g^2}{u},\frac ug,\frac ag\right),\qquad
a=hrs,\quad u=hr^2,\quad\gcd(r,s)=1.
\]

Every positive rational ES tail pair at the integral first denominator \(a\)
with integral sum occurs, up to a retained exterior swap, in one of the two
forms

\[
E:\left(a,\frac{pa+a^2/u}{R},\frac{pa+p^2u}{R}\right),\qquad
M:\left(a,\frac{p(a+a^2/u)}R,\frac{p(a+u)}R\right).
\]

Writing \(G_E=4u+1\), \(G_M=p+4u\), and choosing
\(\chi\in\{E,M\}\), the trace condition in channel \(\chi\) is exactly

\[
R\mid G_\chi^2,\qquad d=\frac{R}{\gcd(R,G_\chi)},\qquad d^2\mid R,
\]

where \(d\) is the common reduced tail denominator. For an exterior trace
source, set \(m=4rs\), \(T=pr+s\). A divisor \(k\mid R\) gives a
ray-preserving original exterior return at \(R'=R/k\) exactly when

\[
d\mid k,\qquad k\equiv1\pmod m.
\]

The target is

\[
h'=\frac{p+R/k}{m},\qquad a'=h'rs,\qquad u'=h'r^2,\qquad
\kappa'=\frac{T}{R/k}.
\]

For a proper source \(d>1\), \(a'<a\), and every centred exponent
\(v_\ell(u)-v_\ell(a)=v_\ell(r)-v_\ell(s)\) is preserved. The complete number
of deleting divisors is the integer group-ring coefficient
\[
[d^{-1}]\prod_{\ell^e\parallel R/d}
\bigl([1]+[\ell]+\cdots+[\ell^e]\bigr)
\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z[(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times].
\]
The labelled exponent vectors are not reduced modulo two.

## SZ-20260922-005 — nine automatic rays and the mixed prime-shell return

All unit squares modulo \(m=4rs\) equal one exactly when

\[
m\mid24\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad rs\mid6.
\]

The nine ordered primitive rays are

\[
(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(1,6),(2,1),(2,3),(3,1),(3,2),(6,1).
\]

For a prime \(p\equiv1\pmod {24}\), an exterior trace source on one of these rays has
\(R=\delta t^2\), with \(\delta\) the positive squarefree part, and set

\[
H=\frac{\delta+1}{4rs},\qquad J=\frac{pr+s}{\delta}.
\]

Then \(k=t^2\) gives the exterior return of SZ-20260922-004, and the same
source gives an original middle witness with

\[
(h_M,r_M,s_M,\lambda_M)=(H,s,J,r),\qquad
(a_M,Y_M,Z_M)=(HsJ,pHsr,pHJr).
\]

Before sorting, the canonical M-channel denominator order is
\((HsJ,pHJr,pHsr)\); since \(J>s\), the displayed triple above is the receiver
order \(a_M<Y_M<Z_M\).

Its residual and retained cofactor are

\[
R_M=\frac{s+J}{r},\qquad Q_M=\delta,\qquad 0<R_M<p.
\]

For fixed \(p,r,s,\delta\), the complete source fibre is

\[
R=\delta t^2,\qquad t\mid A_{\mathrm{odd}}/\delta,\qquad \delta t^2<p,
\]

where \(A_{\mathrm{odd}}=\operatorname{oddpart}(pr+s)\). Forgetting \(t\) has kernel basis
\(e_{\delta,t}-e_{\delta,1}\), \(t>1\); retaining \(t\) recovers the old
residual and shell.

Now let \(p\equiv1\pmod {24}\), \(a=cq\), \(c\mid6\), \(q>3\) prime. Every
positive rational ES tail pair at \(a\) with integral sum returns to an
original integral middle witness at the same \(p\). In the exterior branch,
quadratic-character parity forces exactly one factor of \(q\) into \(u\), so
\(rs\mid c\mid6\). In the middle branch, after a retained complement bit, the
small word divides \(c^2\mid36\). Both branches therefore enter a proved
automatic return. A proper input gives a strict decrease of the stated
residual or cofactor; the exterior branch also decreases the first
denominator.

- **Proof:** [nine rays, middle image and complete fibres](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-foundation/blob/e713c342fc16e361e104de3322790ca0cee7281d/research/incoming/es-turn07-mod24-ray-trace-20260922/core.tex#L185-L270); [mixed prime-shell theorem and both branches](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-foundation/blob/e713c342fc16e361e104de3322790ca0cee7281d/research/incoming/es-turn07-mod24-ray-trace-20260922/core.tex#L272-L327).
- **Nonclaim:** the theorem does not assert that its initial rational trace source is nonempty. The empty initial families at \(p=2521\) and \(3361\) are retained.

## SZ-20260922-006 — exact maximality of the universal ray rule

Fix coprime positive \(r,s\) with \(rs\nmid6\), and put \(m=4rs\). Choose a
unit \(b\bmod m\) with \(b^2\not\equiv1\pmod m\), changing its sign so that
\(b\equiv1\pmod4\). Choose distinct primes

\[
t\equiv b\pmod m,\qquad \delta\equiv-b^{-2}\pmod m,
\]

both exceeding \(\max(7,r,s,|r^2-s^2|)\), and put
\(R=\delta t^2\), \(L=\operatorname{lcm}(840,m)\). Choose a prime
\(e\equiv3\pmod4\) coprime to \(LR\). The reduced CRT class

\[
p\equiv1\pmod L,\qquad pr+s\equiv\delta t\pmod R,\qquad
p\equiv-1\pmod e
\]

contains infinitely many primes. For members large enough that
\(p>R\) and \(p>sR^2/(4r)\),

\[
h=\frac{p+R}{m},\qquad a=hrs,\qquad u=hr^2
\]

is an original proper exterior trace source with trace defect \(d=t\). The
only possible deleting factors are \(t,t^2,\delta t,\delta t^2\); their
residues exclude all four from the criterion in SZ-20260922-004. The same
sources can have no fixed-word residual-divisor return, and their exterior
complements can fail the relaxed trace gate. A separately chosen prime
\(e\mid p+1\) constructs the original middle witness

\[
(h_M,r_M,s_M,\lambda_M)=\left(\frac{e+1}{4},1,\frac{p+1}{e},1\right).
\]

Therefore the universal ray-preserving return works for every source on a
fixed ray exactly when \(rs\mid6\). This maximality theorem does not say that
an individual nonautomatic ray never returns.

The executable replay through \(p\le10{,}000\) checked 143 primes in the class
\(1\bmod24\), 171,732 shells and 5,616,298 original divisor words. The main
lane made 126,013 assertions; the separately written checker made 257,382.
The isolated prime \(6{,}975{,}049{,}201\) carries a complete \(p-1\) order
certificate with base 11, not a probabilistic prime label.

- **Proof:** [reduced progression, four failed divisors, complement failure and independent middle witness](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/erdos-straus-foundation/blob/e713c342fc16e361e104de3322790ca0cee7281d/research/incoming/es-turn07-mod24-ray-trace-20260922/core.tex#L405-L464).

## SZ-20260922-008 — complete bare sorted mixed-source fibres

mod-\(24\) domain, with \(a<p<Y<Z\) and \(2\le Y/p<Z/p\). Put

\[
\mathcal I_E(W)=
\left\{t\mid\operatorname{oddpart}(s):
\delta_Et^2<p,
\operatorname{Car}\!\left(\frac{p+\delta_Et^2}{4}\right)\right\}.
\]

\[
R_t=\delta_Et^2,\quad a_t=\frac{p+R_t}{4},\quad

## SZ-20260922-010 — hard-middle sharpness on two reduced progressions

\]

\[
p\equiv4201\pmod{9240},\qquad s=\frac{p+1}{11},

\[
(a,Y,Z)=(3s,3p,3ps).
\]

\[
p^{-1}\|O^{-1}\|_2\to\sqrt{\frac{286200}{360371}},\qquad
