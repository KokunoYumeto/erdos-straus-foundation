## Source-led integration results

The [seven detailed arguments](results/integration-2026-09-10/ARGUMENTS.md) include:

1. A coefficient-derived full M5(F107) algebra, an exact 25-word certificate, and the necessary base-index distinction for its frame transports.
2. Explicit real correspondence and rational non-equivalence for a quadratic-form pencil, with pointed invariants and hyperbolic graph-lattice calculations.
3. A support-sensitive semiring over any nonzero commutative ring, with its universal ring reflection and unique Boolean character.
4. The obstruction to support-independent affine translation, its homogeneous repair, and a finite binary-word identity.
5. Exact abstract C6 Fourier target identities, separated from a demonstrably false modular-840 identification in one source passage.
6. The finite Weyl operator basis, multiplication and commutator laws.
7. A sharp neutral-state gap and four-sector projection algebra for a specified diagonal model, with its quadratic/determinant coordinate identities.

[Source locations and comparison limits](results/integration-2026-09-10/SOURCES.md) make the readings auditable. The [new finite checker](results/integration-2026-09-10/verify_results.py) rebuilds the coefficient data and checks selected finite consequences. [CHECKS.json](results/integration-2026-09-10/CHECKS.json) records its actual run. General arguments do not become proved solely because examples passed. No Lean run or second independent review is claimed.
