# Public editorial changes

This expanded edition contains 72 theorem, proposition, lemma and corollary
statements in 15 canonical mathematical TeX files. Their exact original bytes
and hashes are retained in PROVENANCE.json. Fourteen selected computational
commands were replayed in the standalone source tree; REPLAY_RECEIPT.json
records their commands, finite scopes, software versions and outcomes. These
finite checks supplement the written general proofs, not replace them.

The publication.tex reading edition adds collective authorship, human
contribution credits and citations at the source interfaces. Its three
publication-prefixed leaf variants make the same kind of attribution additions.
PUBLICATION_EDITORIAL_MAP.json records the exact reversible changes; none
changes a mathematical proof. The 9 September publication repair rebuilds
this reading edition until citations and cross-references resolve, corrects
inherited edition/count prose, and supplies a PDF-specific visual review.

Public copies of review and provenance records retain mathematical arguments,
coordinate maps, source hashes, bibliographic authorship and proof findings.
Private account/interpreter paths were replaced with portable commands or
repository-relative source identifiers. Private library routing fields,
assigned task directories, attachment paths, task ownership text and internal
instructions were removed. PROVENANCE.json records the source and public-copy
hashes for these editorial transformations.

The source-claim record supplies the current proof graph and statement locators.
The historical exact_audit/THEOREM_MORPHISMS.json was omitted because it names
a different TeX presentation. exact_audit/audit.py was omitted because it reads
external files to append provenance hashes. Its historical certificate is
retained as historical evidence, not represented as a fresh replay. Similarly,
qa/QA.json authenticates the earlier canonical PDF; the reading edition has
the distinct current qa/PUBLICATION_PDF_QA.json receipt.

The expanded edition includes the reverse-transfer, smooth-inverse and
boundary-swap proofs listed in the 15-file graph. Later incoming research not
listed in that graph, including the separate NS foundations, is not silently
added. No RH material, private logs or transcripts, credentials, copyrighted
source books or external source manuscripts are included in this supplement.

README.md supplies reading and exact compilation instructions, CITATION.cff
identifies the collective work and continuing concept DOI, requirements.txt
records the checker dependency, and LICENSE.md states the public terms.
The numbered PDF is the readable preview; the ZIP is the portable source and
check archive. Prior published editions and their file inventories are preserved.
