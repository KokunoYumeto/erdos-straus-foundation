\begin{abstract}
For the specified integral period monodromy we compute a three-coordinate
affine slice at every odd torsion level. Its extension cocycle has exact
order equal to that level. We prove a complete orbit classification, with
one orbit away from three and two orbits at levels divisible by three,
and give closed genus formulas. The return denominator of each fully
marked Erd\H{o}s--Straus divisor candidate maps faithfully to this cocycle
subgroup. The comparison uses the actual source matrices, not the source's
global six-sphere claim. No universal ES positivity theorem or historical
priority is asserted.
\end{abstract}\maketitle

\section{Application with the arithmetic labels retained}
For a prime $p=4m+1$, let $m+1\le a\le3m$, $R=4a-p$, and
$u=\prod q^{f_q}$ where $a=\prod q^{e_q}$ and $0\le f_q\le2e_q$.
The inherited two-channel rational triples are
\[
 Q_E=\left(a,\frac{pa+a^2/u}{R},\frac{pa+p^2u}{R}\right),\qquad
 Q_M=\left(a,\frac{p(a+a^2/u)}R,\frac{p(a+u)}R\right).
\]
Both satisfy the reciprocal equation. The two latter coordinates have
common minimal denominator
\[
 D_E=R/\gcd(R,4u+1),\qquad D_M=R/\gcd(R,u+a).
\]
For $E$ this follows by multiplying the first numerator by the unit $u$
modulo $R$, obtaining $a^2(4u+1)$; the other numerator is congruent to
$4a^2(4u+1)$. For $M$ the same calculation has factor $u+a$.
The denominator is odd, so every candidate gives the affine cover above.
If $g$ is its gate and $h=\gcd(R,g)$, the explicit group isomorphism is
\[
 \langle[g]_R\rangle\longrightarrow\langle[b_D]\rangle,
 \qquad [kg]_R\longmapsto k[b_D].
\]
Its inverse reads $k$ as minus the third cusp evaluation and multiplies
by $g$ modulo $R$. Conversely the inverse from a residue $v$ to $k$ is
$(v/h)(g/h)^{-1}\bmod D$.

Every integral ES solution is caught by this full interval: choose a
smallest denominator $a$, use $p/4<a\le3p/4$, and factor
$(RY-pa)(RZ-pa)=p^2a^2$. Its three possible $p$-valuations give respectively
$E,u=a^2/(RY-pa)$; $M,u=pa^2/(RY-pa)$; or the reversed exterior with
$u=(RY-pa)/p^2$. Thus ES at $p$ is equivalent to $D=1$ for some candidate.
Retain the exterior order bit, middle complementation $u\mapsto a^2/u$,
all exponents, and $p$ in the decoration. By Theorem~\ref{thm:class},
the disjoint union of these decorated covers has a section exactly when
ES holds. There is no claim that forgetting the decoration remains
injective, nor that the resulting compact curves are six-spheres.

For the restricted empty shell $p=241,a=64,R=15$, each channel has return
levels $3,5,15$ with multiplicities $4,6,3$. Keeping both exterior orders
and the original middle order gives 39 states, hence 32,949 sheets in
60 compact components, with total genus 5,448. Other shells at that prime
succeed. The standard-library verifier includes all these records and the
full atlases for $p=5,37,241,1201$; the all-level theorems above are written
proofs, not extrapolations from those finite replays.
