\begin{abstract}
The full finite Erd\H{o}s--Straus divisor atlas has a canonical integer
return denominator at every labelled candidate. We identify its cyclic
obstruction group with a primitive cohomology class of exact order $D$
in the actual rank-four period monodromy circulated with the proposed
$S^6$ construction. The affine slice has three residue coordinates.
We classify all its orbits and all compactified genera for every odd
level, retaining prime-power budgets, raw orders and candidate labels.
A source-derived central commutator is translation by $-12$.
A counterexample prime would give a full sectionless decorated covering;
no such prime is found. We distinguish this construction from the source's
six-dimensional total space, exhibit an obstruction to one proposed
identification, and keep all source-global claims outside the new proofs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Reproducibility and precise scope}
The standalone verifier recomputes primality, factorizations, every full
exponent box, every ordered rational return, all local denominator losses,
and the actual finite affine permutations at the stated levels.
It completely enumerates levels $1,3,5,7,9,15,21,25,27,35,45$ and full
arithmetic atlases at primes $5,37,241,1201$.
Large covers attached to the arithmetic atlases are specified by exact
formulas and proved passports; their $D^3$ sheets are not all expanded.
The restricted negative example $p=241,a=64$ is checked separately and
cannot pass the full-negative validator.

The code uses only the Python standard library and raises explicit errors,
including in optimized execution. The certificate stores every source
matrix and affine branch; the $D\le9$ permutations are supplied in full.
The source topology and analytic period-existence theorem were not
independently rerun, nor was a Lean build performed. A precise dependency
plan accompanies this tranche. No global ES resolution or independently
certified global complex structure on $S^6$ is claimed.

The orbit classification, its exact genus formulas, and the marked
cyclic-obstruction comparison were derived in this investigation. A
content-level search located the source monodromies, the standard
permutation-cover correspondence and classical finite group framework,
but no exact antecedent for these combined formulas was established.
This is not a historical-priority claim. The compact companion preprint
isolates those calculations rather than republishing a long historical
corpus. No repository was modified and no external deposit was made.
