# Typed maps and complete fibres

## A01

**Domain:** full candidate labels.

**Codomain:** positive rational ES triples with distinguished a, channel and order.

**Map:** Q_E/Q_M formulas.

**Inverse/fibre:** undo the swap; u=a^2/(RY-pa) for E, pa^2/(RY-pa) for M; retain p,R.

**Scope:** exponent vector and prime factorization retained; no chamber or R<p restriction.

## A02

**Domain:** a prime-power exponent box.

**Codomain:** divisors of a^2.

**Map:** u=product q^f.

**Inverse/fibre:** f_q=v_q(u).

**Scope:** bijection; all 0<=f_q<=2e_q.

## A03

**Domain:** candidate alpha.

**Codomain:** (D_alpha,alpha).

**Map:** D=R/gcd(R,gate).

**Inverse/fibre:** projection to retained alpha.

**Scope:** forgetting alpha leaves many nontrivial fibres; not a state isomorphism without the label.

## A04

**Domain:** Z/D.

**Codomain:** the cyclic subgroup generated by gate modulo R.

**Map:** k -> k*gate mod R.

**Inverse/fibre:** (v/gcd)*(gate/gcd)^-1 mod D.

**Scope:** every prime-power loss and the gate unit are retained.

## A05

**Domain:** cyclic obstruction O_alpha.

**Codomain:** cyclic cohomology subgroup generated by b_D.

**Map:** k*gate -> k[b_D].

**Inverse/fibre:** k=-third cusp evaluation; then multiply by gate mod R.

**Scope:** isomorphism; exact additive order D.

## A06

**Domain:** L/DL, rank-four period lattice.

**Codomain:** D-torsion of a specified regular source torus.

**Map:** v -> [Pi v/D].

**Inverse/fibre:** flat lattice coordinates modulo D.

**Scope:** Pi is a valid regular period; no claimed immersion of uv=a^2 into its strict domain.

## A07

**Domain:** gamma=1 in L/DL.

**Codomain:** three-coordinate affine fibre F_D.

**Map:** (1,x,y,z) -> (x,y,z).

**Inverse/fibre:** adjoin leading 1 mod D.

**Scope:** bijection with marked gamma; every point has exact D torsion order.

## A08

**Domain:** F_D.

**Codomain:** F_D.

**Map:** a1, a2, a_infinity as in core.tex.

**Inverse/fibre:** a1^2, a2^3, (x,y-x,z+1).

**Scope:** permutations are invertible also when D=1; different from diagonal H_p.

## A09

**Domain:** F_DE with gcd(D,E)=1.

**Codomain:** F_D x F_E.

**Map:** coordinatewise reduction.

**Inverse/fibre:** explicit coprime CRT.

**Scope:** same base and candidate; no arbitrary cross-leaf identification.

## A10

**Domain:** F_E with D|E.

**Codomain:** F_D.

**Map:** coordinatewise reduction.

**Inverse/fibre:** all (E/D)^3 lifts.

**Scope:** surjective, not injective; inverse is a full fibre.

## A11

**Domain:** labelled permutation orbit.

**Codomain:** compact connected branched cover of P1.

**Map:** slit-sphere gluing; cycle of length e filled by w -> w^e.

**Inverse/fibre:** recover marked fibre and loop continuation.

**Scope:** equivalence up to marked cover isomorphism; no general rational equation claimed.

## A12

**Domain:** full candidate atlas.

**Codomain:** disjoint union of decorated torsion covers.

**Map:** alpha -> {alpha} x F_Dalpha.

**Inverse/fibre:** retain alpha; recover D from the cusp length.

**Scope:** all geometric structure depends on D; original arithmetic labels remain essential.

## A13

**Domain:** a common fixed sheet.

**Codomain:** a global cover section.

**Map:** pathwise continuation.

**Inverse/fibre:** evaluate at the marked base point.

**Scope:** bijection; occurs exactly at D=1.

## A14

**Domain:** same-shell labelled arithmetic edge.

**Codomain:** equivariant isomorphism of cover fibres.

**Map:** (alpha,v) -> (alpha_prime,v).

**Inverse/fibre:** reverse edge.

**Scope:** D invariant; full path retained, since forgetting it is not faithful on paths.

## S01

**Domain:** source dual lattices V,L.

**Codomain:** same lattices.

**Map:** (v,l) -> (T_j v,A_j l).

**Inverse/fibre:** T_j^-1,A_j^-1.

**Scope:** pairing preserved exactly by inverse transpose.

## S02

**Domain:** dP6 normalization branch.

**Codomain:** cusp quotient fibre W.

**Map:** identify opposite boundary components.

**Inverse/fibre:** one/two/three branches on smooth/double/triple strata.

**Scope:** not an everywhere bijective mirror; branch marks retained.

## S03

**Domain:** nearby period real vector spaces.

**Codomain:** nearby period real vector spaces.

**Map:** linear plus conjugate term from source key-advances Section 2.

**Inverse/fibre:** real-linear inverse where displayed determinant nonzero.

**Scope:** not generally complex-linear; not equated with the cocycle inverse.

## S04

**Domain:** admissible integer filling parameters.

**Codomain:** cyclic presentation quotient.

**Map:** relation matrix in comparison.tex.

**Inverse/fibre:** for D prime to6 use explicit ell tuple and generator maps.

**Scope:** conditional geometric interpretation follows the source filling theorem; direct integer algebra is proved here.

## S05

**Domain:** a genus-positive cover component.

**Codomain:** normalization/base change of source fibration and chosen resolution.

**Map:** fibre product over P1.

**Inverse/fibre:** not a claimed isomorphism to X.

**Scope:** pi1 surjects to base; positive b1 prevents this specific object being S6.

## B01

**Domain:** base marks (0,1,infinity).

**Codomain:** Fable base marks (1/2,-1/2,infinity).

**Map:** s=1/2-t.

**Inverse/fibre:** t=1/2-s.

**Scope:** only a base-coordinate identification; no automatic equality of the monodromy representations.
