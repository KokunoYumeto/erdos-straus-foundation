# Lean-ready dependency plan

No Lean run was performed. Target: Lean 4 with a pinned mathlib revision selected
by the formalizer. Do not advertise this file as formal certification.

## A. Integral matrix and affine arithmetic (unconditional, decidable)
1. Define the five source matrices T1,T2,A1,A2,Ainf over Matrix (Fin 4) (Fin 4) Z.
2. Prove transpose(Tj)*Aj=I, Aj finite orders, A1*A2*Ainf=I, determinants 1.
3. Prove first row invariance and define gamma, the kernel M=Z^3, and its action.
4. Prove the block cocycle identity b(gh)=b(g)+B(g)b(h).
5. Give the group presentation Delta=C3*C4, factoring the integer representation.
6. Define each affine action on (ZMod D)^3, including D=1; verify all inverses.
7. Prove ainf^n(x,y,z)=(x,y+n*x,z-n) and orbit length exactly D.
8. Compute fixed sets of a1,a2,a2^2 for odd D, including the z equation.
9. Compute H=[A2^2,A1], V=A2*H*A2^-1 and [H,V] as central shift -12.

## B. Cohomology and complete orbit theorems (unconditional)
10. Define crossed homomorphisms and coboundaries directly if group-cohomology
    API would otherwise hide conventions; quotient the additive groups.
11. Prove third-cusp evaluation kills all coboundaries and sends b to -1.
12. Obtain the split Z summand integrally and exact additive order D modulo D.
13. Show the restriction to C3 and C4 is a coboundary using (2,-4,0),(3,-3,0).
14. Prove transitivity for gcd(D,6)=1 using invertibility of 6 and 12.
15. Exhaust the eight nonzero pairs over ZMod 3 with the listed generator words.
16. For 3|D, prove H,V act transitively on every prescribed planar residue lift.
17. When x=0 mod3, solve 6n=-x mod D, and prove H^n*ainf fixes x,y and shifts
    z by c=-1+n(x-6) with gcd(c,12,D)=1.
18. Conjugate the last step for the nonzero planar residue orbit.
19. Conclude precisely two orbits, with cardinalities D^3/9 and 8D^3/9.
20. Prove all fixed-point and cycle counts, and the integer genus formulas.
21. Prove explicit CRT equivalence and all divisor-reduction fibre counts.
22. Level-three finite matrix group: complete enumeration certificate of 216
    matrices, SL2(3) projection, nine-element row kernel, character splitting.
    The input lists are untrusted until verified; no cardinality-only isomorphism.

## C. Arithmetic atlas and its inverse (unconditional)
23. Prove full smallest-denominator interval for prime p=4m+1.
24. Prove gcd(a,R)=gcd(p,R)=1; enumerate the full divisor exponent box.
25. Define the three ordered tags E0,E1,M0; no extra middle swap bit.
26. Prove both rational reciprocal identities and primitive h,r,s formulas.
27. Prove common minimal denominator D by multiplying numerators by units.
28. Prove D odd, D|R, gcd(D,p)=1 and the positive integral cleared identity.
29. Prove the inverse using the three p-adic residual branches 0,1,2.
30. Bound ordered atlas size by 3(p-1)^2/2.
31. Define the cyclic subgroup generated by the gate modulo R, prove order D,
    and verify the explicit inverse (v/h)*(gate/h)^-1 modulo D.
32. Compose with the primitive cohomology class, including its inverse evaluation.
33. Show ES iff at least one D=1 iff the labelled local system has a section.
34. Verify every row, exponent and scope of a proposed negative certificate;
    reject restricted, omitted, modified or positive data.
35. Prove exact D-preservation on complement, exterior reversal and the
    same-shell E/M correspondence; retain source, target and path labels.

## D. Complex geometry: imported foundations with explicit statements
36. Finite permutation representation of pi1(P1 minus {0,1,infinity}) defines
    the corresponding finite topological/holomorphic covering.
37. Compactify length-e cycles by w -> w^e. Riemann existence is a classical
    imported theorem; no claim it is already in mathlib.
38. Euler/Riemann-Hurwitz calculation from retained cycles gives the genera.
39. For a valid regular period map Pi, prove L/DL -> (C^2/Pi L)[D] is a
    bijection equivariant for the dual monodromy. Gamma=1 is its exact-order-D
    affine subset. Analytic existence of the source Pi is an imported theorem.
40. For the specified source fibration, a compact base change/resolution with
    positive-genus base has pi1 surjection onto that base, by path lifting and
    connected regular fibres. No smoothness of a raw normalization is assumed.

## E. Source comparison, not promoted dependencies

42. Prove the explicit admissible twist assignment for gcd(D,6)=1 and why
    3|D cannot be represented by the same free (3,4)-filling family.
43. The source theorem identifying the original completed threefold with S6
    is not a dependency of A-C or the orbit/genus theorem. A future formalization
    must not silently introduce it as a proven axiom of this package.
44. Verify native period coefficient substitution gives determinant zero when
    uv=a^2; do not use it as a strict regular-period inequality.
45. Catalogue separately source-normalization branches, real-linear conjugate
    period comparisons, and dual inverse-transpose maps. None is inferred to
    be the inverse of another solely from the word 'mirror'.
