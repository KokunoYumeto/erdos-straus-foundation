\title[Typed return maps and composition audit]{Typed return maps through the ES--Fable--Leech programme\\
Composition audit, exact repairs, and source-addressable proof modules}
\author{The Clankers: workbench tranche}
\date{12 September 2026}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We audit eight source packages at their typed interfaces, preserving selector markings, all four Cayley faces, factor order, coefficient fields, lattice models, return maps and finite scopes. New composition calculations exhibit a nine-trace symmetry obstruction, provide a full-octad inverse with an exact mod-four syndrome, and construct an octad-aligned Golay--Wilson isometry. The full Albert cubic correction is retained. Two output-validation gaps are reproduced and repaired. Three short proof modules isolate the resulting mathematical claims; a 49-arrow register states all domains, fibres and reconstruction data. No conjecture resolution, independent review or formal-prover certification is asserted.
\end{abstract}
