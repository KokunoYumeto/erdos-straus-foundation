\section{Scope, evidence, and the rule for an inverse claim}
This audit uses eight locally available mathematical packages, beginning with the marked exterior/middle selector and ending with the three-octad Leech inverse. The exact 105 source files and their SHA-256 identities are in \texttt{source\_inventory.json}. It does not reconstruct an unavailable conversation or certify an uninspected Git branch. The original files are preserved unchanged under \texttt{inputs/}.

Eighteen original execution processes returned zero exit status. The first two reproduced the original bounded arithmetic census and the preserved covering-certificate replay through $10^{10}$. The other eight checker families were each run normally and under Python optimization, with byte-identical paired output files. The first two assertion-using programs intentionally reject optimized execution. Re-running a program is not an independent reviewer or a proof of every written assertion. The new checker is separately written, uses only Python's standard library, and exercises the interfaces that the original per-package tests did not join.

A many-to-one morphism is legitimate. What requires proof is the inference through it: either retain its complete fibre, or prove that the property being transported is constant on that fibre. Recording a discarded coordinate does not by itself prove an inverse; the return equations below are checked on their specified domains.

\section{The complete marked arithmetic--factor junction}
Write a selector state as
\[
m=(p,R,u_E,h,r,s,\mathsf c,q;x,y,z),\qquad \mathsf c\in\{E,M\}.
\]
The subscript on $u_E$ distinguishes the ES divisor from the Fable source coordinate $u_F$; it does not restrict the channel to $E$. All denominators are ordered. On the inherited bounded domain, $p$ is odd prime, $0<R<p$, $p+R=4hrs$, $u_E=hr^2$ and $\gcd(r,s)=1$. The channel formulas are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\mathsf c&q&(x,y,z)\\\hline
E&(pr+s)/R&(hrs,hsq,phrq)\\
M&(r+s)/R&(hrs,phsq,phrq).
\end{array}
\]
The quotients must be positive integers. The inverse divisor formulas are
\[
u_E=\frac{x^2}{Ry-px}\quad(E),\qquad
u_E=\frac{px^2}{Ry-px}\quad(M).
\]
With $d=\gcd(x,u_E)$, the inverse is $r=u_E/d$, $s=x/d$, $h=d/r$. These are canonical factor-exponent equalities, not choices from an unbounded generated subgroup. The original Yamamoto/Elsholtz--Tao lineage and exact references are retained in the source package; the mapped identities here can also be checked by clearing their displayed denominators.

\subsection{Four faces, three distinguished factors, and two orientations}
Set $a=(-p,4x,4y,4z)$ and retain it affinely. It satisfies $e_3(a)=0$, equivalently $\sum_i a_i^{-1}=0$. For a flag $(i,j,k,l)\in S_4$, put
\[
 h_i=-\frac1{4a_i},\qquad
(r_0,r_1,r_2)=\left(-\frac{4a_i}{a_j},-\frac{4a_i}{a_k},-\frac{4a_i}{a_l}\right).
\]
Their sum is four. The complete open domain here requires nonzero, pairwise distinct $a_i$; hence the roots are nonzero, pairwise distinct and not $-4$. The inverse is
\[
a_i=-\frac1{4h_i},\qquad
(a_j,a_k,a_l)=\frac1{h_i}(r_0^{-1},r_1^{-1},r_2^{-1}).
\]
The affine scale $h_i$ is essential for recovering $p$ and the denominator marking.

The normalized cubic is
\[
f(Z)=-\tfrac14(Z-r_0)(Z-r_1)(Z-r_2)
=-\tfrac14Z^3+Z^2+\tfrac\beta2Z+\alpha.
\]
Retain $r=r_0$, $d=f'(r)\ne0$ and $\eta=r_1-r_2$. Then
\[
\eta^2=(3r-4)^2+16d,\qquad
(r_1,r_2)=\bigl((4-r+\eta)/2,(4-r-\eta)/2\bigr).
\]
The derivative and its square-root discriminant are insufficient without the sign of $\eta$ when companion order is required. The oriented face change is
\[
(r_0,r_1,r_2)\mapsto(16/r_0,-4r_2/r_0,-4r_1/r_0),
\]
\[
(r,d,\eta)\mapsto
\left(16/r,\;8+16(d-8)/r^2,\;4\eta/r\right).
\]
It exchanges the two leading flag indices and the two companion indices, preserving parity. These formulas are rational involutions on the stated domain.

The exact Fable source point for a finite selected root is
\[
(u_F,v_F,w_F)=\left(d^{-1},-r-d,5d^2+3rd+\tfrac12d^3\right).
\]
The inverse selected-root data are $d=1/u_F$ and $r=-v_F-d$. In the general target cubic, $\tfrac12d^3$ is replaced by $-\gamma d^3$. The infinity branch is separately present when $\gamma=0$; it must not be obtained by substituting infinity into the finite formula. The underlying normalized factor construction is Tao's equations (2), (5), (7), and (10).

On the distinct-root locus, forgetting the companion order is $2:1$, forgetting the selected root is $3:1$, and forgetting all root order is $6:1$. Four face choices yield 12 orientation-preserving flags or 24 flags with both orientations. The 12 fixed-cubic tetrahedral pencil states in the earlier note are a different fourfold fibre over the three selected factors. They are not twelve geometric inverse points of one unmarked polynomial map.

\[
\mathscr R=\{(m,\lambda):m\text{ is an actual selector state},\ P\lambda=d(m)\}.
\]
If $K=\ker P$ and $\sigma$ is the integral section, then
\[
(m,k)\mapsto(m,\sigma(d(m))+k),\quad
