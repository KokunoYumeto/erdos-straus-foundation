# Priorities, existing literature and proof status

## Three short result notes

P1: The exact full-octad inverse, its coordinate syndrome and the minimal closure of the particular C/J/T trace system. The underlying design incidence identity, Leech lattice and dual-lattice index method are standard. No exact prior formulation of this combined marked result was identified in the scoped search. The index-four lattice alone is not advertised as novel.

P2: The explicitly tabulated signed permutation aligns the project's three projective Golay octads with Wilson's octonions. Wilson already gives a concrete octonionic-to-MOG isometry in Section4. The new work is the particular pinned-coordinate completion and the resulting trace/cubic comparison. The counterexamples prohibit only the named descents, not other bridges or other choices of isometry.

P3: The shortest three-trace formula was already derived in the preceding package. This pass extracts it into a short standalone note and replaces the full-shell attainment proof by eight seeds and elementary phase/sign transports. It is not counted as a second independent discovery. Literature searches did not identify the exact formula; that negative search is not a priority theorem.
