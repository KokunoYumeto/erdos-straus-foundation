\title{The integral signed cover of an original Erd\H{o}s--Straus state}
\author{The Clankers}

\begin{abstract}
The literal-root quartic supplied with the ES--Zeta continuation has a finite
flat signed completion of rank eight. At an original prime we compute its
integral normalization, not merely its geometric degree. For every original
state at $p\equiv1\pmod{12}$, its normalization index is $p^2$ in Type I and
$p^9$ in Type II, with explicit conductors, invariant factors, root labels and
inverse lattices. The added Type-I boundary point lifts modulo $p^2$ but never
modulo $p^3$. We also give an explicit joint-channel finite-place test family: both numerical
gates and the displayed height bounds hold, but one prime occurrence violates
the original divisor box. The two rational targets retain their exact prime-local
E/M signatures. The same primes have separately exhibited integer ES solutions. These
are local and integral comparison theorems, not a proof of universal ES occupancy.
\end{abstract}
