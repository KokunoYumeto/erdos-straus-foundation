# The exact local signed-label representation

## Normalized factors and their characters

Let \(p>2\), let \(K=\mathbb Q_p\), and retain the integral signed order

\[
\mathscr E_p=\mathbb Z_p[T,\sigma]/(f(T),\sigma^2-Af'(T))
\]

from the defining-prime calculation. For the distinct labelled roots
\(t_0,\ldots,t_3\), put

\[
b_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\nu_p(t_i-t_j),\qquad
m_i=\left\lfloor\frac{b_i}{2}\right\rfloor,\qquad
\epsilon_i=b_i-2m_i\in\{0,1\},
\]

\[
u_i=p^{-b_i}Af'(t_i)\in\mathbb Z_p^\times,\qquad
\tau_i=\sigma_i/p^{m_i},\qquad
\tau_i^2=p^{\epsilon_i}u_i.
\]

Fix a coefficient field of characteristic zero. Let \(\chi_i\) be the
quadratic character of \(G_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\overline K/K)\) attached to
the quadratic \(K\)-algebra
\(K[\tau_i]/(\tau_i^2-p^{\epsilon_i}u_i)\); it is trivial when that algebra
splits. The permutation representation on the two geometric signed labels of
this factor is

\[
W_i\cong\mathbf1\oplus\chi_i.
\]

This is an equality of \(G_K\)-representations. It retains a split factor as
two fixed labels and a field factor as the two embeddings exchanged by its
quadratic character.

## The cluster quotient

Let \(\mathcal C\) be a residue cluster of \(m\ge2\) root labels. Its
nonreduced special fibre has one reduced geometric point. The common reduced
point supplies the diagonal invariant line

\[
\mathbf1_\Delta\longrightarrow
\bigoplus_{i\in\mathcal C}W_i,
\qquad
1\longmapsto((1,1))_{i\in\mathcal C}.
\]

Define the signed-label defect representation by the exact quotient

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbf1_\Delta
\longrightarrow\bigoplus_{i\in\mathcal C}
(\mathbf1\oplus\chi_i)
\longrightarrow V_{\mathcal C}
\longrightarrow0.
\]

Representations of the finite elementary \(2\)-group generated by the
\(\chi_i\) are semisimple in characteristic zero. Removing the displayed
diagonal trivial line therefore gives the exact decomposition

\[
\boxed{
V_{\mathcal C}\cong
\mathbf1^{\,m-1}\oplus
\bigoplus_{i\in\mathcal C}\chi_i.
}
\tag{LG1}
\]

In particular, \(\dim V_{\mathcal C}=2m-1\). This construction records the
loss from \(2m\) generic signed labels to one reduced special-fibre label. It
does not identify a nonreduced scheme with its normalization.

Every quadratic extension of \(\mathbb Q_p\) for odd \(p\) is tame.
Moreover,

- if \(\epsilon_i=0\), the factor is split or unramified and
  \(a(\chi_i)=0\);
- if \(\epsilon_i=1\), the factor is ramified quadratic and
  \(a(\chi_i)=1\).

Thus the Swan conductor is zero and

\[
\boxed{
a(V_{\mathcal C})=\sum_{i\in\mathcal C}\epsilon_i.
}
\tag{LG2}
\]

The same conductor is obtained before quotienting by
\(\mathbf1_\Delta\), because a trivial representation has conductor zero.

## Equality with the integral defect formula

For all four labels, let \(n_{ij}=\nu_p(t_i-t_j)\). The normalization
calculation proves

\[
L:=
\operatorname{length}_{\mathbb Z_p}
(\widetilde{\mathscr E}_p/\mathscr E_p)
=
3\sum_{i<j}n_{ij}-\frac12\sum_i\epsilon_i.
\]

Let \(W=\bigoplus_i(\mathbf1\oplus\chi_i)\). Since
\(a(W)=\sum_i\epsilon_i\), multiplication by two and rearrangement give the
exact identity

\[
\boxed{
2L+a(W)=6\sum_{i<j}\nu_p(t_i-t_j).
}
\tag{LG3}
\]

This relates two different invariants of the same normalized factors: the
left term \(L\) measures the integral lattice defect, while \(a(W)\) measures
ramification of the generic signed-label representation. Neither determines
the other.

## Exterior pair

For a separated one-divisible-denominator state, write \(z=pZ\). The
collision cluster is \(\{p,z\}\), with
\(\nu_p(p-z)=1\). The normalized derivative units at the two labels have
ratio

\[
\frac{p^{-1}Af'(p)}{p^{-1}Af'(z)}
=
-\frac{(p-x)(p-y)}{(z-x)(z-y)}
\equiv-1\pmod p.
\]

Because \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), \(-1\) is a square in
\(\mathbb F_p^\times\), so the two ramified quadratic characters are equal.
The exterior-cofactor calculation identifies their common field as

\[
\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt{pD_E}),\qquad
D_E=\frac{4u+1}{R}=\frac{r+\kappa}{s}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
V_{\{p,z\}}
\cong\mathbf1\oplus\chi_{pD_E}^{\oplus2},
\qquad
a(V_{\{p,z\}})=2,
\qquad
\dim V_{\{p,z\}}^{I_p}=1.
}
\tag{LG4}
\]

The invariant line is the remaining trivial summand; each ramified character
has no inertia invariants.

## Middle triple

For a separated two-divisible-denominator state, write

\[
y=pY,\qquad z=pZ,\qquad Y+Z\equiv4YZ\pmod p,
\]

with \(1,Y,Z\) pairwise distinct. Each difference in the cluster
\(\{p,y,z\}\) has valuation one, so every clustered derivative valuation is
two. All three \(\chi_i\) are therefore unramified or trivial.

For \(\{\alpha,\beta,\gamma\}=\{1,Y,Z\}\), the normalized derivative unit is

\[
u_\alpha\equiv(\alpha-\beta)(\alpha-\gamma)\pmod p.
\tag{LG5}
\]

Indeed the omitted common factor is
\(-Ax=x/S\equiv1\pmod p\), so LG5 retains the complete square class. The
product of the three units is

\[
-\bigl[(1-Y)(1-Z)(Y-Z)\bigr]^2.
\]

At \(p\equiv1\pmod4\), this is a square. Consequently either all three
unramified characters are trivial, or exactly two are the nontrivial
unramified quadratic character. Since LG1 contributes two further trivial
summands, the Frobenius characteristic polynomial on the rank-five defect
representation is exactly one of

\[
\boxed{(1-T)^5,\qquad (1-T)^3(1+T)^2.}
\tag{LG6}
\]

## The \(p=1201\) middle witness

For

\[
(p;x,y,z)=(1201;306,21618,61251)
\]

one has \(Y=18\) and \(Z=51\). Formula LG5 gives the three retained units

\[
850,\qquad640,\qquad449\pmod{1201}.
\]

Euler's criterion gives their characters

\[
-1,\qquad+1,\qquad-1.
\]

The isolated root \(x=306\) has derivative unit \(42\), which is a square
modulo \(1201\). Thus the normalized signed fibre has four
\(\mathbb F_{1201}\)-points: two from the isolated split factor and two from
the one split clustered factor. The original special fibre has those two
isolated points plus its one reduced cluster point, hence three. The
rank-five defect representation has

\[
\boxed{\det(1-T\,\mathrm{Frob}\mid V)=(1-T)^3(1+T)^2}
\]

and Frobenius trace \(3-2=1\), exactly the point-count difference \(4-3\).

The integral defect remains independent data:

\[
\operatorname{length}
(\widetilde{\mathscr E}_{1201}/\mathscr E_{1201})=9,\qquad
\operatorname{Smith}(\mathsf C_{1201})
=(0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3).
\]

This example proves that an unramified normalized representation can coexist
with a large integral gluing defect. It does not identify local Frobenius
with any complex zeta operator.
