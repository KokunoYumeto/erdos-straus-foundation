# Defining-prime normalization of the signed Erdős--Straus quartic

This module studies the literal rank-eight signed quartic at the same prime
that appears as its distinguished root. It retains the original integral
order, its normalization, every Smith factor, the nonreduced special fibre,
the fixed-prime \(2+6\) gluing and the specified complex Gamma metric.

## Principal results

- The coefficient hypersurface has normalization
  \(\mathbb C[A,A^{-1},P,W]\), with the complete coefficient map, rank-three
  inclusion, module cokernel and conductor ideals.
- Every positive integral witness at \(p\equiv1\pmod {12}\) has distinct
  literal roots. Exactly one or two denominators are divisible by \(p\), and
  every such denominator has valuation one.
- On the integral coefficient chart \(p\nmid S\), the complete signed
  normalization matrix is
  \[
  \mathsf C_p=\operatorname{diag}
  \bigl(V,\operatorname{diag}(p^{m_i})V\bigr).
  \]
  The one-divisible-denominator and two-divisible-denominator Smith formulas
  retain every unit factor and every closer-pair valuation.
- The separated E and M states are exactly the \(n=0\) and \(n=1\) instances:
  \[
  E:(0,0,0,0,0,0,1,1),\qquad
  M:(0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3),
  \]
  with indices \(p^2\) and \(p^9\).
- The translated chart used when \(p\mid S\) is an exact quadratic twist.
  If \(c_\lambda=S/(S-4\lambda)\) and \(q^2=c_\lambda\), then
  \(T_\lambda\mapsto T_0-\lambda\) and
  \(\sigma_\lambda\mapsto q\sigma_0\) give an isomorphism over
  \(\mathbb Q_p(q)\). Descent over \(\mathbb Q_p\) requires the retained
  square-class condition.
- The two \(p=1201\) witnesses give the displayed length-four and length-six
  boundary algebras, all nilpotent block sizes, and the exterior local field
  \(\mathbb Q_p(\sqrt{pD_E})\).
- The fixed-prime cover is the exact fibre product of rank-two and rank-six
  sectors over \(B/(R)[\sigma]/(\sigma^2)\). At a collision the local algebra
  is \(B[\zeta]/(\zeta^4-t^2)\), with Smith exponents
  \((0,0,1,1)\) and a two-dimensional specialization kernel.
- The original fixed Gamma metric gives the exact reproducing-kernel minimum,
  CRT excess and four raw-evaluation covariance scales
  \(1,|t|,|t|^2,|t|^3\).
- The normalized quadratic factors carry an exact signed-label
  representation. Its conductor satisfies
  \[
  2\,\operatorname{length}
  (\widetilde{\mathscr E}_p/\mathscr E_p)
  +a(W)=6\sum_{i<j}\nu_p(t_i-t_j).
  \]

## Scope

The results classify the integral defect of the signed completion and its
local representations. They do not produce an Erdős--Straus counterexample,
prove universal occupancy, turn the collision into a zeta zero, or identify a
finite-prime conductor with the fixed complex weighted conductor.
