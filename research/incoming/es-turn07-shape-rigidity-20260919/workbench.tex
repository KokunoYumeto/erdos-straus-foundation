\begin{abstract}
An original exterior state determines a marked integral cubic with coordinates
$X=a^2/u$, $Y=2a$ and defects $\alpha=X-R$, $\beta=X-D$.
The original nonresidue grade divides $\alpha\beta-1$. We construct a same-prime
middle return from every diagonal cofactor state, and prove that every hard-prime
exterior state with both defects of absolute value at most seven is on the
singular family already responsible for the preceding cutoff's sharpness.
It has an explicit middle state at its unchanged shell. Radius eight reduces to
one specified quartic. All source maps, image conditions, fibres and channel
normalizations are retained. Universal ES existence remains unproved.
\end{abstract}
