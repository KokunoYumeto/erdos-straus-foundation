\begin{abstract}
The complete local prime-power tests on original ES divisor states give a
support-indexed resolution. Its higher truncation error is an explicit
cohomology group, not an unassigned inclusion--exclusion remainder.
Combining this resolution with actual split-occurrence relations preserves
original state counts and admits a homotopy uniformly bounded by one.
The prime-jet comparison and an absolutely convergent arithmetic supertrace
are explicit. The construction does not force universal occupancy.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Original arithmetic and support.}
For a prime $p=4m+1$, retain $m+1\le a\le2m$, $R=4a-p$,
$a=\prod_iq_i^{e_i}$, $-e_i\le\beta_i\le e_i$, and
$u=\prod_iq_i^{e_i+\beta_i}$. Each original label $\alpha$ includes these
fields and $c\in\{E,M\}$. Put
\[
 g_E=4u+1,\quad g_M=u+a,\quad D_\alpha=R/\gcd(R,g_c).
\]
The inherited rational Type I/II triples are
\[
 E:\left(a,\frac{pa+a^2/u}R,\frac{pa+p^2u}R\right),\quad
 M:\left(a,\frac{p(a+a^2/u)}R,\frac{p(a+u)}R\right).
\]
Their two nonfirst reduced denominators are both $D$ by reducing their
numerators modulo $R$ and multiplying by units. The factor identity
$(RY-pa)(RZ-pa)=p^2a^2$ gives reciprocal sum $4/p$. Thus $D=1$ is the
integral criterion. At a hit let $d_0=\gcd(a,u)$,
$(h,r,s)=(d_0^2/u,u/d_0,a/d_0)$; the quotients are
$\kappa=(pr+s)/R$ in E and $\lambda=(r+s)/R$ in M.
The ordered inverses are $u=a^2/(RY-pa)$ and $u=pa^2/(RY-pa)$,
respectively. No prime exponent or channel order is discarded \cite{ET}.

Order the tests $T=\{(\ell,j):1\le j\le v_\ell(R)\}$ lexicographically.
In $V=k^{\mathscr A}$ let $B_t$ be spanned by states failing test $t$.
For $S\subseteq T$, let $F_\alpha(S)$ be its failed tests and $r_\alpha=|F_\alpha|$.
Form the cochain
\[
 C_S^{-j}=\bigoplus_{\substack{I\subseteq S\\|I|=j}}\bigcap_{t\in I}B_t,
 \qquad
 d(e_{\alpha,I})=\sum_{t\in I}(-1)^{\operatorname{pos}_I(t)}
                                     e_{\alpha,I\setminus\{t\}}.       \tag{1}
\]
For $j=0$ the intersection is the unchanged $V$. The support lattice is
$\{\bot\}\sqcup\mathcal P(T)$ with a zero complex at the additional bottom.
The empty test set is present and distinct from absence. Inclusions of
supports give actual cochain maps; reconstruction uses the original
SplitZero addition at the support join and its internal quotient \cite{SZ}.

\begin{theorem}[Full resolution and all missing higher primitives]
Over every commutative ring,
\[
 H^0(C_S)=k^{\{\alpha:F_\alpha(S)=\varnothing\}},\qquad H^{-j}(C_S)=0\ (j>0).
\]
The complete primitive on a failed block is $h z=e_{t_0}\wedge z$, with
$t_0$ its least failed test. All other primitives differ by an incoming
boundary. If (1) is truncated at degree $-d$, $d\ge1$, its extra top
cohomology on a block with $r>d$ is
\[
 \bigwedge^d\ker(\mathbf1^*:k^r\to k),\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}=\binom{r-1}{d}.                       \tag{2}
\]
Its integral basis consists of wedges of $e_i-e_{t_0}$; its inverse reads
subsets omitting $t_0$, and its omitted full primitive is $e_{t_0}\wedge z$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each block of (1) is the exterior algebra on the failed tests, in negative
degrees, with contraction $\iota$ by the all-ones covector. Expanding on
ordered basis subsets gives $\iota\varepsilon_v+\varepsilon_v\iota=\iota(v)$.
At $v=e_{t_0}$ it is the identity. A passing block consists only of its
original degree-zero coordinate. Finally split
$k^r=ke_{t_0}\oplus\ker\mathbf1^*$. Contraction sends $e_{t_0}\wedge z$
to $z$ and kills the second summand, proving (2) and every inverse.
\end{proof}
This uses the classical simplex/Koszul construction \cite{Kos}, with the
actual arithmetic labels rather than a presumed geometric realization.

\paragraph{Support changes and the original metric.}
Give every exterior basis term of state $\alpha$ its original weight $w_\alpha>0$.
Over $\mathbb Q$ or $\mathbb R$, the canonical homotopy is wedge by
$\overline e_F=r^{-1}\sum_{t\in F}e_t$. Its adjoint calculation gives
\[
 \Delta=dd^*+d^*d=rI,\qquad h=d^*\Delta^{-1},\qquad\|h\|=r^{-1/2}\le1.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
On passing blocks the harmonic projection is the identity. For $S\subseteq T$,
$K_{ST}=h_Ti_{ST}-i_{ST}h_S$ satisfies
\[
 dK_{ST}+K_{ST}d=i_{ST}\pi_S-\pi_Ti_{ST},\qquad
 K_{SU}=K_{TU}i_{ST}+i_{TU}K_{ST}.                          \tag{4}
\]
These follow by inserting $dh+hd=1-\pi$ and telescoping. The contractions
are fibrewise, not asserted natural; $K_{ST}$ records their exact defect.
A killed degree-zero class has its receiving primitive $e_{\alpha,t}$,
not an absent receiving state. If the failed counts are $0<r\le s$,
the squared norm of its change in canonical primitive is exactly
$w_\alpha(1/r-1/s)$; if $r=0<s$ it is $w_\alpha/s$.
The formula follows by computing the cross pairing $1/s$ between uniform
vectors on nested failed sets.

\paragraph{Arithmetic meaning of the higher correction.}
For a finite original atlas with $H$ hits, alternating binomial summation gives
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{j=0}^d(-1)^j\dim C^{-j}
 =H+(-1)^d\sum_{r_\alpha>0}\binom{r_\alpha-1}{d}.}         \tag{5}
\]
At $p=1129$, $a=396$, $R=455=5\cdot7\cdot13$, all 150 states fail and
the full dimensions are $(150,378,313,85)$. Every pair of local exterior
conditions has an actual divisor witness: $726,26136$ for $(5,7)$,
$16,81$ for $(5,13)$, and $432$ for $(7,13)$. None satisfies all three.
The pair-truncated value 85 is exactly its missing top cohomology, killed
by the original 85 third-support primitives. The prime itself has a witness
at $a=285,R=11,u=1$ in M: $(285,8365890,29354)$.
At $p=2689$, $a=781$, $R=435$, the dimensions are $(18,36,24,5)$.
The unique canonical hit is
\[
 E:\ (u,h,r,s,\kappa)=(8591,71,11,1,68),\quad
 (781,4828,142807412).
\]
Its pair-truncated value six returns to the correct one after (5).

\paragraph{The actual split relation direction.}
A split $e=f+b$ has complete fibre
$\max(-f_i,\beta_i-b_i)\le\xi_i\le\min(f_i,\beta_i+b_i)$,
$\eta=\beta-\xi$. Retain its entire ordered set $F_\alpha$ of size $n$.
Use $S^{-1}=\ker(q:k^{F_\alpha}\to k)\hookrightarrow S^0=k^{F_\alpha}$,
where $q$ sums coefficients. With original coefficient cost $nw_\alpha$
on each occurrence, $\sigma(c)=(c/n)_\omega$ is an isometry,
$\sigma^*=q$, and $P_S=\sigma q$ on degree zero. On degree $-1$ take
$P_S=0$. The relation metric in its star basis is $nw_\alpha(I+\mathbf1\mathbf1^*)$.
The homotopy sends $F$ to $F-\sigma qF$ in the actual kernel; hence
$\Delta_S=1-P_S$.

\begin{theorem}[Mixed double complex with constants independent of the array]
On $\mathcal T=S\otimes C$, use
$d=d_S\otimes1+(-1)^{\deg S}\otimes d_C$. The augmentation $q\otimes1$
and section $\sigma\otimes1$ are cochain maps with full fibre kernel retained.
At a state failing $r$ tests,
\[
 \boxed{\Delta_{\mathcal T}=rI+(I-P_S)\otimes I.}           \tag{6}
\]
Its Green operator is $P_S/r+(I-P_S)/(r+1)$ for $r>0$, and $I-P_S$ for
$r=0$. Both the Green and $d^*G$ have norm at most one. The harmonic image
has one direction per original successful state, not per split occurrence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The tensor sign cancels the mixed terms in $d^2$ and in the Laplacian.
Add (3) to $\Delta_S=1-P_S$. The two orthogonal sectors give (6) and its
inverse. On every positive eigenvalue $\lambda\ge1$,
$\|d^*G\|\le1/\sqrt\lambda$. Its homotopy identity gives every primitive;
the missing inverse coordinates are exactly the original split relations.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Natural return to the SplitZero prime jets.}
Put $d_{\alpha\ell}(S)=\#\{(\ell,j)\in F_\alpha(S)\}$ and
\[
 J_{\alpha,S}=k[z_\ell]_\ell/(z_\ell^{d_{\alpha\ell}(S)+1})_\ell,
 \qquad D_S=(I_S^+=\ker\epsilon\hookrightarrow J_S).
\]
The cochain map $F:C_S\to D_S$ sends degree-zero $e_\alpha$ and every
failed singleton to the same top monomial $z^{d_\alpha(S)}e_\alpha$;
it is zero in lower degrees. Singletons have positive-degree targets in
$I^+$; pair boundaries have opposite equal images. This proves $dF=Fd$.
For $S\subseteq T$ the target map multiplies by
$z^{d(T)-d(S)}$, an injection with inverse exponent subtraction on its image.
It is a module map, generally not a unital algebra map; it makes $F$ natural
and is not the opposite truncation map.
On $H^0$, the comparison includes precisely the passing coordinates into
the ordinary augmentation space. The complete mapping cone
\[
 \operatorname{Cone}(F)^n=D^n\oplus C^{n+1},\quad
 d(y,z)=(d_Dy+Fz,-d_Cz)
\]
has $H^0$ freely based by failing original states and no other cohomology.
To verify, eliminate positive-degree monomials by their identical primitives
in $I^+$ and the simplex pairs by Theorem 1. The remaining coordinates are
exactly those stated. Thus the derived top line, supported zero and actual
success remain three different observations \cite{SZ}.

\paragraph{Infinite completion with an actual trace domain.}
For a specified full-support packet, take its Hilbert amplitude direct sum.
The carrier retains the support labels separately from zero amplitudes. Use maximal
componentwise domains for $d,d^*$ and $\Delta$. Closedness follows by taking
components of graph limits; testing finitely supported vectors identifies
the adjoint domain. The Green and homotopy bounds of (6) extend uniformly,
and
\[
 \|e^{-t\Delta}-\Pi_H\|\le e^{-t}.
\]
In a split fibre the raw heat trace is
$[1+2(n-1)e^{-t}]2^re^{-tr}$; the actual split-harmonic compression has
trace $2^re^{-tr}$. Changing the metric alone would not remove the first
factor. Therefore retain the compression $P_S$ in the arithmetic trace.

If $M_s$ multiplies the prime-$p$ row by $p^{-s}$ and $t\ge\log2$, then
for $\sigma=\Re s>2$
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Str}(M_sP_Se^{-t\Delta})=\sum_p p^{-s}H_p.} \tag{7}
\]
Absolute convergence precedes cancellation: there are at most
$W_p\le4m(1+\lfloor\log_2(2m)\rfloor)^2$ original states, because
$\tau(a^2)\le d_3(a)$ and $\sum_{a\le N}d_3(a)\le N(\sum_{j\le N}1/j)^2$.
The compressed state trace is $(2e^{-t})^r\le1$.
On $2^j\le p<2^{j+1}$ we have $W_p\le pj^2$ and at most $2^j$ indices,
so the trace-norm tail after $2^L$, $L\ge3$, is bounded by
\[
 \rho^L\left(\frac{L^2}{1-\rho}+\frac{2L\rho}{(1-\rho)^2}
            +\frac{\rho(1+\rho)}{(1-\rho)^3}\right),
 \qquad \rho=2^{-(\sigma-2)}.
\]
Now each failed block has alternating sum $(1-1)^r=0$ and each successful
block contributes one, proving (7). At $\sigma=3$ the full bound is $9/2$.
No unrestricted or conditionally convergent infinite trace is asserted.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The ordinary intersection resolution and Koszul contraction are classical.
The contribution retains the actual ES tests, every prime-power threshold,
split primitives, cochain comparison and infinite trace domain. It gives an
exact account of the simultaneous-failure object and of the additional
higher relations omitted by pair truncation. It does not prove that every
prime row has $H^0\ne0$. An arithmetic lower bound on the same original
cohomology count remains necessary; higher exactness alone does not supply it.
The standalone checker covers 9531 shells, 412838 original states, all stated
examples, matrices and negative controls. No historical-priority, independent
review or Lean-build claim is made.
