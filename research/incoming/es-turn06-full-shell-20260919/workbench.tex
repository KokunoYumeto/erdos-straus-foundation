\begin{abstract}
For the complete marked first-half divisor-pair source, we prove that isolating
only the principal character and bounding the rest with the adverse sign gives
a summed lower estimate at most $-p\log p/24$ for every prime $p\equiv1\pmod4$,
$p\ge2^{20}$. The same bound holds when at most $J$ even-character modes
are retained per shell and $p\ge2^{20}J^3$, including adaptive subgroup observations. We retain nonprincipal even information
through unsaturated cosets and give a sharp integer balancing bound, a
complement-aware refinement, free fourfold original pair orbits, and complete
marked denominator returns. All four failures of the principal-only test in
the stated two-million hard-prime census have certified nonprincipal or
integer repairs. A proper five-mode observation also attains the target lower
bound at a prime-order quotient with no intermediate subgroup. A universal positive lower bound for the full ES pair sum is
not established.
\end{abstract}
