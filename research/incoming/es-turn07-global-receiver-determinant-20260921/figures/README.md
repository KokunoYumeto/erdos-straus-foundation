# Receiver-determinant figures

`coordinate_domains.tex` records the exact exterior and middle parameter
domains used by the sign proof. `determinant_mechanism.tex` records every map
from the literal witness through the complex receiving matrix to the lower
determinant and inverse-norm bounds. Both are TikZ sources included directly by
the TeX readers, so labels, constants and coordinates remain reproducible.

The figures distinguish continuous certificate regions from the arithmetic
witness points inside them. The exterior boundary `w=1` and the middle equality
point `(X,Y)=(0,0)` are shown as excluded limits, not as integral witnesses.
