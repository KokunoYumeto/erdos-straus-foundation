\begin{abstract}
For the literal prime--denominator quartic of an integral Erd\H{o}s--Straus
witness, we prove strict negativity of the original odd-receiver determinant
numerator at every prime $p\equiv1\pmod{12}$. Explicit universal constants give
$-\mathfrak N>cp^8$ and $\|O^{-1}\|_2<360S^9/(cp^8)$. The proof uses the complete
Type I/II domain and two exact positive-coefficient polynomial certificates.
This settles the arithmetic nonvanishing question isolated in the supplied
programme audit, without its three-character restriction. It does not prove
that an ES witness exists at every prescribed prime. Full integer coefficients,
source maps, negative controls and two standard-library verification procedures
are supplied.
\end{abstract}
