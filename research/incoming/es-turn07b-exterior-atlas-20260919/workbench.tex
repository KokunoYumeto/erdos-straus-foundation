\begin{abstract}
We retain the original exterior residual $R$, its cofactor $D$, and the
complementary divisor $v=a^2/u$. Every unit first-half exterior state satisfies
$(p+w)^2>4(w+1)^2(w-1)$ for $w=\min(v,R,D)$. This yields a complete disjoint
three-chart atlas whose outer parameter cutoff is
$(p/2)^{2/3}+O(p^{1/3})$. The complement chart is an actual norm-divisor
construction with the additional indispensable square-divisor availability
condition. An original hard-prime state disproves transferring the preceding
middle square-root cutoff to exterior cofactors. An explicit unit integer
family establishes asymptotic sharpness in that stated domain. The universal
existence assertion remains unproved.
\end{abstract}
