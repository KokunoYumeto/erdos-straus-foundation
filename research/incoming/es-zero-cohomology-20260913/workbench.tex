\begin{abstract}
We instantiate the existing SplitZero internal quotient and original-Gram
construction on the full labelled divisor boxes of an ES shell. A
three-term finite cochain removes internal cancellations with explicit
primitives; its degree-zero classes are exactly common channel/residue
fibres. We compute their canonical metric and distinguish their dimension
from both matched occurrences and original divisor-state counts. An exact
inverse-fibre correction recovers every arithmetic mass and the entire
original denominator-defect polynomial. Iterated quotients agree only
when their inherited metrics are retained. All maps, inverses, empty
fibres, coarsening kernels, and the original ES reconstruction are stated.
No universal ES or RH claim or historical-priority conclusion is made.
\end{abstract}
