\title{Relative boundary packets and lifting actual Erd\H{o}s--Straus factors}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{16 September 2026 --- research continuation}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We replace full cyclic coverage by a relative kernel generated by available
bounded factors. A disjoint mixed-radix decomposition preserves every original
exponent and identifies the exact quotient target to be lifted. The relative
norm is a nonzero boundary image whose ordinary quotient can be a supported
zero. We give complete maps, inverse fibres and original ES reconstruction,
a sharp all-multiplicity one-prime lifting theorem, and a bound on the returned
cofactor length. A certified hard-prime example succeeds beyond the previous
full-affine and short-word tests; another prime shows why a coarse target does
not lift without its factor capacity. No universal ES or priority claim is made.
\end{abstract}
