\section{Complete fibres and the common-modulus return}
This section expands the arithmetic and geometric statements above. No
analytic compactification or global six-manifold theorem is used.

\paragraph{Labelled state domain.}
A split state is the full tuple
\[
(p,a,(q_i,e_i)_i,f,b,\xi,\eta,\ell,t),
\]
where $p$ and $a$ lie in the declared first-half domain, $f+b=e$,
$\xi\in B_f$, $\eta\in B_b$, and $(\ell,t)$ is one of the three
labelled targets. The pair $(\xi,\eta)$ is retained even when two pairs
sum to the same vector. The primewise interval formula in (2) gives the
complete fibre of this addition map. Numerical target deduplication occurs
only when forming a forbidden set. A selected factor label is never inferred
from its residue when those residues coincide.

\paragraph{Defect before positivity is decided.}
For every divisor $u\mid a^2$, put $d=a^2/u$ and retain
\[
 Q_E=\left(a,\frac{pa+d}{R},\frac{pa+p^2u}{R}\right),\qquad
 Q_M=\left(a,\frac{p(a+d)}R,\frac{p(a+u)}R\right).
\]
All entries are positive rationals. Writing their last two entries as
$Y,Z$, the residual pair is $(d,p^2u)$ in E and $(pd,pu)$ in M.
Thus $(RY-pa)(RZ-pa)=p^2a^2$, and expansion proves
$1/a+1/Y+1/Z=4/p$. In E, multiplying the first numerator by the unit $u$
gives $a^2(4u+1)\pmod R$, and the second numerator is
$4a^2(4u+1)\pmod R$. In M, multiplying the first numerator by $u$
gives $pa(a+u)$, and the second is $p(a+u)$. All displayed multipliers
are units modulo $R$. The two reduced denominators are consequently equal:
\[
 D_E=\frac{R}{\gcd(R,4u+1)},\qquad
 D_M=\frac{R}{\gcd(R,u+a)}.
\]
No gcd is taken across different leaves. The ordered integral triple
$DQ$ solves the equation at $pD$. Retaining $p$ and $D$ gives its inverse
$Q=(DQ)/D$; forgetting that scale could confuse a failed return at $p$ with
a successful return at the different parameter $pD$.

\paragraph{Every local prime power is retained.}
The unit identity (7) gives, for every $\lambda^a\parallel R$,
\[
\max(a-v_\lambda(g),0)=\max(a-v_\lambda(\rho),0).
\]
The notation $v_\lambda(0)=+\infty$ is used when a gate vanishes, or
one may replace this display by equality of the two gcd quotients at
modulus $\lambda^a$. The same original integer exponent vectors are
reduced at every prime power. Separate local choices do not give a common
bounded vector without the fibre (2).

\paragraph{Actual affine inverse.}
Write each generator as $A_j^g(v)=B_jv+g b_j$, with the three matrices and
vectors read from the displayed formulas above. If $g=c\rho$ in
$\mathbb Z/R\mathbb Z$, then
\[
 c(B_jv+\rho b_j)=B_j(cv)+g b_j.
\]
The map $S_c(v)=cv$, with inverse $S_{c^{-1}}$, therefore intertwines each
generator, all its inverse powers, and every word in the generators.
This establishes an isomorphism of the full finite affine systems, not
merely equality of an invariant or agreement on successful points.

Their coefficient cocycle has exact order $R/\gcd(R,g)$. At the cusp,
its value is $(0,0,-g)$ and
$(B_\infty-I)(X,Y,Z)=(0,X,0)$. Third-coordinate evaluation consequently
annihilates every coboundary and detects order $R/\gcd(R,g)$. That same
integer annihilates every coefficient of the cocycle, proving the reverse
bound. This proves the exact order without assuming the source's global
complex-geometric claim.

\paragraph{Counterfactual scope.}
For a false ES prime, every candidate in the complete first-half atlas
must have $D>1$, and every split and every character cell must obey (5).
This is a stronger family of necessary conditions. It is not asserted that
the conditions cannot hold simultaneously. In particular the present unit
conjugacy preserves $D$; it is not itself a defect-reducing cross-shell move.
The sufficient criterion may force a target when an unsplit moment test
does not, as in the displayed example. It does not manufacture a target by
replacing the actual exponent box with its generated group.

\paragraph{Execution boundary.}
The standalone core verifier compares the greedy optimizer with an
independent exhaustive dynamic programme on the specified small unit groups.
It checks all splits in first-half shells for $p=13,37,61,97$, all overlaps
in the declared two-coordinate boxes, four empty shells, and the full
9531-shell comparison. The companion verifier checks 12042 labelled split
states at $p=5,13,37,61,97$, including 11658 unsuccessful states, plus full
finite affine maps at levels $3,5,9,15$. All requested labels and original
rational quotients are retained in its example outputs. These finite checks
are distinct from the general proofs. No independent reviewer or Lean
execution is claimed.
