\begin{abstract}
The target-pullback construction applies exact collision minima to a split
of the original bounded prime-exponent box. Complete addition fibres retain
the nonconstant multiplicity of overlapping splits. We give a strict example
beyond the earlier quadratic-character criterion, all ordered arithmetic
inverses, and a unit-valued isomorphism of the associated affine monodromy
systems. Failed candidates keep their denominator defects, not just their
projective shape. The result is a sufficient theorem and exact counterfactual
constraint, not a proof of universal ES existence.
\end{abstract}
