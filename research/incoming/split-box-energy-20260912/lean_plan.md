# Lean-ready dependencies

No Lean execution is claimed. Work over a finite abelian group, then specialize
to the units of ZMod R after proving every divisor prime is invertible.

1. Define labelled targets, nonnegative budget vectors and centered integer boxes.
2. Prove addition B_f x B_b -> B_(f+b), with the exact coordinate interval fibre.
3. Prove nonemptiness of that fibre, hence surjectivity.
4. Count its cardinality as the product of interval lengths.
5. Prove residue_add and the labelled target-pullback equivalence.
6. Prove weighted_convolution; do not replace its fibre weights by one.
7. Prove inversion_fibres, unique fixed exponent zero, and involution parity.
8. Show elementary-2 character cells are inversion-stable.
9. Bijection between permitted integer distributions and mass-pair allocations.
10. Prove the three quadratic marginal-cost formulas and all infeasible cases.
11. Prove greedy optimality by exchange of selected and unselected costs.
12. On no-hit, place the actual partial distribution in the feasible set.
13. Derive the strict energy forcing implication and character-refinement rule.
14. Recover the full beta, its recorded exterior inversion, and original divisor.
15. Prove gcd/valuation normalization to h,r,s, gates and positive quotients.
16. Check the cleared reciprocal identity and the ordered inverse divisor map.
17. Prove all three gate-unit identities, including x^-1 for inverted exterior.
18. Prove equality of gcd quotients globally and at every prime power.
19. Prove scalar affine intertwining on each source generator and its inverse.
20. Derive exact cocycle order by the third cusp coordinate and annihilation.
21. Replay the finite example distribution, optimum, witness, overlap example,
    and declared empty shells. Keep the larger scan separately bounded.

Imported first-half completeness is only needed for the global atlas statement,
not for soundness of a specified shell. No universal successful split, analytic
compactification, global S6 theorem, or novelty claim is a premise.
