# Exact arithmetic maps and their information

All prime-domain maps use p prime, p=1 mod4, p/4<a<p/2, R=4a-p. The narrower
prime-square statements use p=1 mod24 and q>3 prime. No extra chamber condition
is imposed. A positive-Koide-chamber test is a separate condition on the retained
r,s, not an invariant asserted for every returned state.

1. Original word -> primitive coordinates: u|a^2, g=gcd(a,u),
   (h,r,s)=(g^2/u,u/g,a/g). Inverse a=hrs,u=hr^2. The full exponent vector
   f in [0,2e] and centred beta=f-e are retained.
2. E or M -> rational ordered tails: core.tex (E),(M), with quotient named
   kappa or lambda. Full gate R|4u+1 or R|p+4u. Trace gate squares that same
   integer. Ordered inverse u=a^2/(Ry-pa) in E, u=pa^2/(Ry-pa) in M.
3. Ordered rational pair with integral sum -> original word: the factors
   Ry-pa,Rz-pa have product (pa)^2. Their p-valuations determine E/M; E
   requires its one recorded swap. An M word already determines its tail order.
4. Original trace -> fine defect: K=prod l^ceil(v_l(R)/2),D=R/K,
   z=p^eta*u/a=-1+K*c modR, c in Z/D. The ideal KZ/R is square-zero.
   This projection forgets the actual word; its complete bounded fibres are
   enumerated by the part map below. Additive order(c)=actual tail denominator.
5. Mixed integer combination -> original trace: N=sum n odd,
   eta*=sum n*eta must be 0 or1 exactly, beta*=sum n*beta inside [-e,e].
   u*=a^(1-N)prod u^n and c*=sum n*c. The exact original trace domains are
   still imposed on every input. For E1-E2+M, retaining the other two words
   inverts the operation. Unmarked multiple-input fibres need not be singletons.
6. Coarse-neutral exponent line: gamma with prod q^gamma=1modK,
   lambda=(prod q^gamma-1)/K modD, beta(t)=beta0+t*gamma within the actual
   floor/ceiling interval. Defect c0-lambda*t; full indices form its explicit
   congruence class modulo D/gcd(D,lambda), or are absent. No free period is added.
7. Complete box partition: beta_i=b_i+ord_K(q_i)*t_i, 0<=t_i<N_i.
   This is a bijection after keeping the original part b. Its integer
   geometric coefficient polynomial records every word once. Projection onto
   actual defect bins has kernel generated by differences in each occupied
   fibre. The zero bin need not be occupied. Coefficient stabilizers, not just
   support stabilizers, are computed by the exact finite Fourier zero test.
8. Prime-power M exponents: half coarse order b, exact defect order d_b,
   full half order B=b*d_b. Traces/full states are the bounded odd multiples
   of b/B respectively; signs and both original tail orientations are retained.
9. General factor-sum arrow: R=delta*t^2, keep raw r and h+s, put
   h'=h-delta*w,s'=s+delta*w. Exact test r(delta*w^2+(s-h)*w)=(t^2-1)/4.
   Primitive target gcd c=gcd(r,s') must be retained. Target marking is
   (h'c^2,r/c,s'/c,(r+s')/(c*delta)); target word h'r^2. All target prime
   exponents are recalculated. Positivity follows from the positive product
   (p+delta)/(4r) and unchanged positive sum. Proper sources strictly decrease a.
10. Complete inverse of 9: enumerate c^2|h0; raw h'=h0/c^2,r=c*r0,s'=c*s0;
    then -h'/delta<w<s'/delta with the exact positive odd-square and trace
    tests in core.tex. Mark(c,w) restores every input. Its fibres can have
    three or five members in the serialized examples. No canonical c=1 is imposed.
11. Pell restriction: r=1,h=s=q -> t^2-4delta*w^2=1. With q=delta*t*k-1,
    p=4q^2-delta*t^2, the integer identity and all marks are explicit; p and q
    can be composite. The three claimed prime examples have deterministic
    primality checks. The matrix recurrence constructs infinite integer
    parameters, not infinitely many prime values.
12. Fixed numerical tail-sum fibre: for N=Y+Z, map an ordered integral target
    to rho=4A-p and Delta=Z-Y. Its complete inverse is
    A=(p+rho)/4,Y=(N-Delta)/2,Z=(N+Delta)/2, subject exactly to rho|N,
    0<rho<p, rho=-p mod4, Delta^2=N(N-p)-Np^2/rho, positivity and parity.
    Writing rho=delta*v^2 and X=v*Delta gives the exact Pell lattice
    X^2-N(N-p)v^2=-Np^2/delta, together with delta*v^2|N, v|X and
    X=Nv mod 2v. This map retains Y+Z, not h+s. In shifted factors the norm
    equation alone is larger than the target: rho|w is the missing integer-tail
    gate. Its failure is retained as an exact defect, not discarded.
13. Prime-square fixed-sum divisor obstruction: for a proper source at
    a=q^2, write its auxiliary H as p+q, pq+1 or q+1 according to the marked
    E/M word. The canonical exact factorization
    R=k0*eta^2, H=k0*eta*F has eta>1 and gcd(R,N)=k0. Any same-N integral
    target at S|R forces S|k0, stays in the source channel by p-adic parity,
    and forces q to divide its gcd factor h. The retained equations then give
    separate contradictions for E,q; E,q^3; and the two M orientations. The
    map does not identify k0 with the square-zero modulus K. It leaves
    S<R with S not dividing R, and every change of N, outside its domain.
14. Literature crosswalk: M -> ET tuple(s,r,lambda,h,R,4hs*lambda-1),
    preserving the denominators under ET (2.22). E ->
    (r,kappa,s,h,(r+kappa)/s,R), with ET output cyclically (z,a,y).
    The maps return ET integer points only after the original full gates.

The new square-zero ideal is not identified with a Split-Zero cohomology
class, a 4-label receiver space, or an absent/non-absent support type without
an additional proved connecting map. Its demonstrated role is the exact
fine additive defect of these original bounded integer coefficient arrays.
