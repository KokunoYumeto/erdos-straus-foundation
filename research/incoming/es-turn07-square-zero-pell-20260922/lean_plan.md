# Exact Lean-ready dependency plan (not a compiled Lean file)

No Lean toolchain or theorem build is claimed. The proof skeleton below specifies
propositions and dependencies rather than inserting axioms, `sorry` placeholders,
or a favourable-source hypothesis as the conjecture's conclusion.

## Integer and rational layer

L01. For p prime1mod4 and p/4<a<p/2: R=4a-p is odd, R=3mod4,
     3<=R<p, gcd(pa,R)=1.
L02. Primewise normalization is a bijection between u|a^2 and primitive
     h,r,s>0 with a=hrs,u=hr^2. Prove valuations h=e-|f-e|,
     r=max(f-e,0),s=max(e-f,0).
L03. Cleared polynomial versions of both original reciprocal identities.
L04. Original E/M congruences are equivalent to normalized pr+s/r+s gates.
L05. Ordered denominator inverses recover u and L02's primitive coordinates.
L06. For positive rational tails with integral sum, their reduced denominators
     coincide; valuation comparison proves d^2=R/gcd(R,N).
L07. Positive integral factors Ry-pa,Rz-pa multiply to (pa)^2; classify their
     p-valuations and recover both channels, retaining the E swap.
L08. Character parity for traces from quadratic reciprocity at each prime
     factor. Prove only rad(R)|G from a square gate, not R|G.

## Finite additive layer

L09. Define K,D,delta by valuations; prove R=KD=delta*D^2, D|K,
     delta squarefree3mod4, D^2<=R/3.
L10. In ZMod R the ideal generated by K has square zero, with underlying
     additive isomorphism ZMod D -> ideal, c |-> K*c.
L11. Trace z=p^eta*u/a is exactly -1+K*c; full gate iff c=0;
     additive order(c)=D/gcd(D,c)=original tail denominator.
L12. (1+v)^n=1+n*v for square-zero v and n integer, with the inverse case.
L13. Exact mixed exponent law, with odd integer N, literal eta in{0,1}, and
     beta inside the original finite box; its word integrality is a conclusion
     of that box, not inferred from a residue calculation.
L14. Coarse-neutral line formula and complete finite congruence count.
L15. Coordinatewise partition [-e_i,e_i] by ord_K(q_i), bijection and inverse.
L16. In AddMonoidAlgebra Z (ZMod D), geometric product coefficients count
     exact original t-vectors. State extraction separately from basis elements.
L17. Nonempty proper finite subset plus a generating step increases cardinality.
L18. Repeated L17 proves the unit-width saturation lower bound. Use repeated
     binary factors only for support; retain geometric integer coefficients.
L19. Complex finite Fourier coefficient of a geometric factor has the exact
     zero criterion. Derive the coefficient stabilizer via Fourier inversion.
L20. Free Z-module map onto actual occupied bins: kernel differences and rank.
     No target section exists if the target bin is absent.
L21. Sharp width packet: use a parameterized reduced progression theorem
     separately as the classical Dirichlet input. All congruence/valuation
     identities and finite templates are pure integer lemmas. Do not infer
     prime values of the Pell expression from this theorem.

## Prime powers and new arithmetic return

L22. With an M trace at q^e, character parity makes beta odd. Coarse cyclic
     order=2b, q^b=-1. Fine order=2b*d_b by L10--L12. Enumerate all exponents.
L23. For q^2: q=2mod3; exactly four possible channel/word pairs. Prove the
     displayed 15-resultant identity and the R3/R75 collision cases.
L24. Proper M q^2 trace terminality for every residual divisor: convert k,v
     to 1+qA,1+qB and derive qAB+A+B=q+2 contradiction.
L25. Low-E q^2 trace same-word deletion classification k=4q+1.
L26. Factor-sum expansion: h'=h-delta*w,s'=s+delta*w. The new residual equals
     delta iff r(delta*w^2+(s-h)w)=(t^2-1)/4.
L27. Positive product plus positive sum forces h',s'>0; full raw M gate;
     target word availability and exact quotient restoration by c=gcd(r,s').
L28. Complete quadratic integer-root test and at-most-two forward arrows.
L29. Full inverse fibre indexed by c^2|h0 and finite w interval. Prove both
     compositions including primitive gcd recovery. This is not only an r=1 inverse.
L30. Pell specialization and integer family; prove source denominator t/gcd(t,k),
     positivity and first-half range. Prime checks are separate optional inputs.
L31. Recurrence matrix preserves t^2-12w^2 and has order12mod840 by exact finite
     powers. Prove infinite integer parameter sequence; no prime-supply conclusion.
L32. Fixed numerical tail-sum identity: from Y+Z=N derive
     rho*(Z-Y)^2=N*((N-p)*rho-p^2), prove rho|N for prime p and 0<rho<p,
     and construct the exact ordered inverse with positivity and parity gates.
L33. Unique squareclass rho=delta*v^2 and the fixed-sum Pell lattice. Prove
     X^2-N(N-p)v^2=-Np^2/delta with every divisibility/congruence gate. Derive
     the shifted-factor norm equation and prove rho|w is equivalent to integer
     tails; do not identify an unrestricted norm point with an exact return.
L34. Prime-square fixed-sum divisor obstruction. Define the local factor
     k0 by R=k0*eta^2 and H=k0*eta*F without reusing the square-zero modulus K.
     Prove gcd(R,N)=k0, exact E/M target decoding with the tail-order bit,
     channel rigidity by p-adic parity, q|h, and the three contradiction
     branches. Retain the exact chain A<q^2<p/2 and S>=3 in the E,q^3 bound.

## Exhaustive finite certificate layer

L35. First-half completeness of every positive integer witness at p1mod4,
     including the Type-I inequality proof in core.tex.
L36. Correct finite sieve and full trial division tests; divisor enumeration
     completeness by unique factorization.
L37. Define firstTrace/firstFull with option-valued or infinity minima.
L38. Verified finite computation for all primes1mod24<=67369 and every original
     word through each first full shell; derive leastness and the obstruction
     to an everywhere nonincreasing-a repair. This is the only theorem relying
     essentially on the displayed finite exhaustion.
L39. Check all 69 proper trace tags and 51 distinct sums at p67369. For each,
     enumerate the complete residual-divisor list supplied by L32 and reject
     every discriminant by an exact square or modular certificate. Check the
     positive R31 recovery separately.
L40. Check the three isolated Pell primes and q carriers, general gcd2 example,
     incoming fibres, mixed p48049 return, and explicit outside-box rejection.

Prioritize L26--L34 and L09--L16 for universal coordinate formalization. The
classical prime-supply theorem and the finite leastness proof need independent
imports/certificate kernels. A future build must pin its actual Lean/mathlib
versions and record commands and axioms; this plan does not invent those pins.
