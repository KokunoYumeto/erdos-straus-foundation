\title[ES trace defects and Pell returns]{Bounded gate defects, factor-sum returns and fixed-tail Pell fibres\newline for Erd\H{o}s--Straus trace sources}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{22 September 2026}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
An original rational Erd\H{o}s--Straus trace has an exact additive defect in a
square-zero ideal modulo its residual. We retain the full bounded prime
exponents and both channel types, derive their mixed composition law, and
partition the complete original source into finite cyclic coefficient
polynomials. A sharp unit-direction saturation theorem forces a full gate on
specified original parts. Prime-square middle traces are terminal for both
inherited residual-divisor descent systems, but a different factor-sum arrow
has a complete quadratic inverse and a Pell specialization with verified
hard-prime examples. An exhaustive certificate identifies $67369$ as the least
prime $1\bmod24$ at which the first trace precedes every full original gate;
thus arbitrary-source repair cannot universally decrease the distinguished
denominator. A second exact Pell lattice classifies every target that retains
the numerical tail sum. A universal four-case argument proves that no proper
prime-square trace has such a return at a proper divisor of its source
residual; the complete certificate also proves that all 51 proper trace sums
at $p=67369$ have empty integral fibres. These are arithmetic source results,
not post-witness spectral bounds or a universal existence theorem.
\end{abstract}
