The fixed-tail-sum continuation proves a different obstruction. At p67369 the
69 proper trace tags have 51 distinct numerical sums, none shared by the 37
integral sums. The exact divisor-and-square classifier checks 507 eligible
residuals and finds no integral fixed-sum target. This does not obstruct the
factor-sum map, which retains h+s instead of Y+Z, or any map that changes the
numerical tail sum.

The same exact fibre also yields a universal prime-square result. For every
proper E/M trace at a=q^2 with word q or q^3, no integral target with the same
N can have residual a proper divisor of R. The proof first factors the source
as R=k0*eta^2 and H=k0*eta*F, then proves gcd(R,N)=k0. Exact target decoding
forces the target channel to equal the source channel and q to divide its gcd
factor. The E,q; E,q^3; and paired M cases then give separate contradictions.
The new k0 is deliberately not identified with the earlier square-zero
modulus K. Smaller nondivisor residuals remain outside the theorem.
