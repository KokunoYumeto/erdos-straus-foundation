# Stage 6--7 remainder after the arithmetic continuation

The universal first-source theorem remains unproved. Do not treat finite
return tests, a full fibre inverse, a coefficient stabilizer or receiver
invertibility as a nonempty zero coefficient at every prescribed prime.

The genuinely new positive arrows in this tranche are:
- exact additive fine-defect cancellation among E/M traces when the channel
  exponent is literally 0 or 1 and the resulting integer prime exponents
  remain in the original box;
- unit-direction saturation of a complete coarse trace part, with sharp width
  D-1, exact multiplicities and coefficient stabilizer;
- factor-sum return (all primitive r), with its quadratic test, target gcd,
  ordered witness, complete incoming fibre and Pell specialization.
- a complete fixed-numerical-tail-sum fibre, expressed both as a finite
  divisor-and-square test and as a Pell lattice with all recovery gates. At
  p67369 all 51 proper trace sums have empty integral fibres; the unrestricted
  norm point is retained and its failed congruence is explicit.
- a universal prime-square fixed-sum obstruction: a proper E/M trace at
  a=q^2 has no same-N integral return whose residual is a proper divisor of
  the source residual, even after changing word, channel or tail order.

All require the stated actual source. The code constructs every original box
part at a fixed p, so a failure object is the complete list of its zero target
coefficients, not a dyadic or generated-subgroup replacement. Counterfactual
ES failure forces every squarefree shell to lack traces, every squareful trace
part to miss its fine zero, every such unit width to be <=D-2, every supplied
M trace to fail the factor-sum root test, and every prime-power M trace to have
e < b*d_b. These simultaneous implications are not yet contradictory.

Critical new route-selection gate: p=67369 has its first trace at R27 and its
first full gate only at R31. Every lower prime1mod24 has equal first trace and
full residuals in the certified prefix. Thus any proposed theorem repairing
EVERY trace cannot universally demand a'<=a. A proof can select a suitable
initial trace, use a different decreasing invariant, or allow controlled
upward shell moves. Do not try to strengthen the existing a-descent into a
false universal statement.

At prime-square M traces the old word and ray deletion systems are terminal;
that does not imply failure of other maps. The Pell return crosses them at
p27409, p5382049 and p853922067121. At p2003761 the general map crosses a
composite source with raw r2 and target gcd2; retain the latter in its inverse.
The fixed-N theorem rules out only residual divisors of the source residual.
Smaller nondivisor residuals and every map that changes N remain open.

No priority claim, prime infinitude for the Pell expression, uniform positive
full-shell count, Lean build or universal ES result has been established.
