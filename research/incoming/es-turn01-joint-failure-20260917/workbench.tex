\begin{abstract}
We use the actual stabilizer of the complete centered divisor box and retain
both Erd\H{o}s--Straus targets and their inversion label. For arbitrary
residual modulus, a coefficient envelope and every original factor cut give
explicit joint defect budgets. All effective quotients of order at most eight
are classified: deleting at most two prime occurrences leaves an actual
saturated kernel block. Target collisions, fine integer multiplicities and
the nonsplit extension carry are retained. The prime-shift index-six
specialization is attributed to the existing literature/workbench source.
This is a structural reduction, not a universal ES occupancy theorem.
\end{abstract}
