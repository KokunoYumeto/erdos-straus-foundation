# Exact Lean-ready dependency plan (no build claimed)

1. Define the original E and M records over positive naturals, including p-prime,
   p mod4, first-half inequalities, a-square divisibility, channel tags and ordered
   denominators. Prove gcd normalization by prime valuations, plus field identity.
2. Prove the E shape identities. Establish Jacobi(d/R)=Legendre(d/p) primewise,
   including the supplementary law for 2, then the negative-four grade constraints.
3. Define Capacity(t,j) as a Finset of the divisors of j*j with the exact residue.
   Prove the three-case partition using n,V,k,ell and integer Euclidean division.
4. Construct FiniteTemplate(t) by ell in [1,(t-2)/4], w|ell^2 and exact n division.
   Prove both directions and the inverse maps without a source-subgroup surrogate.
5. Prove the grade bound by cross-multiplying the positive denominators in the
   monotonic rational expression; prove its equality cases. Include the companion
   as a separate unbounded branch and the h<=t finite fallback.
6. Formalize the complete cofactor normalization U|j^2 -> original M record.
   Prove positive range p>2h, coprimality and the exact ordered divisor inverse.
7. Prove automatic alpha/beta lines. The beta mod3 case is a finite residue lemma
   checked in ZMod 3; the mod8 parity proof retains r+kappa=sD.
8. For infinite prime realization, state the exact reduced-progression theorem
   used (Dirichlet), prove every CRT gcd and residue, then invoke it. Separate
   the sharp-family result from the all-t obstruction construction.
9. Prove the finite quadratic-character coset count via CRT and a nontrivial
   positive quadratic character on units congruent to one modulo four. Derive
   singleton iff t=1,2,3 with a totient classification on multiples of four.
10. Verify examples and finite tables by native computation; their bounds are not
    used to assert universal prime occupancy. Full source lists and finite word
    fibres must match, not merely their totals.
11. Formalize the corrected alpha inverse domain with the E gate explicitly.
    The complete counterexample to omitting that gate is a Nat calculation.

No theorem or axiom of global ES/Type-II existence is permitted in this plan.
The ordinary and optimized Python replays are not Lean certificates.
