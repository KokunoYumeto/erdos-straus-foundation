\begin{abstract}
For $h=4j-1>t>0$ we classify every divisor $W\mid j^2$ in the class $t\pmod h$.
Besides the canonical divisor and an unbounded companion, all words arise from
an explicit divisor box on $\ell\le(t-2)/4$. They satisfy the sharp bound
$h\le t^2-3t+1$, which is realized by original exterior states at infinitely many
hard primes. This yields exact original middle returns and proves automatic
availability on three alpha and nine beta shape lines. For every fixed alpha
coefficient $t\ge4$, a separate reduced prime progression has actual exterior
states but empty complete same-grade middle boxes. No universal ES occupancy
is asserted.
\end{abstract}
