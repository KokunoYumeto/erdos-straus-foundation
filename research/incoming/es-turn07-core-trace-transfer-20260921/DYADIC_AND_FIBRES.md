# Additional exterior return and retained fibres

The Clankers, 21 September 2026. This note extends, rather than changes, the original source in the preprint.

## Dyadic exterior theorem

Let p be prime with p=1 mod 24. Let a=2^e q, q an odd prime, p/4<a<p/2, e>=1. An original rational E trace candidate is a divisor u of a^2 satisfying R | (4u+1)^2, R=4a-p. The character calculation in the preprint forces q to be a nonresidue and u=2^f q with 0<=f<=2e.

Write R=Q v^2 with Q its squarefree part, j=(Q+1)/4, and t=v_2(j). Since a is even, R=7 mod 8; v is odd, hence Q=7 mod 8 and t>=1. Put z=e-f. The exact reduced gate is U = 2^(z-2) mod Q.

The following three numbers have that residue:

    U0 = 2^(z-2),  U1 = 2^z j,  U2 = 2^(z+2) j^2.

Their complete integer-divisor-of-j^2 domains are respectively

    2 <= z <= 2t+2,
    -t <= z <= t,
    -2t-2 <= z <= -2.

These three integer intervals cover [-2t-2,2t+2]. Thus e<=2t+2 guarantees an available word for every original f. In particular e<=4 always suffices. The cofactor inverse in the preprint returns the original middle state. No rational expression outside its availability interval is rounded or treated as a divisor.

A deterministic selection is: use U0 when z>=2; otherwise use U1 when z>=-t; otherwise use U2. The chosen source label remains attached. If the old tails are nonintegral, Q<R. The new residual may increase.

At p=12049, a=3064=8*383, R=207, u=64*383, the old E tails are 535051/3 and 51574635992/3. Here Q=23,j=6,z=-3, so U=j^2/2=18. The exact target is (a',R',u',h',r',s',lambda')=(3144,527,18,2,3,524,1), with denominators (3144,12627352,72294).

## Source and coefficient inverse

For an automatic-core target read j=h' r' lambda', Q=4j-1 and U=u'. For each retained c in {1,2,3,6}, let d=U for M or d=cj/U for E. Require d to be an integer divisor of c^2 and c|j. Choose odd v with Qv^2<p, reconstruct q=(p+Qv^2)/(4c), and test its integrality, primality, and gcd(q,c)=1. Reconstruct u=qd for E or u=d,a^2/d for the two M orientations. The original squared gate at R=Qv^2 must still be tested. This is the complete finite fibre; a gate at Q alone is not substituted for it.

For the dyadic map, retain e,f and the selected branch above, use c=2^e in the same finite v-range, and perform the original squared-gate test. Substituting the reconstructed j and f into the deterministic selection verifies exactly which preimages have the stated output.

At fixed p,Q,c, the automatic E and M words collide exactly when d_E*d_M=cj. Both M orientations have the same selected output. Other collisions are given by the full finite inverse fibre. On free integer modules, pushforward sums original coefficients, with kernel generated by differences of original words in the same fibre. Reducing a positive even coefficient modulo two does not remove its original support.

The original a^2 box, the j^2 cofactor box, and the new a'^2 box retain separate factorizations. The common returned U and the gcd maps connect them. No unrestricted subgroup or independent prime inventory replaces any box.
