# Turn 4: explicit maps, fibres and information loss



21 maps are registered. The maps act on different original domains; no map is inferred merely from a common name or cardinality.



## T4-M01: Marked square source

**Domain:** p hard prime; q<p prime with (q/p)=-1; t odd; sigma(q) q t^2<3p

**Codomain:** Original integer source (p,q,sigma,t,R0,R,A)

**Map:** R0=sigma(q)q; R=R0t^2; A=(p+R)/4

**Inverse / fibres:** Retain q,t,sigma; A returns R=4A-p. R alone need not identify the original square marking.

**Information retained or lost:** No q or square marking is dropped. A varies with t.

**Proof:** core.tex Section 2, equation (1)



## T4-M02: Centered exponent box

**Domain:** A=product l^e; beta_l in [-e,e]

**Codomain:** Divisors u of A^2, including all distinct words

**Map:** u=product l^(e+beta_l)

**Inverse / fibres:** beta_l=v_l(u)-e_l; bijective

**Information retained or lost:** Reducing u modulo R is a separate many-to-one observation.

**Proof:** Section 2, equation (2)



## T4-M03: Fine residue count

**Domain:** Free integer module on original beta vectors

**Codomain:** Integer coefficients on (Z/R)^x

**Map:** v(beta)=u A^(-1) mod R; sum coefficients along equal v

**Inverse / fibres:** Fibre consists of the original exponent vectors with that residue; least representative plus all difference coordinates reconstructs it.

**Information retained or lost:** Support loses multiplicity; parity can send a present coefficient to supported zero.

**Proof:** Section 2



## T4-M04: Divisor normalization

**Information retained or lost:** None with source and channel retained.

**Proof:** Section 2, equation (4)
