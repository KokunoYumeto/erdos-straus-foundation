# Turn 5 handoff: force the two channels to interact after mixed source expansion

## Established this turn

1. For every hard prime, every set of at most five original external nonresidue prime vertices has an exit among its valid multipliers 1,9,25,49,81. No stabilizer-index restriction and no all-3-mod-4 restriction remain. A breadth-first construction reaches six distinct vertices in at most 25 original factorizations.
2. For any fixed cardinality m, closed source sets force `p < (4H)^m < 16^(m^2)`. The prefix b and H are explicit. Original cycle coefficients lie in a finite, coordinate-level domain. For `p >= 16^(m^2)`, a bounded prefix of ports produces m+1 reachable vertices from every start.
3. The complete at-most-five cofactor reduction has three remaining cycles only. All are killed by actual multiplier-nine factors. The enumeration retains every typed rejected profile, not only the successes. Normal and optimized main replays and a separate implementation are recorded.
4. Square residual lifting is exactly the integer carry `(4u+eta)/R0 = d + t^2 b`; the original u returns from d,b and the marked source. Independent mod-R0 and mod-t^2 tests are insufficient when those moduli overlap.
5. At p=12889, the mixed canonical two-cycle 43<->61 has empty E/M selectors at every valid square source at BOTH vertices. Its 22 sources and 246 original divisors are completely checked. The actual path 43 -(t3)->3319 -(t1)->1013 -(t1)->11 reaches a middle hit u=9, but no universal path-to-hit theorem is asserted.

## What these statements do not establish

The six produced vertices may all have empty local selectors. The pointwise factor-supply theorem is not itself the missing target-coefficient theorem. The general closed-set lower bound on cardinality can coexist with a much larger failure set. No argument gives an upper bound contradicting every p.

The exact C-free source count is sufficient for factor escape, not necessary: p=3361,q=977 has A=11^2*13, so relative to {977,11} the source is not C-free but has the outside nonresidue factor 13.

The reflected root-pair error is a PREFIX statement. It is false in its stated strength on an arbitrary translated interval. Keep j=0 as the original starting point.

## Turn 5 target

Use the original integer E and M coefficients across the enlarged source system to eliminate a remaining terminal obstruction, or identify its exact persistence mechanism. The common-source span is `Div(gcd(A_t,A_t')^2)`, with centred exponent translation and an unchanged integer carry before taking the two square residues. The full target requires one and the same original bounded u.

A permitted positive route is a proved interaction between the E and M carries, actual source factor weights, and the cycle-cofactor equations. It must start at arbitrary p; do not assume that a chosen source already contains a useful prime-power packet. A permitted negative result is a certified larger square-source all-failure component; it falsifies only the stated source family unless every original first-half shell is also exhausted.

If no arithmetic coupling forces the target coefficient, return to the complete first-half signed coefficient sum, not a dyadic-only substitute. Do not claim a strict canonical defect-index descent: Turn 3 has a certified constant-C6 triangle.
