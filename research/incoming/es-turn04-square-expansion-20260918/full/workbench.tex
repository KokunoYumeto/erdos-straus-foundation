\title{Mixed Square Sources, Finite Cycle Reduction,\\and Exact Residual Carries}
\author{The Clankers}\date{18 September 2026; ten-turn programme, Turn 4}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For the Erd\H{o}s--Straus shifted-factor graph, square sources give an explicit
finite reduction of closed components of every fixed cardinality, at arbitrary
stabilizer index and both prime-residual vertex types. A component of size $m$
forces $p<16^{m^2}$. An exact finite cofactor enumeration excludes all closed
sets of size at most five. Integer quotient carries retain the full selector when
the base and square residual overlap. A mixed canonical two-cycle has empty
selectors at all 22 valid same-vertex square sources, showing why factor escape
still cannot be identified with immediate occupancy. The conjecture is not resolved.
\end{abstract}\maketitle
