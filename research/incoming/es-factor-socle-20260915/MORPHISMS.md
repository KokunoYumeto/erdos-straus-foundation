# Typed maps and retained information

1. Original factor atlas: fixed (p,u), RQ=p+4u, Q=-1 mod4K(u) maps to a=(p+R)/4, d=gcd(a,u), (h,r,s)=(d²/u,u/d,a/d), lambda=(r+s)/R and (a,phs lambda,phr lambda). The ordered inverse is u=pa²/(Ry-pa). Keep p, channel, order and both factorizations.

2. Half-turn comparison: at p=1+2^(k-1) mod2^k, coarse divisors d=-1 mod2^(k-1) lift to either -1 or -p. They return as Q or R respectively. Fibre over a state is exactly {R,Q}, with no fixed point. No further modulus descent is claimed.

3. Unit coordinates: q=(-1)^epsilon g^j for g=3 or5. Both epsilon and j are retained; w=2^v2(j), or sentinel L at j=0, alone loses the remaining phase. Arithmetic q and its actual exponent remain in the state.

4. Group-ring isomorphism: [(-1)^epsilon g^j] maps to (1+S)^epsilon(1+T)^j in F2[S,T]/(S²,T^L). Inverse S=1+[-1],T=1+[g]. Complete Pascal coordinate map, not a dimension-only identification.

5. Joint annihilator: scalar c maps to cST^(L-1); inverse extracts the top monomial coefficient. The group-basis image is proved to have all coefficients c.

6. Positive divisor lift: bits of b_q contained in bits of selected f_q<=e_q map to product q^b_q. Unique factorization and binary expansion invert it. Coefficients after residue collision keep all actual words. It is not the integer expansion of repeated factors (1+[q])^f_q.

7. Parity observation: integer counts and their original presence masks map to F2 amplitudes with the same masks. Additive kernel2Z[G]; inverse fibres parity+2Z at each coordinate. Zero parity does not imply absent target.

8. Pivot product: original f satisfying weighted degree and parity conditions maps to ST^(L-1). The expansion records every vanishing lower/outside term. This class of products is sufficient, not all possible ES hits.

9. Capacity-to-coin map: internal and lower outside occurrences are individual coins. Higher outside primes pair only their own occurrences, counted by sum floor(e_q/2). The odd pivot aggregate has its bounded-composition inverse. Original prime labels are not collapsed to a single count without their fibres.

10. Prefix inversion: descending greedy allocation returns the exact weight L-1; bounded compositions return every possible actual f within the selected aggregate. The prefix equivalence applies to this certificate class only.

11. Residue extraction: dynamic programming stores actual binary-block predecessors. A target word d is an original divisor, not an arbitrary representative of the same residue. Other residue-collision words remain in the integer polynomial.

12. Disjoint packing: exponent=(f_q+1)t_q+b_q with b_q a submask of f_q. Quotient and remainder invert it; disjoint boxes give distinct coarse divisors. Only the proved half-turn factor fibre may divide the state lower count by two.

13. Original arithmetic return: the short word may be residual or cofactor. Recover the other factor from N and the returned a factorization separately. No sorting or channel change is implicit. The <=L occurrence bound is for one marked factor, not necessarily R.

All modules and coefficient observations retain original labels. Supported zero is not external absence. No inversion of a parity quotient, unbounded supply of prime occurrences, or independent choice of local divisors is used.
