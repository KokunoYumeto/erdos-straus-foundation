# Typed morphisms and their retained data

All p,R,u,h,r,s,channel,original exponent and orientation marks travel through their declared maps. Local support labels are not dropped when amplitudes become zero.

## S1: Canonical source

Domain: prime q in V_p. Codomain: (p,q,sigma_q,R_q,A_q,complete factorization).

Inverse/fibres: sigma is determined by q mod4; R=sigma q and A=(p+R)/4. q=R/sigma with the type retained.

Information loss/exceptions: No prime vertex or auxiliary range is discarded.

Proof: (1)-(2).

## S2: Centered divisor bijection

Domain: product_t[-e_t,e_t]. Codomain: Div(A_q^2).

Inverse/fibres: u=product t^(e_t+beta_t); beta_t=v_t(u)-e_t.

Information loss/exceptions: Same exponent vector at every local modulus; no independent CRT choices.

Proof: (4).

## S3: Fine coefficient observation

Domain: free integer module on original beta labels. Codomain: integer residue coefficient array mu.

Inverse/fibres: Fibre sums; kernel generated by nonreference differences in each actual residue fibre. Recover with all differences retained.

Information loss/exceptions: Residue values alone lose multiplicities and witnesses; supported zero is not absence.

Proof: (4)-(5).

## S4: Ordered E/M return

Domain: passing original divisor/channel state. Codomain: positive ordered rational equation solution in integers.

Inverse/fibres: Formulas (6)-(7); the channel, p and distinguished denominator retained.

Information loss/exceptions: E reflected test first negates beta. The channels are not identified.

Proof: (6)-(7).

## S5: Auxiliary first-half recoding

Domain: E state with A>p/2. Codomain: E state with first denominator y<p/2.

Inverse/fibres: (h,r,s,kappa)->(h,kappa,s,r), permutation (y,A,z), inverse the same switch.

Information loss/exceptions: No M state exists in the larger range. Not deletion of that source.

Proof: section2.

## O1: Actual period quotient

Domain: fine support W subset Gamma. Codomain: S=W/K in Q=Gamma/K.

Inverse/fibres: K=Stab_G(W); W is complete inverse image of S in Gamma.

Information loss/exceptions: Boolean membership descends; coefficient values need not be uniform. Ambient and effective indices remain separate.

Proof: (8)-(11).

## O2: Local extension coordinates

Domain: Gamma at one vertex. Codomain: pairs (coset,kernel element).

Inverse/fibres: g -> (pi(g),g/s(pi(g))); multiply using omega=s(c)s(d)/s(cd).

Information loss/exceptions: No comparison of different vertex K groups without a further map.

Proof: section3.

## E1: Complete factor-edge incidence

Domain: original factorization of A_q. Codomain: all r with (r/p)=-1, weighted by v_r(A_q).

Inverse/fibres: Given q,r recover m=v_r(A_q),B=A_q/r^m,c=A_q/r.

Information loss/exceptions: All even positive edge weights retained, including at p3361,q977.

Proof: (3),(13).

## E2: Successor arithmetic source

Domain: marked edge p+sigma_q q=4cr. Codomain: A_r=((4c+sigma_r)r-sigma_q q)/4.

Inverse/fibres: Keep p and edge; successor is exact integer; its factorization is computed separately.

Information loss/exceptions: No transfer of source prime exponents to the new factorization is asserted.

Proof: (14).

## E3: Character observation

Domain: every source factor t|A_q. Codomain: Jacobi(t/R_q)=Legendre(t/p).

Inverse/fibres: Identity proved by reciprocity, and supplementary law at t=2.

Information loss/exceptions: A sign does not recover the factor or the full residue-group class.

Proof: (15)-(16).

## E4: Composite successor target CRT

Domain: -p in units modulo 3r. Codomain: (2,sigma_q q mod r).

Inverse/fibres: Inverse ar*(r^-1 mod3)+3b*(3^-1 modr) modulo3r.

Information loss/exceptions: 3q is a nonunit. The correction -4cr and the same original word are indispensable.

Proof: (17)-(18).

## E5: Common unchanged-divisor span

Domain: Div(A_q^2) and Div(A_r^2). Codomain: Div(gcd(A_q,A_r)^2).

Inverse/fibres: Literal inclusions, inverse reads the identical integer u on their intersection.

Information loss/exceptions: Outside the intersection no unchanged-divisor map exists; other proposed maps are not ruled out.

Proof: (19).

## E6: Centered vector and gate transition

Domain: common u. Codomain: new beta and gate values.

Inverse/fibres: beta_r=beta_q+v(A_q)-v(A_r); E gate unchanged, M gate plus Delta.

Information loss/exceptions: Modulus changes; a source hit can become a miss even on this domain.

Proof: (20).

## G1: All-hit reachability

Domain: original complete directed graph with gate labels. Codomain: distance to nearest hit or infinity.

Inverse/fibres: A chosen distance-decreasing original edge reconstructs a terminating path; its terminal word returns ES.

Information loss/exceptions: No path exists from the greatest closed failure set.

Proof: (21)-(22).

## G2: Closed-failure reduction

Domain: same complete graph. Codomain: greatest fixed point U_infinity.

Inverse/fibres: Successor-closed failed-set certificate; finite elimination <=|V|.

Information loss/exceptions: A failed cycle with an exit is not a closed-set certificate.

Proof: Theorem closure.

## G3: Strong-component quotient

Domain: full graph. Codomain: condensation graph with member lists and original edges.

Inverse/fibres: Membership plus original edges reconstructs graph; SCC labels alone do not.

Information loss/exceptions: Bottom component means no outgoing edge outside it.

Proof: section5.

## C1: Cycle coefficient recurrence

Domain: ordered simple cycle with (sigma_i,c_i). Codomain: D,S,rotated positive numerators T_i.

Inverse/fibres: Recurrence (23); retain cyclic order and starting point.

Information loss/exceptions: Chosen cycle may omit other exits; closure is a separate factor test.

Proof: (23)-(24).

## C2: Integer cycle inverse

Domain: ordered coefficient word satisfying all converse checks. Codomain: prime p and distinct prime vertices q_i.

Inverse/fibres: h=gcd T_i, p=(D-S)/h, q_i=T_i/h.

Information loss/exceptions: Primality, signs, ranges, actual edge factors, no-hit predicates and closure must all be checked.

Proof: (25).

## C3: Forget cycle origin

Domain: marked length-l simple cycle. Codomain: unrooted cyclic ordering.

Inverse/fibres: Exactly l rotations, retained as inverse fibre.

Information loss/exceptions: Reverse order is not presumed to be a cycle.

Proof: section6.

## P1: Additional neutral multiplier source

Domain: (p,q,s): Legendre(s/p)=1, sq=3mod4, sq<3p. Codomain: new A=(p+sq)/4 and full box.

Inverse/fibres: Keep q and s; inverse sigma mark explicit; not identified with canonical source.

Information loss/exceptions: Each extra source must actually be evaluated; no universal success theorem imported.

Proof: (32)-(33).

## P2: Same-prime multiplier comparison

Domain: two admissible ports s,t at q. Codomain: A_t-A_s=q(t-s)/4 and gcd identity.

Inverse/fibres: gcd(A_s,A_t)=gcd(A_s,(t-s)/4).

Information loss/exceptions: For difference4 the source factors are coprime; a new success is not transport of an old divisor.

Proof: (32).
