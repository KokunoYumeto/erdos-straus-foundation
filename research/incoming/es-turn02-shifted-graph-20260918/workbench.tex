\begin{abstract}
We attach the complete marked exterior/middle divisor source to every vertex of
the universally generated external-nonresidue factor graph, retaining both prime
and composite shifts and every actual factor multiplicity. We give exact
transition formulas, common-source domains, finite closure certificates and an
integer inverse for simple cycles. A closed seven-cycle at the hard prime 2521
has no occupied local selector; thus the proposed universal local-cycle rescue
is false, although ES holds there. A sextic defect also feeds a closed component.
All proofs, finite scopes and typed inverses are explicit. No universal ES result,
historical-priority claim or new Lean certificate is asserted.
\end{abstract}
