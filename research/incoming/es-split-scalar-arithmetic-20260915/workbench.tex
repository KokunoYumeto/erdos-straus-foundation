\begin{abstract}
We return to the distinction between absent zero and supported zero rather
than substituting a new meaning of mixed support. A sparse split group-algebra
inverse has a different identity from its ordinary amplitude inverse; the
precise completion and its lost mask are computed. Actual positive divisor
factors instead admit finite, weight-preserving formulas for all exponent
multiplicities. At residual 27 a complete finite classification gives 174
minimal capacity profiles and an optimal three-residue-class witness reduction.
A separate hand proof classifies all multiplicities in a two-residue family.
The same original coefficients have an absolutely convergent Dirichlet carrier
and explicit $L$-function component formulas. No global ES, RH, historical
priority or formal-proof certification is claimed.
\end{abstract}
