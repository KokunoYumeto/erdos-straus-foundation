# Explicit maps, domains, fibres and information loss

1. Original square-divisor box: f_l in [0,2v_l(a)] -> u=product l^f_l.
   Inverse f_l=v_l(u). The centred beta is f_l-v_l(a).
2. Original u and E/M tag -> rational channel-labelled triples in equations (1)-(2).
   The full original reciprocal identity holds before integrality. First-trace
   integrality is the squared gate, not the full gate.
3. Rational trace tails -> b=Ry-pa,c=Rz-pa, bc=(pa)^2. The p-valuation pair
   recovers E canonical orientation or either M orientation and original u.
4. Trace source -> d=R/gcd(R,G). The exact common tail denominator is d and
   d^2|R. A supported rational trace is not an original integer hit when d>1.
5. Original budget u|a^2 <-> K(u)|a <-> p+R=0 mod m=4K(u).
6. Complete divisor-removal domain: k|R, d|k, k=1 mod m.
   Return R'=R/k,a'=(p+R')/4,u'=u. A proper input strictly decreases a.
7. Target coordinate reconstruction: gcd(a',u) gives h',r',s' and the original
   quotient kappa' or lambda'. Both labelled reciprocal identities and inverses
   are checked. The label order is not a numerical size order.
   Old and new factor inventories differ; their beta vectors change by
   v_l(a)-v_l(a'), including all zero coordinates.
8. Fixed-source inverse and complete global fibres: with the old R retained,
   k=R/R'. After R is forgotten this is a relation, not a function. The domain
   is the decorated-arrow set A={(s,k):k in K_s}; one source may carry several
   arrows. Above a full target satisfying R'=4a'-p, 3<=R'<p, R'=3 mod 4,
   u|(a')^2 and R'|G, enumerate the positive k <= (p-1)/R' satisfying
   k=1 mod m, R'k|G^2, R'k not dividing G. The pushforward Z^(A)->Z^(T_hit)
   sends each arrow to its target. A chosen arrow in every fibre gives a right
   inverse; same-target arrow differences give the kernel and the exact
   direct-sum reconstruction.
9. Coefficient pushforward from divisors of R/d into (Z/m)^*: geometric prime
   factors, not binomial coloured occurrences. Coefficient at d^{-1} counts
   the entire removal set. The group-ring image does not retain divisor labels;
   the labelled certificate and source basis do. The kernel is the original
   same-target differences, and a chosen representative gives a section on the
   image rather than an inverse after labels are discarded.
10. Square subdomain R=delta*t^2, q0=K(d), q0|q|t, q^2=1 mod m. Its inverse
    reads q=sqrt(k). The squarefree part delta is not the radical and can share
    prime factors with t.
11. Square-image coefficient polynomial: divisors v of t/q0 -> v^2 mod m.
    Coefficient at q0^{-2} counts square deletions. Original exponent vectors
    and any repeated residue multiplicities remain attached.
12. Shortest completion: an identity subword can be deleted from the actual
    remaining divisor. It bounds a word already known to exist, and is not
    a target-occupancy theorem.
13. Complement u -> a^2/u: always a relaxed M map and swaps the tails. The
    identity 4u G_M(u*)-p G_M(u)=R(4a+p) preserves its exact gcd with R, so a
    proper M complement stays proper. At E its exact relaxed-gate domain is
    K(R)|(p^2-1), but the complement may already be integral. A complement
    followed by factor k decreases a exactly when k>1; strictness is not
    asserted for the E example (37,16,27,2,E), whose complement has d*=1 and
    only k=1. The flag and old a invert the word change. At u=a do not invent
    a second original word.
14. Uniform square-image collapse happens exactly at m|24, equivalently u|36.
    The integer coefficient there is tau(t/q0), not its reduction modulo two.
15. Counterfamily CRT returns p congruences, not an arbitrary chosen prime.
    Dirichlet is used only after proving reducedness. Keep u,m,b,delta,t,e,L,
    residue, modulus and a lower threshold. Intermediate numerical residues in
    the finite construction table are not asserted prime.
16. Counterfamily M complement budget: K(a^2/u)=a/B(u). Its modulus exceeds R;
    the original word box is what obstructs every residual-divisor return.
17. Cofactor-delta box is Div(((delta+1)/4)^2), NOT the original residual-delta
    shell Div(((p+delta)/4)^2). Empty cofactor capacity does not identify them.
18. Independent endpoint returns recover valid E/M states at each counterfamily
    prime. The counterfamily is not a family of ES counterexamples.
