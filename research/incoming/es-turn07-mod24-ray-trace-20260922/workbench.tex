{An Exact Modulo-24 Trace Return, Primitive-Ray Boundary,\\
and Literal Receiver Composition}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{22 September 2026}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For a prescribed prime $p\equiv1\pmod{24}$, every positive rational
Erd\H{o}s--Straus representation whose first denominator is $cq$, with
$c\mid6$ and $q>3$ prime in the first-half range, and whose two tails have
integral sum, yields an explicitly reconstructed integral Type-II state.
The proof combines a primitive-ray-preserving trace return with the original
small-word middle return; their different exponent transport is retained.
All trace fibres of the nine automatic rays have explicit integer inverses.
For every other primitive ray, a reduced prime progression supplies proper
trace sources with no residual-divisor return preserving that ray, even when
fixed-word deletion and the exterior complement are also tested.
These are source-changing theorems and exact obstructions, not universal
positivity of the initial source. Code and original-coordinate certificates
accompany the complete proofs.  Composing the return with the literal
four-label receiver yields an invertible frame with explicit determinant,
inverse, and exterior-power bounds on every returned witness.
\end{abstract}
