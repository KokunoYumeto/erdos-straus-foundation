\begin{abstract}
The original ES return operator has reciprocal odd-integer spectrum, not an
arbitrary interval spectrum. Explicit interpolation filters give one-sided
counting bounds with factorial error and coefficient norm growing only
linearly. They yield uniform, unscaled count certificates of moment degree
$O(\log p/\log\log p)$, with all source and split-fibre marks retained.
An exact hard-prime shell shows that occupied shells need not be detected by
the previous aggregate first-degree lower bound. A source-resolved estimate
and a fixed quartic filter both recover its positive integral witness; a second
hard-prime example also defeats separate-channel degree one. An explicit
all-integer arithmetic progression follows from the recovered selector.
No universal ES theorem or historical-priority conclusion is asserted.
\end{abstract}
