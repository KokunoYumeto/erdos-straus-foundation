\begin{abstract}
Starting from the corrected integrated receiver composite, we classify its
entire bare sorted mixed-trace fibre, including middle inputs, both orders,
original carrier tests and cross-channel collisions. The full fixed-prime
receiver matrix reconstructs the returned target and its four selected
square-root branches, so the arithmetic fibres remain exactly the source
fibres calculated here. Separately, an exact
fixed-residual middle family has singular powers $9,4,3/2,-3/2$, all first
relative coefficients, and observation powers $0,2,3,9$. Two explicit reduced
prime progressions in $1\bmod840$ show that the existing uniform inverse
exponents $p^{3/2},1,p^{-2}$ are all sharp already on middle witnesses.
Complete coordinate proofs and standard-library checks are supplied.
No universal witness-existence assertion, nor optimality on the narrower
mixed-return image, is assumed.
\end{abstract}
