# Integration audit: mixed fibres and middle spectra

Date: 22 September 2026.

## Admission decision

The supplied continuation is admitted after correction. Independent algebraic
derivations found no error in the complete E/M inverse fibre, the fixed-
$(R,u)$ singular expansion, the observation hierarchy, or the two hard-middle
prime families. The module does not prove the Erdős–Straus conjecture: every
receiver theorem begins with an existing integral witness, and the mixed return
begins with an existing rational trace source.

## Corrections made during integration

1. The predecessor source tuple contained both an already ordered pair $(y,z)$
   and an unconstrained bit $\omega$. That doubled the literal typed domain.
   The redundant bit was removed; the order of $(y,z)$ retains the orientation.
2. The E inverse now proves
   \[
   r(4u_t+1)=\delta_E(s+\lambda t^2),\qquad
   \gcd(r,\delta_Et^2)=1,
   \]
   before reducing the square gate. It also proves
   $\gcd(t,4r\lambda)=1$, so the unit-square congruence is justified.
3. The M inverse now displays
   \[
   A_{hr^2}=4hr\lambda-1,
   \qquad A_{hs^2}=4hs\lambda-1,
   \]
   and gives the unit-multiple congruence proving that complementary words have
   equivalent square gates.
4. The free-abelian arithmetic kernel and the sign-decorated kernel are
   separated. Their ranks are respectively
   $\sum_W(n_W-1)$ and $16\sum_W(n_W-1)$.
5. The quotient metric now fixes a square-root frame and types the map
   $L_{W,\xi}$. The minimum-energy vector-space section is not described as an
   integral arithmetic section.
6. The receiver inverse is stated only on its marked image, with every
   denominator and consistency equation explicit. It recovers $(W,\xi)$, not a
   unique member of $\rho^{-1}(W)$.
7. The middle family now requires $R>0$. The expression
   $G^{1/2}O^{-1}Q^{-1/2}$ is identified as the inverse of the orthonormal
   representative $Q^{1/2}OG^{-1/2}$.
8. JSON and log writers emit explicit UTF-8 bytes with line feed endings, so
   normal and optimized replays are byte reproducible on Windows as well as
   Linux.
