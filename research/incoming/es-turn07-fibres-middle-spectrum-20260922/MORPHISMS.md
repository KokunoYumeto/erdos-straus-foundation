# Typed maps, inverse fibres and losses

1. Original square divisor (a,u) -> (h,r,s) by g=gcd(a,u), h=g²/u, r=u/g,
   s=a/g. Inverse a=hrs,u=hr². Complete prime-exponent box remains recorded.
2. Positive rational integral-trace tails -> the original factor pair
   (Ry-pa,Rz-pa), product (pa)². Its p-colours recover E plus swap, or M word.
3. Corrected E mixed return -> (H,s,J,r), ordered denominators
   (HsJ,pHJr,pHsr). The inequality J>s determines sorting. Old R=delta*t²;
   delta is returned Q, not generally returned R.
4. Corrected M mixed return -> small u0 at a'=(p+delta)/4. Complement uses
   the target a'², not the old a². Sorting forgets the complement orientation.
5. Bare sorted M target -> unique primitive r<s,h,lambda by gcd(Y/p,Z/p).
6. Full E-origin inverse: deltaE=4hr*lambda-1 squarefree, r*lambda|6,
   t|oddpart(s), deltaE*t²<p, exact carrier test on old a. Source ray(lambda,r).
7. Full M-origin inverse: R squarefree, nu in {hr²,hs²} intersect Div(36),
   t|(p+4nu)/R, R*t²<p, old carrier and nu|c². Both original words are retained.
8. Bare sorting fibres: disjoint union of 6 and 7, each arithmetic t/seed has
   exactly two original ordered inputs. E/M origins can collide at one target.
9. Undecorated coefficient pushforward: sums n_W original values. Its kernel
   consists of same-fibre differences and has rank sum_W(n_W-1). With all 16
   receiver frames, the decorated rank is 16 sum_W(n_W-1). An arithmetic
   section must select an actually present source, not a possibly unavailable
   t=1 record.
10. Witness plus 16 square-root frames -> literal receiver O. Given p, its
    first three rows recover xi,d,A,ell exactly. Fourth row is retained
    consistency. Without p, no such injectivity assertion is made. The matrix
    does not choose one arithmetic preimage; singular values alone lose more data.
11. Original receiver O = compensated diagonal factors times analytic B.
    Its inverse restores *both* diagonal factors. B is not declared isometric to O.
12. Middle fixed-R family -> B0,B1 -> complete compound maps -> all four
    singular coefficients. Both rank-three leading minors remain, giving sqrt2.
13. Original inverse -> rank-one limit. This retains only its leading operator;
    finite-p other coordinates remain in B(z)^-1 and its explicit expansion.
14. Tiny Y/Z components -> observation powers 3/9. They are proved relatively,
    not inferred from a coarser vector remainder. Fixed nonzero row tests are exhaustive.
15. Two reduced hard-prime progressions -> actual M states. No mixed-carrier
    preimage is assumed. Their narrower input fibre is separately calculated by 6--7.
16. Fixed metrics and conductor: for the orthonormal representative
    O-hat=Q^1/2 O G^-1/2, its inverse is G^1/2 O^-1 Q^-1/2. After conductor,
    keep the literal inverse and metric pullback. No phase, boundary or
    resonance is deleted.
