# Attempt record

The prior tranche showed that a finite local-place predicate can miss one foreign
prime occurrence. The first target here was a controlled same-prime shell repair.
The exact square-removal map suggested by an integral first tail trace was tested:
its original integer defect is d^2=R/gcd(R,y+z). At p=1009 it takes R135,a286 to
R15,a256, where both complete original gates are empty. That specific map was
rejected, with its full arithmetic counterexample retained.

The surviving route tests global integrality before applying the Fabel inverse.
Newton's identities yield a common-denominator theorem, not merely a necessary
local signature. Its numerator-four corollary proves that two integer root traces
recover all rational ES denominators. On the raw integer sum-gate source one trace
already returns the exact original divisor budget, including the E reclassification
when the raw word contains precisely one p. The coefficient kernel is calculated,
not assumed nonzero.

A further attempted relaxation was to replace rational roots by algebraic-integer
roots satisfying all integer traces and the original p-local signature. The explicit
quadratic construction works at every hard prime and shows why that relaxation is
insufficient. It retains both quadratic branches and the nonrational field.

The remaining obligation is still to produce an integral trace from an original
rational raw source at every prescribed hard p, or to prove a positive global pair
coefficient. This note does not assert that obligation.
