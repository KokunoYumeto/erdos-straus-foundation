\begin{abstract}
For $n\ge3$ rational nonzero denominators with reciprocal sum $4/p$, the first
$n-1$ integral power sums force all denominators to be integers. We prove the
more general common-denominator bound $d^n\mid(n-1)!m$ for reciprocal numerator
$m$, and classify the exact possible denominators at $n=3$. On a complete
unfiltered integer ES gate source, a single literal trace recovers the full
original divisor budget and the channel. Its two-point fibres are exactly the
original middle orientations. An integral first trace alone on arbitrary
rational tails leaves a precisely computed square-part obstruction. Finally,
for every hard prime we construct positive irrational algebraic-integer targets
with all integer traces, the original middle prime-local signature, and the
inherited scalar height bounds. Thus the rationality domain and the global
integrality observation are both indispensable. No universal ES occupancy
statement is asserted.
\end{abstract}
