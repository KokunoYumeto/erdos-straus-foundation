# Strategic sweep — scope and decision

This is a quick sweep of the recent visible research continuations, with the current two-block proof and the archived local Kneser proof reread. It is not a fresh audit of all earlier archives, references or reported Lean/CI status.

Push actual bounded prime-factor products and same-p cofactor maps: they can produce the original divisor before the successful states are known. Use the SplitZero coefficient distinction here: an actual product reaches the nonzero socle, every residue coefficient is odd, and its augmentation is a receiving supported zero rather than absence.

Retain fixed 3,11 cycle/lattice estimates as complementary. They succeed on some inputs where the new criterion fails. Retain moment/filter and completed cohomology machinery as a certificate/control layer, not as proof that a prescribed target is occupied. Preserve every existing geometric map, but do not prioritize another Leech/S6 inverse as a proof of integral occupancy without a new arithmetic selection identity. Smaller cyclotomic cofactors and fixed-list logarithmic escape have their proved scopes; neither automatically supplies a prime or bounded word in the required class.

Recover the archived Star–Kneser theorem explicitly, rather than rediscovering it. The current factor-occurrence special case has a direct proof and enumerates only 2k-3 possible target-avoiding subgroups. A surviving candidate subgroup is not asserted to be the actual stabilizer.

The important baseline repair is evaluative, not a correction of the old theorem: two-prime words in both roles already outperform the previous capacity test in the inherited finite range. The new test adds three branches and one prime to their union. It is not presented as a 129-prime advance over every available simple method.

The remaining arithmetic arrow is fixed-p simultaneous avoidance: every empty seed must fail a specific prefix capacity in both applicable charts and obey actual stabilizer concentration. The exact correlations between different p+4u values are not yet proved to make those simultaneous restrictions impossible.
