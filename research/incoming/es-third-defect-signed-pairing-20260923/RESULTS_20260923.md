# Structural results established on 23 September 2026

## SZ-20260923-017 — Exact weighted signed-pair fibres

The complete divisor opposition pairing at every original shell is exactly the
sum over original E and M words in Theorem 2.1. Every fibre weight is positive,
so the pairing is strictly positive exactly when the shell contains a word.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 2.1.

## SZ-20260923-018 — Character sign and degree covariance

The signed pairing retains `chi(-1)` and is the difference of the two
inversion energies. The global degree pullback satisfies `A_q^*JA_q=qJ`, so
degree amplification preserves a signed zero.

Proof: `core.tex`, Proposition 2.2.

## SZ-20260923-019 — Cross-shell boundary obstruction

Every actual cross-shell opposed divisor pair yields the two determinant
identities and the four-term reciprocal formula in Theorem 3.1. The extra term
fuses exactly on `nu|a`. Outside that locus, its cyclic obstruction has order
`rho=nu/gcd(a,nu)`, the retained numerator of the failed fusion.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 3.1 and Definition 3.2.

## SZ-20260923-020 — Exact odd-quotient complement decomposition

For `gcd(R,p-1)=m` and `R|m^2`, complementation reverses the exact quotient
colour. If `U_N` is the complement-stable uncovered word set, then
`|T_N|=|M|+2|E|+|U_N|`; the uncovered complement orbits are exactly
`U_N/<iota>`.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 4.1.

## SZ-20260923-021 — Unique and sharp three-colour closure

`N=3` is the unique nontrivial odd quotient whose every colour orbit meets a
gate. For every odd `N>3`, an explicit reduced prime progression realizes an
uncovered complement orbit.

Proof: `core.tex`, Corollary 4.2 and Proposition 4.3.

## SZ-20260923-022 — Same-shell third-defect return

Under `gcd(R,p-1)=R/3`, every trace in `T_3` returns at the same shell to an
original E or M word. Exactly `2|E|+|M|=|T_3|`, and the number of increasing
triples is `|T_3|/2`.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 5.1.

## SZ-20260923-023 — Complete applicable-residual family

Writing `p-1=2^k 3^eta b`, all applicable residuals are exactly
`R=3^(eta+1)d` with `d|b` and `d=(-1)^eta mod 4`.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 6.1.

## SZ-20260923-024 — Complete residual-27 empty locus

For the two applicable prime classes modulo 216, the original residual-27 shell
is empty exactly in the two factorization types stated in Theorem 7.1.

Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem 7.1.
