\begin{abstract}
For every original Erd\H{o}s--Straus shell we place the complete divisor source in
its signed opposition pairing before selecting an integer witness.  Its exact
fibres are the original exterior and middle words with strictly positive,
explicit divisor weights, so strict positivity is equivalent to shell
occupancy.  The character sign and the even--odd energy difference show why an
ordinary receiver norm cannot replace this pairing.  An exact cross-shell map
produces a four-term reciprocal identity; its remaining term is classified by
a finite cyclic obstruction whose order is the retained numerator of the failed
unit-fraction fusion.  We then prove a general complement law on an odd residual
quotient.  It decomposes the trace source into middle, exterior, and uncovered
orbits and maps exactly to the earlier affine channel coordinate.  Quotient
three is the unique nontrivial odd quotient on which complementation alone
forces every colour orbit into an integer gate.  This gives a same-shell return
theorem, an exact solution count, the complete applicable-residual family, and
a factor-level classification of the empty residual-27 locus.  The missing
global step is now explicit: for each prescribed hard prime one must force a
nonempty three-colour trace source or eliminate a cross-shell obstruction.
\end{abstract}
