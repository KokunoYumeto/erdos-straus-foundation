# Exact objects, maps, inverses, fibres, and metrics

1. **Integral witness to ordered labels.** Domain: positive integral triples
   at prime \(p\equiv1\pmod {12}\). The unique smallest first-half denominator
   is \(a\); retain the tail exchange needed for \(a<Y<Z\). Codomain:
   \(\ell=(p,a,Y,Z)\). Forgetting the exchange loses the original tail mark.

2. **Labelled quartic.** The ordered roots and \(S=p+a+Y+Z\) give
   \(H(T)=-S^{-1}\prod_j(T-\ell_j)\). Coefficients without labels forget the
   root order; no inverse to that quotient is asserted.

3. **Signed root cover.** Over the distinct-root locus, each label has two
   choices \(\xi_j^2H'(\ell_j)=1\). The fibre has sixteen points. A branch
   change is right multiplication by a diagonal sign isometry.

4. **Literal receiver.** \(O:\mathbb C^4_{\rm labels}\to\mathbb C^4_q\)
   is the displayed column map. Its domain and codomain carry the stated
   standard Hermitian metrics until the explicit metric transport.

5. **Determinant scalar.** The oriented Vandermonde and square-root product
   give \(\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}\), with
   \(\det O=-4\epsilon\mathfrak N/S^3\). Permuting labels and changing branches
   alter \(\epsilon\) and the determinant compatibly; the modulus is preserved.

6. **Exact inverse.** ES3--ES4 give every entry of \(O^{-1}\) on
   \(\mathfrak h\ne0\). The derivation interpolates a cubic \(L\) and retains
   all powers of \(S,d_j\), and \(\xi_j\).

7. **Defect space.** At \(\mathfrak h=0\), the last three receiver rows have
   rank three. The kernel generator and image hyperplane are written in ES5.
   This defines the precise rank-three object replacing the inverse.

8. **Exterior chart.** The map from \((p,w,t,u)\) to
   \((p,a,Y,Z)\) is ES6. Integrality is an additional arithmetic property;
   the real chart is not substituted for the integral domain.

9. **Uniform singular spectrum.** ES7--ES13 calculate all four singular
   scales through the complete exterior maps. The products
   \(s_1\cdots s_j=\|\wedge^jO\|_2\) recover each individual value without
   dropping total volume.

10. **Rank-one inverse and heat.** ES14 maps the unique soft target direction
    to \(v_0=2^{-1/2}(-i,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}\). ES15 exponentiates the positive
    operator \(O^*O\); the three faster eigendirections vanish after the exact
    rescaling.

11. **Fixed-\(c\) integral family.** ES16 maps each admitted pair \((c,p)\) to
    the four literal integral roots. Its inverse recovers \(c\) from the
    displayed formulas. The reduced \(c=29\) progression is the only use of
    Dirichlet's theorem.

12. **Analytic weighted matrix.** ES18 defines \(B(z)\) with \(z=1/p\) by
    exact left and right diagonal weights. ES19--ES22 invert its full first
    jet; no entry or fractional exponent is removed.

13. **Observation flag.** The successive annihilation tests
    \(\ell v_0\), \(\ell w_0\), \(\ell e_Y\), \(\ell e_Z\) partition every
    nonzero scalar row and yield powers \(0,2,3,15\). The tests are ordered;
    later tiers apply only after earlier numerators vanish.

14. **Metric transport.** For positive \(G,Q\), the orthonormal receiver is
    \(\widehat O=Q^{1/2}OG^{-1/2}\) and its inverse is
    \(G^{1/2}O^{-1}Q^{-1/2}\). ES33--ES36 retain the exact metric factors and
    the possible \(p^{-3/2}\) correction.

15. **Conductor composition.** For a fixed admitted invertible conductor
    \(L\), \((LO)^{-1}=O^{-1}L^{-1}\). ES37 retains the directional
    denominator \(\|e_0^*L^{-1}\|\). Approaching its zero locus removes any
    uniform relative bound; this is an explicit obstruction, not a reason to
    identify or discard the conductor.

16. **Mod-\(24\) source composition.** The adjacent trace-return module maps
    its marked rational source to an integral middle witness before applying
    \(O\). Its square factor \(t\) lies in the return fibre and is not a
    receiver coordinate. The exact composite and its kernel are proved in
    *../es-turn07-mod24-ray-trace-20260922/receiver_bridge.tex*.
