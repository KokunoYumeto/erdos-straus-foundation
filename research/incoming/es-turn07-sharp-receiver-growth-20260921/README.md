# Sharp inverse growth and finite heat limits for the ES receiver

This module proves quantitative and asymptotic theorems for the literal
four-label receiving map attached to an existing positive integral
Erdős–Straus witness at a prime \(p\equiv1\pmod {12}\). It retains the
ordered labels \((p,a,Y,Z)\), every square-root branch, the Euclidean source
and target metrics, and the exact conductor factors. It does not assert that
every prime has a witness.

The complete readable paper is *preprint.tex*. Its first part proves uniform
bounds for every integral witness; the two continuation sections prove the
exact inverse, the rank-three defect, uniform tail asymptotics, first
corrections, heat limits, and fixed-metric/conductor transport.

## Uniform theorem

For

~~~text
4/p = 1/a + 1/Y + 1/Z,
p prime, p = 1 mod 12,
p/4 < a < p/2,
a < Y < Z,
R = 4a-p, w = p/R,
~~~

let \(O:\mathbb C^4_{\rm labels}\to\mathbb C^4_q\) have columns

~~~text
o_j = xi_j (1, -i d_j, A d_j^2 + 2 ell_j d_j,
            i(7 ell_j^2 d_j - 13 d_j^2))^T,
~~~

where \(H(T)=-S^{-1}\prod_j(T-\ell_j)\), \(d_j=H'(\ell_j)\), and
\(\xi_j^2d_j=1\). With \(\gamma=80/6279^4\), the paper proves

~~~text
||O^(-1)||_2 < (360/gamma) p sqrt(w)
||wedge^2 O^(-1)||_2 < 180/gamma
||wedge^3 O^(-1)||_2 < 41/(4 gamma p^2)
|det O^(-1)| < 1/(4 gamma p^2 S^3).
~~~

The middle-channel proof gives the stronger first constant \(224/\gamma\).
The third singular value is bounded below by
\(\sqrt{\gamma/180}\), so at most one inverse singular direction can become
unbounded. Two infinite prime families in \(13\bmod24\) attain the three
displayed growth exponents.

## Exact defect and asymptotics

For four distinct positive labelled roots, equations ES1--ES5 give the full
rational inverse. Its only exceptional divisor is

\[
\mathfrak h=\sum_j P_2(\ell_j)/d_j^2.
\]

At \(\mathfrak h=0\), the receiver has rank exactly three. The kernel is
generated by \((\xi_j^{-1}P_2(\ell_j)/d_j^2)_j\), and the image is the
explicitly displayed cokernel hyperplane. The defect is therefore a defined
rank-three space, not an omitted inverse formula.

In the full large-\(w\) exterior chart the four singular values are evaluated
uniformly, including the rank-one inverse and heat limits. For the exact
fixed-\(c\) family

~~~text
c >= 2, c = 2 mod 3,
p = 1 mod 12, c | (p+3)/4,
a=(p+3)/4,
T=pa,
Y=(T+c)/3,
Z=T(T+c)/(3c),
~~~

the first relative singular corrections are

~~~text
18, -3/2, -41/4, 11/4,
~~~

the determinant correction is \(9/p\), and

\[
p^3s_4^2=32+\frac{176}{p}+O_c(p^{-2}).
\]

The slow singular vector has exact tail corrections \(21/(4p)\) and
\(-105/(4p)\). Scalar heat observations form an exhaustive hierarchy with
powers \(0,2,3,15\). The same hierarchy is transported through arbitrary
fixed positive metrics \(G,Q\) and through a fixed invertible conductor with
its full directional denominator retained.

## Reproduction

Run the certificate lanes from this directory:

~~~text
python verify.py
python derivation/check_es.py
~~~

*verify.py* reconstructs the global coefficient arrays, exact constants, two
sharp families, and high-precision singular values. The second checker derives
fifty exact identities and performs fifty-six high-precision tests of the
first corrections, observation hierarchy, and metric transport. Numerical
evaluations test formulas; they do not replace the analytic proofs in the TeX.

## Exact scope

- The theorem begins with an existing integral ES witness.
- The mod-\(24\) trace-return theorem in the adjacent module supplies such a
  witness from its own rational-trace domain; it does not prove that domain is
  occupied at every prime.
- The sharp families lie in \(13\bmod24\), so optimality is not claimed on the
  restricted image of the mod-\(24\) return.
- No Riemann-hypothesis conclusion, universal ES theorem, historical-priority
  determination, Lean build, or independent human review is claimed.
