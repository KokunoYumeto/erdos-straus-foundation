\title[Sharp inverse growth for the Erd\H{o}s--Straus receiver]
{Sharp inverse growth, singular defect, and finite heat limits\\
for the literal Erd\H{o}s--Straus receiver}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{22 September 2026}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For every existing positive integral Erd\H{o}s--Straus witness at a prime
\(p\equiv1\pmod {12}\), we prove sharp prime-growth bounds for an explicit
four-mark complex receiving map. In its original ordered label and receiving
coordinates,
\[
 \|O^{-1}\|_2=O(p^{3/2}),\qquad
 \|\wedge^2O^{-1}\|_2=O(1),\qquad
 \|\wedge^3O^{-1}\|_2=O(p^{-2}).
\]
Only one inverse singular direction can become unbounded. Two infinite prime
families in the class \(13\bmod24\) attain all three exponents; their complete
singular constants, a rank-one inverse limit, and a finite receiver heat limit
are evaluated.  On the exact exceptional divisor the receiver has rank three,
with an explicit kernel and cokernel hyperplane.  A fixed-\(c\) integral family
has four computed first singular corrections and an exhaustive observation
hierarchy with powers \(0,2,3,15\). The proof retains the two exact arithmetic channels, every
root label and square-root sign, the standard Hermitian coordinates, and the
conductor and metric factors. Three exact positive-coefficient comparisons
and a high-precision replay accompany the proof. The argument begins with an
existing witness and is not an existence theorem for the Erd\H{o}s--Straus
equation.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Acknowledgments and provenance}
The Type I/II coordinate antecedents are due to Elsholtz and Tao
\cite[\S2, Propositions 2.2 and 2.6]{ET}. The literal four-mark receiver and
its determinant polynomial entered this project through the ES--Fable
bridge~\cite{Receiver}; the complete integral sign theorem and coefficient
arrays used here are proved in~\cite{GlobalSign}. The three-dimensional
Jacobian construction that motivated that bridge was publicly announced by
Levent Alp\"oge, with Akhil Mathew credited for the question and Claude
Fable~5 for the work producing the construction; Tao's later article is an
independent exposition~\cite{TaoDigestion}. This paper proves only the ES
receiver statements displayed here. Dirichlet's theorem supplies infinitely
many primes in the reduced class \(13\bmod24\)~\cite{Dirichlet}.
