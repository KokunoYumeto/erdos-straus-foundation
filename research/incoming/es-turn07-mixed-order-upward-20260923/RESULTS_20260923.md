## SZ-20260923-001 — Mixed-order coefficient completion

For a cyclic fine-defect group `Z/D`, a strict gcd chain
`g_0=D`, `g_j=gcd(g_{j-1},lambda_{i_j})`, `g_k=1` with relative radices
`m_j=g_{j-1}/g_j` gives an explicit reverse-decoded section whenever the actual
digit length in each selected coordinate is at least `m_j`. Every original
coefficient has the proved positive integer lower bound in equation (lower),
with exact equality at the radix lengths. The transported law retains cyclic
carry. Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem `thm:chain`, equations `lower`, `radix`, and
`decode`. Certificates: `certificates/abstract.json` and
`certificates/source_parts.json`. Human lineage for the original ES coordinates:
Elsholtz--Tao, Section 2, Propositions 2.2 and 2.6; Yamamoto, Lemma 2,
equation (4), page 38.

## SZ-20260923-002 — Exact divisor-DAG optimizer

The strongest bound within the stated radix-chain certificate class is obtained
by dynamic programming on the divisor DAG of `D`, with at most `n tau(D)`
candidate edges. Failure of this algorithm is only failure of this certificate
class. Proof: `core.tex`, Proposition `prop:dag`. Independent exhaustive
ordered-subsequence comparison: `check_independent.py`, `check_abstract` and
`full_chain_certificate`.

## SZ-20260923-003 — Arithmetic mixed-order packets

The `D=9` and `D=15` hard-prime packet families have uniform original
coefficient two while the earlier unit-width test fails; the `D=15` selected
chain uses two nonunit steps. The exact packet polynomials, word inverses,
reduced prime progressions and full words are proved in `core.tex`, section
“Actual prime arithmetic with genuinely mixed orders,” and certified in
`certificates/packets.json` and `certificates/prime_certificates.json`.
Dirichlet's theorem is used only after reducedness is proved; see Sutherland,
Theorem 18.1.

## SZ-20260923-004 — Sharp last-capacity boundary

For every odd `D>1` and every strict chain ending at one, there are actual
hard-prime packets whose coefficient is zero on `H_m=m Z/D` and one on its
complement after the last radix interval loses one digit. Its stabilizer is
exactly `H_m`. The finite `D=9` realization at `p=3904062961` has coefficient
vector `(0,1,1,0,1,1,0,1,1)` and stabilizer `{0,3,6}`. The same prime has a
separate full exterior witness, so the boundary is not an ES counterexample.
Proof: `core.tex`, subsection “Arithmetic sharpness at an arbitrary odd defect.”
Evidence: `certificates/arithmetic_boundary.json`.

## SZ-20260923-005 — Complete plus-factor fibre and upward return

At fixed `p,C`, every cell preserving `M=p+C=4a+s` is classified by the exact
divisor conditions in Theorem `thm:plus`; its inverse, unit-ray terminal gate,
coprimality, first-denominator displacement and complete canonical incoming
trace fibre are proved. At `p=67369`, the proper M trace at `a=16849` maps to
the full E state at `a'=16850`, and the formula extends over the reduced prime
progression `p=67369+3775800k`. Proof: `core.tex`, section “A factor exchange
that permits controlled upward movement.” Evidence:
`certificates/upward.json`. The inherited obstruction being crossed is proved
in `../es-turn07-square-zero-pell-20260922/core.tex`, Section 7.

## SZ-20260923-006 — Exact affine E/M channel operator

For a fixed shell and the same original divisor word, simultaneous E/M trace
admissibility is equivalent to one trace gate together with `K | (p-1)`. On
that locus the full fine coordinates are
`c_E=(4u+1)/K`, `c_M=p^(-1)(p+4u)/K`, and
`c_E=c_M-(p-1)/K` in `Z/D`. Hence the labelled two-channel coefficient vector
is `J=(I+T_omega)C`. Since `D` is odd, this operator is injective, has an exact
alternating inverse on each translation orbit, and has one integral parity
cokernel bit per orbit. Uniform paired positivity is the exact support-cover
condition, not a consequence of total coefficient mass. Proof: `core.tex`,
Theorem `thm:affine-channels`, equations `common-trace` through
`support-bound`. The result is derived from the original channel formulas; no
finite search is used.

## SZ-20260923-007 — Partial-chain quotient criterion and obstruction

A strict mixed-order chain ending at a divisor `g` gives the exact decomposition
`C_x=B Q_(x mod g)+E_x`, with `E_x>=0` retaining every terminal interval. Its
complete-block subsource forces paired occupancy precisely when the quotient
support and its shift by `omega mod g` cover `Z/g`. For odd `g`, the sharp
support threshold is `(g+gcd(g,omega))/2`. If all residual steps lie in a
proper subgroup, two channel labels occupy at most two of at least three
cosets, so this certificate cannot close. Proof: `core.tex`, Theorem
`thm:partial-chain`, equations `partial-chain` through
`quotient-support-bound`. This identifies the structural datum still needed
after a partial radix chain; it does not infer arbitrary-prime occupancy.
