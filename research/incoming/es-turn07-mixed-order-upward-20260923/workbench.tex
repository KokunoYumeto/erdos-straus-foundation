\begin{abstract}
The complete bounded divisor source has an additive fine-defect coordinate.
On the exact common-trace locus, the E and M coordinates differ by a fixed
affine shift. Their labelled count operator is injective, has an alternating
inverse and an orbit-parity cokernel, and reduces partial radix chains to an
exact quotient-support cover. We then give a mixed-order subgroup-chain section
with a literal inverse, a positive
integer coefficient bound, and an optimal finite divisor-DAG test within that
certificate class. The section retains cyclic carries and actual exponent
multiplicities. Original hard-prime packets with defects 9 and 15 pass this test
while the previous unit-direction width test fails. A reduced prime progression
realizes an exact missing-quotient boundary. Separately, a labelled factor exchange
preserves $4a+s$ while changing the shell and the word; it returns an integral
state at the first full shell above the critical $p=67369$ trace. The same one-shell
upward return holds on an explicit infinite progression of hard primes. Neither
criterion is asserted to produce an initial source at every prime.
\end{abstract}
