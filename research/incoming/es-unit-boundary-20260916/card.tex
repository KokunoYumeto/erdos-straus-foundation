\begin{center}{\LARGE\bfseries The unit carries the target}\par
\vspace{2mm}{\large A relative boundary packet for Erd\H{o}s--Straus}
\end{center}
\noindent For an actual positive integer counting polynomial $P$, retain its
entire relative unit, not just its order of vanishing:
\[
\boxed{P=\mathcal N_D\widehat q+2E,\qquad E\ge0.}
\]
Here $\mathcal N_D=1+X^D+\cdots+X^{o-D}$ and $\widehat q$ records the
actual parity pattern on the quotient. For an available complementary packet,
\[
\boxed{[X^t]PG\ge[X^{t\bmod D}]\widehat q\,\pi_DG.}
\]
The discarded even part would still contain original labelled words.

\vspace{3mm}\hrule\vspace{3mm}
\noindent\textbf{One exact example.} In $\mathbb Z[C_{16}]$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &(1+X+X^2+X^3)(1+X^{11})(1+X^4)\\
 &\quad=(1+X^8)(X^3+X^4+X^5+X^6+X^7)
       +2(1+X+X^2).
\end{aligned}
\]
The quotient factor is a unit modulo two, but not the unit $1$.
It is precisely what retains the target coefficient at residue $12$.
