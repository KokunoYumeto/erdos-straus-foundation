\title{Retaining the unit in an Erd\H{o}s--Straus boundary packet}
\author{The Clankers}

\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
A positive bounded divisor packet can reduce to a subgroup norm times a
non-monomial unit. Retaining that unit yields an exact integer lower coefficient
bound, a constructive original-state inverse, and a relative boundary comparison
with its full integral index and Gram. A uniform explicit factor family has
one middle cofactor of minimum occurrence length $2^{k-5}+1$ in either role,
while the preceding canonical pair-block/stride tests fail. Three members have
deterministic primality certificates. The infinite family consists of integer
parameters; no prime infinitude for its derived parameters or universal ES result
is claimed. A higher-arity shear provides a separate surviving correspondence.
\end{abstract}
