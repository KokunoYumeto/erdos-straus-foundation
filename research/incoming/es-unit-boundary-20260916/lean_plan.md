# Lean-ready dependency plan — not a Lean build

Use the workbench's actual coordinated toolchain; no toolchain or mathlib update
is authorized by this note. Names below are proposed obligations, not declarations
claimed to exist.

1. `fixed_seed_cofactor_equiv`: A1--A3 with positivity, R,Q<p and original u.
2. `prime_valuation_normalization`: gcd normalization and ordered inverse.
3. `affine_actual_box`: endpoint availability and injective inverse on disjoint blocks.
4. `role_fibre`: cofactor/residual choices have fibres at most two after forgetting role.
5. `cyclic_T_equiv`: F2[C_(2^n)] equals F2[T]/T^(2^n), retaining binomial coordinates.
6. `relative_norm`: norm_D = T^(o-D), full constant-on-cosets image.
7. `exact_boundary_readout`: B2 for every nonzero parity polynomial in the declared layer.
8. `positive_even_remainder`: B3 over natural integer coefficients, not only in F2.
9. `labelled_remainder_partition`: chosen odd representative plus ordered residual pairs.
10. `integer_complement_convolution`: B4 with all multiplicities and disjoint source indices.
11. `unit_inverse`: finite geometric inverse modulo T^D.
12. `singular_relative_map`: kernel T^(D-z), image T^nu, inverse modulo full kernel.
13. `BR1_actual_instance`: use F2[[T]], not the polynomial ring with a false ideal equality.
14. `BR2_X_action`: multiplication by X commutes with the actual f.
15. `supported_zero`: the coefficient reduction keeps its receiving support, not tau.
16. `binary_packet_exact_order`: C1--C3 with original submask parameters.
17. `odd_suffix_inverse`: one of two suffix coefficients is odd; complete terminating path.
18. `weight_retention_binary`: induction using F=A+X^mB; classical antecedent retained.
19. `geometric_cyclic_inverse`: D1, all integer factors and rational denominators.
20. `integral_circulant_image`: coefficient-sum criterion, cokernel Z/h, determinant magnitude.
21. `coefficient_Gram`: D3, no claim of norm preservation.
22. `word_quotient_metric`: full fibre relations, canonical 1/P_r lift and Pythagoras.
23. `available_periodic_domain`: D4 and its exact inverse-image constraints.
24. `original_word_Gram`: D5, including every mixed term and zero-fibre condition.
25. `family_integer_packet`: E2 as an integer coefficient identity for every dyadic d>=4.
26. `family_unique_cofactor`: all four choices of outside prime and q exponent.
27. `family_exact_lengths`: E3--E4 in both roles, without a search cutoff.
28. `canonical_capacity_envelope`: E5 for the explicitly declared singleton/pair class.
29. `three_prime_shear`: E6, inverse and original endpoint availability.
30. `auxiliary_prime_family`: Dirichlet/CRT input only for auxiliary primes; derived p not presumed prime.
31. `complete_order_prime_certificate`: all factors of p-1 prime plus the checked modular orders.
32. `small_certificate_replay`: exact example packets, normalizations and deterministic prime proofs.

Do not import a universal ES occupancy axiom, an analytic theta norm, an unbounded
prime supply inside one prescribed N, or a claim that the lower boundary inverse
is an inverse of ordinary quotient summation. Integer lower coefficients may be
even and positive; parity-only inference is used only at the selected high word.
