# Attempt record

## Starting point

The previous p-adic tranche strengthens a sufficient factor capacity, but its
uniform onset is enormous. Its estimate presupposes available powers of 3 and 11.
The user asked both to continue the actual arithmetic and to record work in PRs.

## Selected calculation

An available relative packet has the form `T^nu U(T)`. Previous simple selectors
used only its order or arranged a bare subgroup norm. The next concrete question
was whether retaining U, rather than deleting it, supplies a constructive target
coefficient before the original successful states have been enumerated.

## What worked

The complete parity readout is constant on the relative cosets and has an exact
nonnegative integer even remainder. Multiplying by an independently available
complementary packet gives a genuine lower target coefficient. Its inverse selects
one real lower word and then an odd high-word path. Original labels and even-word
pairs make the integer decomposition reversible as a correspondence.

The family with q-log `3d-1`, r-log `5d/2`, s-log `d/2+2` has a non-monomial unit
`X^(d-1)+...+X^(2d-1)`. It has exactly one original cofactor and an unbounded minimum
length in either role. A symbolic capacity table proves that every pattern in the
preceding canonical paired-line/relative-stride class fails. Three actual prime
members were then supplied with complete-order certificates.

## Boundaries that matter

1. A nonzero relative kernel element is not a bare norm. Dropping U changes target
   positions; the smallest worked prime is an explicit counterexample to that deletion.
2. Reducing an even positive coefficient to zero does not make its word fibre absent.
3. The formal unit map can have a nontrivial integer index, and its inverse has signs.
4. A periodic formal amplitude can fail the original word-space availability test.
   The domain and original 1/P_r quotient metric are therefore computed separately.
5. The test-class separation is not a statement about all affine maps. A three-prime
   shear survives and is given with its inverse and compensating exponents.
6. The main comparison checks all seed inputs without first filtering to occupied
   branches. The earlier exploratory prototype did restrict to missed occupied rows;
   that prototype was not used as the final census.
7. The finite gain is one prime against the prior combined baseline, and zero against
   the stronger three-occurrence-plus-prior baseline. The unbounded theorem is the
   stronger structural result.

## Remaining arithmetic target

Force an actual unit-corrected positive lower coefficient, or a coordinated
higher-arity factor exchange, in at least one seed of every required prime.
Nothing in the formal unit inverse or its uniform finite algebra alone establishes
that universal factor inventory.

## Publication attempt

Full connector discovery found 48 read operations and no write operation. The
installed provider describes a hybrid CLI workflow, but `gh` and an authenticated
Git helper were absent and the local public remote lookup failed DNS resolution.
Account permission is not the obstacle: the connector reports push permission.
The response therefore creates and checks actual local commits and a portable
Git bundle, while explicitly leaving the remote PR uncreated.

## Final notation correction

The full integer norm in the circulant inverse is now named `J_D`; it is not
the relative norm `N_D` in the boundary inclusion. This removes an overloaded
symbol in D1 without changing its proved or computed identity.
