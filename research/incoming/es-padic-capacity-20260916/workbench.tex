\begin{abstract}
A specialized two-$2$-adic-logarithm estimate gives a uniform power saving in
the exponent capacity needed for the actual $3,11$ factor block modulo $2^k$.
The threshold is explicit, though extremely large. Exact two-dimensional
lattice rounding supplies original bounded words and ordered ES returns.
Dividing the relative exponent strides identifies the higher-layer relation
lattice at lower precision, without inflating the original logarithmic heights.
The imported theorem, new deductions, finite certificates, and unproved
universal arithmetic occupancy are kept separate.
\end{abstract}
