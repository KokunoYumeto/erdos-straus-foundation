# Lean-ready dependency plan — not a Lean build

This tranche contains a written proof and exact Python certificates. The imported
Chim theorem is not declared proved in Lean. A formalization importing it must
expose that dependency, not hide it behind an unreported axiom.

1. `dyadic_order_three`: the exact order of 3 modulo 2^k and both unit cosets.
2. `log311_unique`: the unique cyclic logarithm J_k; compatibility of reductions.
3. `relation_lattice_equiv`: original Z^2 basis and complete integral inverse.
4. `signed_two_power_morphism`: all four exponent-sign choices, unit multiplier,
   rational prime heights, and the separate zero-coordinate loci.
5. `log_interval_series`: atanh-series rational tail and three coarse enclosures.
6. `chim_specialization_A_B`: exact human-theorem parameters; source theorem is
   an explicit external obligation unless separately formalized.
7. `relation_exponential_lower_bound`: strict onset argument and floor-power bound.
8. `gauss_reduction_trace`: unimodular swaps/subtractions, equality stop, full trace.
9. `reduced_basis_shortest`: shortest-vector proof from the perpendicular component.
10. `determinant_rounding_error`: C1 and complete half-open rounding fibres.
11. `uniform_capacity_A_B`: both onset/saving constants and original word bounds.
12. `symbolic_threshold_arithmetic`: compare powers without expanding giant moduli.
13. `power_precision_equiv`: R1/D2 with group domain, image and inverse.
14. `actual_digit_partition`: unique Euclidean digits, exact floor capacities.
15. `relative_target_return`: congruence D4, original endpoints and target inverse.
16. `relative_boundary_comparison`: reuse the actual SplitZero quotient convention;
   coefficient repetition, quotient sum and supported zero are distinct maps.
17. `fixed_seed_state_equiv`: actual cofactors, gcd normalization and ordered ES inverse.
18. `disjoint_original_mass`: distinct words from disjoint source rectangles.
19. `finite_logarithm_algorithm`: normalized log series, odd inverses and exact tail.
20. `certificate_replay`: exact integer basis certificates and original witness examples.

Do not replace the original source metric, support carrier, prime multiplicities,
or ordered denominator fields. The logarithmic bound proves a capacity implication,
not that the required inventory occurs for every prime. No RH, analytic theta,
Frobenius/purity, fast factoring or universal ES statement is a dependency.
