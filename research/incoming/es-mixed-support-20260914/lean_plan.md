# Exact Lean-ready dependency plan — not a compiled proof

Use the existing workbench's coordinated toolchain/resource policy before any
Lean execution. These are proposed obligations, not claims that declarations
already exist or that imported CI certifies this application.

1. `return_denominator`: reproduce A1–A4 on the exact positive shell and its complete original exponent box; include the ordered inverse and rational candidates at failures.
2. `mixed_synthesis`: define A on a finite original counting space, its ordinary Gram K and signed form J. Prove ker K = ker A <= ker J.
3. `success_positive_index`: restrict Pi A to a positive subspace of J and prove injectivity; conclude n_+(J) <= H.
4. `successful_amplitude_bound`: derive H >= ceil(3 q_J(v)/(2 M(v))) from a proved pattern envelope; do not assume the location of a successful state.
5. `signed_schur_coordinates`: exact matrix congruence S5–S6, inverse triangular map and preservation of inertia.
6. `strict_ternary_test`: negative diagonal + positive pair determinants + positive full determinant gives inertia (1,2,0). Keep vanishing-pivot cases outside this strict lemma.
7. `mixed_moment_entries`: prove E4 from the four common source cells, not products of independent marginal counts.
8. `bounded_group_polynomial`: show the actual modular multiplication records all exponent tuples and the upper-half flag; retain coefficients.
9. `hard_certificate`: integer matrix N, pair determinants, full determinant, trial (-1,36,36), all four possible endpoint amplitudes, and exact positive fraction.
10. `primality_order_certificate`: recursive full-(n-1) order proof, terminal trial division, and the actual root. The main rational identity does not need the primality subtheorem.
11. `marked_witness_inverse`: h,r,s,kappa and ordered triple at the root, original beta and u on return.
12. `source_image_inverse`: pivot evaluation map for the three actual rows, kernel/image characterization, and K^{-1}A* on the image.
13. `complete_split_fibre`: exact intervals and n_beta, including their complete inverse and support predicate after addition.
14. `isometric_split_return`: duplication U into weight1/n and E=U*, EU=1; exact star-difference primitive of ker E and unchanged original labels.
15. `mixed_forms_split`: (UA)*h(Xtilde)(UA)=A*h(X)A for every polynomial; all cross terms, not only diagonal ones.
16. `boundary_comparison`: commute this finite source transport with the original prime-power top insertion and augmentation; supported zero remains supported.
17. `mixed_filter_order`: derive U1 from the proven discrete pointwise filter bound and test vectors. Singular K gives a quotient metric, not a fictitious inverse.
18. `infinite_direct_sum`: assemble the quotient-metric isometries, establish all domains, and prove U2 with a constant independent of support size and count.
19. `compression_square_defect`: U3 and vanishing exactly on an invariant feature subspace; retain the complement (I-P_A)X A and its norm.
20. `arithmetic_false_compression`: actual failed states u=1,12 at (37,18,35), average 6/35, correct value0, incorrect value17/343000, variance1/1225.

Not included: a proof of universal ES occupancy, analytic transfer of the Gamma
measure, RH, a first historical priority, or an identification of the three
feature labels with unrelated geometric dimensions. Finite tests are checked
instances of the lemmas and exact certificates, not a substitute for general
proofs.
