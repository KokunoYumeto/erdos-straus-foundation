\section{Original arithmetic and the question being tested}
Fix an integer $p\equiv1\pmod4$ and a shell
\[
 p/4<a<p/2,\quad R=4a-p,\quad \gcd(pa,R)=1,\qquad
 a=\prod_{i=1}^tq_i^{e_i}.
\]
Primality is needed for the usual complete-prime-row equivalence, but the
individual candidate identities below require only these displayed conditions.
An original state $\alpha$ retains $(p,a,R,c,\beta)$, the ordered prime list,
$-e_i\le\beta_i\le e_i$, and
\[
 u=\prod_iq_i^{e_i+\beta_i},\quad c\in\{E,M\},\quad
 g_E=4u+1,\quad g_M=u+a,\quad
 D_\alpha=\frac R{\gcd(R,g_c)},\quad x_\alpha=D_\alpha^{-1}.
 \tag{A1}
\]
The canonical source has counting measure on these labels, not on distinct
residues, split occurrences or sorted triples. In particular,
\[
 x_\alpha\in\{1,1/3,1/5,\ldots\},\quad
 H=\#\{\alpha:D_\alpha=1\}.
 \tag{A2}
\]
All failed states have $x\le1/3$ because $R$ is positive and odd.

The inherited positive rational triples are
\[
 E:\ \left(a,\frac{pa+a^2/u}{R},\frac{pa+p^2u}{R}\right),\qquad
 M:\ \left(a,\frac{p(a+a^2/u)}{R},\frac{p(a+u)}{R}\right).
 \tag{A3}
\]
They obey $(RY-pa)(RZ-pa)=p^2a^2$, hence have reciprocal sum $4/p$.
The gcds of the two nonfirst numerators with $R$ are both
$\gcd(R,g_c)$: reduce modulo $R$ using $4a\equiv p$ and multiply by the
units $a,u,p,4$ as required. Thus their reduced denominators are exactly $D$.
At a hit let $d_0=\gcd(a,u)$ and
\[
 (h,r,s)=(d_0^2/u,u/d_0,a/d_0),\quad
 a=hrs,\quad u=hr^2,\quad \gcd(r,s)=1.
\]
Prime valuations prove all integrality assertions. The original ordered return is
\[
 E:\ \kappa=(pr+s)/R,\quad(a,hs\kappa,phr\kappa),\qquad
 M:\ \lambda=(r+s)/R,\quad(a,phs\lambda,phr\lambda).
 \tag{A4}
\]
The inverse recovers $u=a^2/(RY-pa)$ in E and $u=pa^2/(RY-pa)$ in M,
then every $\beta_i=v_{q_i}(u)-e_i$. These are inherited Type I/II coordinates
\cite[\S2, Propositions 2.2 and 2.6]{ET}; no new parametrization is claimed.
The middle reversal $u\mapsto a^2/u$ and an exterior ordering bit, when used,
remain distinct operations. No positive-Koide restriction is imposed in the new
mixed test.

\section{One original source, several overlapping observations}
Let $\Omega$ be a finite set of original states, and set
$\mathcal H=\mathbb R^\Omega$ with its original counting inner product.
Take explicit real functions $f_0,\ldots,f_{r-1}$ on this same set. Their
supports may overlap. Define the synthesis map and its two pullback forms:
\[
 A:\mathbb R^r\to\mathcal H,\quad (Ac)_\alpha=\sum_i c_if_i(\alpha),
 \quad K=A^*A,\quad
 J=A^*(X-\tfrac13I)A,\quad Xe_\alpha=x_\alpha e_\alpha.
 \tag{S1}
\]
Thus
\[
 K_{ij}=\sum_\alpha f_i(\alpha)f_j(\alpha),\qquad
 J_{ij}=\sum_\alpha(x_\alpha-\tfrac13)f_i(\alpha)f_j(\alpha).
 \tag{S2}
\]
Every cross term is on the original arithmetic source. Separate labels for two
columns do not make their images orthogonal.
These are finite signed localizing forms, a classical construction
\cite[\S2, equations (2.3)--(2.4); Theorem 3.2]{Lasserre}.
The particular support functions and arithmetic certificates below are the
new application, not a claim to have invented that construction.

\begin{theorem}[Mixed-support positivity and multiplicity]
For the actual forms (S1), $\ker K=\ker A\subseteq\ker J$ and
\[
 n_+(J)\le\operatorname{rank}(\Pi A)\le H,
 \quad \Pi e_\alpha=\mathbf1_{D_\alpha=1}e_\alpha.
 \tag{S3}
\]
In particular, a coefficient vector with $c^*Jc>0$ forces an original hit.
If the explicitly proved bound $|(Ac)_\alpha|^2\le M(c)$ holds on all possible
successful patterns, then
\[
 \boxed{H\ge\left\lceil\frac{3c^*Jc}{2M(c)}\right\rceil}
 \quad(M(c)>0).
 \tag{S4}
\]
No occupied support, successful state, or unknown positive contribution is
assumed in calculating $J$ or in the pattern bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equality of kernels follows from $c^*Kc=\|Ac\|^2$.
On $D=1$ the multiplier $x-1/3$ equals $2/3$; on every other state it is
nonpositive. Thus
\[
 c^*Jc\le\frac23\sum_{D_\alpha=1}|(Ac)_\alpha|^2.
\]
This proves (S4). On a subspace where $J$ is positive definite, $\Pi A$ is
injective: a nonzero vector in its kernel would have nonpositive form by the
same calculation. Taking dimensions proves (S3).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Kernels, inverses and actual source norms.}
The image of $A$ has the quotient norm $c^*Kc$ on
$V=\mathbb R^r/\ker A$. In this metric $\bar A:V\to\operatorname{im}A$ is
an isometry. The inverse on the image is the coefficient class of any solution
of $Ac=f$; all solutions are $c_0+\ker A$. Exact row reduction gives a solution
and every kernel generator. If $K$ is invertible, the inverse is $K^{-1}A^*$.
For arbitrary original $f$ the discarded component is explicitly
$(I-P_A)f$, where $P_A$ is the orthogonal projection onto $\operatorname{im}A$.
The full return keeps this component, rather than identifying all original
states with an $r$-coordinate observation.

If several independently supplied support functions are first assembled by
addition, the larger synthesis map
$\bigoplus_i\mathbb R^{\operatorname{supp}f_i}\to\mathbb R^\Omega$ has
one fibre-sum condition at each original coordinate. Its kernel is the full
space of those sums zero, with primitives
$\sum_{i\ne i_0}v_i(e_i-e_{i_0})$ at that coordinate. Cancellation of values
retains the support union in the SplitZero carrier; it is not external absence.
Restricting this larger map to the specified column functions gives (S1).

\paragraph{The source comparison actually used.}
The pinned Zeta proof calculates a full joint covariance
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}K&C\\C^*&B\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, its Schur
complements, and the original relation-valued minimum-section correction
\cite[JSA12--JSA20]{Joint}. Our positive $K$ and signed $J$ are not those
analytic Gamma matrices. We apply their same-source/cross-term discipline,
and prove the signed elimination needed here directly. No Gamma asymptotic,
theta-source norm, or analytic endpoint claim is imported by analogy.

\section{The exact third-support contribution}
The following calculation keeps the first column and both other support
columns in their stated order. Write
\[
 J=\begin{pmatrix}-a&b^*\\b&C\end{pmatrix},\quad a>0.
\]
Then the reversible coordinate change
\[
 (t,z)\mapsto(s,z),\quad s=t-b^*z/a,
 \qquad(t,z)=(s+b^*z/a,z)
 \tag{S5}
\]
gives
\[
 (t,z)^*J(t,z)=-a|s|^2+z^*Sz,
 \qquad S=C+bb^*/a.
 \tag{S6}
\]
This is a congruence with the original signed form, not a positive-norm
orthogonal projection. It preserves inertia and has no unspecified scale.

\begin{theorem}[Strict ternary criterion]
For a real symmetric $3\times3$ form whose three diagonal entries are negative
and whose three two-coordinate principal determinants are positive,
\[
 \boxed{J\text{ has a positive direction}\iff\det J>0.}
 \tag{S7}
\]
When this holds its inertia is $(1,2,0)$: exactly one positive and two negative
directions. All two-column restrictions remain negative definite.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two diagonal entries of $S$ in (S6) are negative by the principal-minor
conditions involving the first coordinate. Also $\det J=-a\det S$.
A $2\times2$ symmetric matrix with negative diagonal entries is negative
semidefinite exactly when its determinant is nonnegative. A negative
determinant gives one positive and one negative direction. Equation (S6)
proves all assertions. Zero pivots and vanishing principal determinants are
excluded from this strict statement, not inverted; (S3)--(S4) still apply
there using their actual kernels.
\end{proof}

The mixed condition is $S_{12}^2>S_{11}S_{22}$, with the complete conditional
entry $S_{12}=C_{12}+b_1b_2/a$. Dropping $C_{12}$ or replacing the three-column
space by the common-polynomial diagonal restriction changes the problem.
The strict comparison refers to the three given coordinate subspaces.
It does not assert that every imaginable two-function reparametrization fails:
one may name an already combined function as a new column, provided its full
three-term decomposition remains attached.

\section{Finite certificates lifted back to all split supports}
For an actual budget split $e=f+b$, let $q(\xi,\eta,c)=(\xi+\eta,c)$.
Its complete inverse fibre is
\[
 \max(-f_i,\beta_i-b_i)\le\xi_i\le\min(f_i,\beta_i+b_i),\quad
 \eta=\beta-\xi,
\]
with size the product $n_\alpha$ of these interval lengths.
Equip the occurrence space with weight $1/n_{q\omega}$ and define
\[
 (Uv)_\omega=v_{q\omega},\qquad
 (EF)_\alpha=\frac1{n_\alpha}\sum_{q\omega=\alpha}F_\omega.
\]
Grouping each finite fibre proves $U^*=E$, $EU=I$, and
$\|F\|^2=\|EF\|^2+\|F-UEF\|^2$. The kernel of $E$ consists of fibre sums
zero, with the explicit star-difference primitives at every fibre.
The full source image retains those kernel coordinates on return.
For any original multiplier $h(X)$ and actual feature synthesis $A$,
\[
 \boxed{(UA)^*h(\widetilde X)(UA)=A^*h(X)A.}\tag{W1}
\]
Here $\widetilde X$ repeats the original coefficient and every support
predicate is pulled back through $q$. In particular $\beta_{457}>0$ is tested

\section{Uniform mixed control and the order of compression}
The previous discrete arithmetic filters are
\[
 \Phi_j(x)=x\prod_{\nu=1}^j\frac{(2\nu+1)x-1}{2\nu},\quad
 \varepsilon_j=\frac1{2^j j!(2j+3)}.
\]
At $x=1/(2k+1)$, the value is zero for $1\le k\le j$, otherwise
$(-1)^j\binom{k-1}{j}/(2k+1)^{j+1}$; it is one at $x=1$.
This proves the inherited bounds
$\Phi_{2j+1}\le\mathbf1_{\{1\}}\le\Phi_{2j}$ and uniform error
$\varepsilon_j$ directly \cite{Prior}. For every actual mixed synthesis $A$,
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 A^*\Phi_{2j+1}(X)A\preceq A^*\Pi A\preceq A^*\Phi_{2j}(X)A,\\
 0\preceq(-1)^j\bigl(A^*\Phi_j(X)A-A^*\Pi A\bigr)
          \preceq\varepsilon_j K.
 \end{gathered}\tag{U1}
\]
These are inequalities of full forms, including off-diagonal entries, not
entrywise inequalities. To prove them, evaluate both sides on any coefficient
vector and sum the pointwise filter inequality weighted by $|(Ac)_\alpha|^2$.

The conclusion is independent of the number and sizes of overlapping supports.
For any countable prime-indexed family, use the actual metric quotient
$V_p=\mathbb C^{r_p}/\ker A_p$ with Gram $K_p$. The induced maps $\bar A_p$
are isometries; their direct sum is an isometry into the original counting
Hilbert space. Consequently on $\bigoplus_pV_p$,
\[
 \boxed{\left\|\bigoplus_p\bar A_p^*
             (\Phi_j(X_p)-\Pi_p)\bar A_p\right\|\le\varepsilon_j.}
 \tag{U2}
\]
No uniform bound on $r_p$, support multiplicity, or the original row mass is
needed. The metric quotient and its inverse on the image are retained; this is
not a rescaling of the original successful-state count.
If the $A_p$ have proper images, $A_p^*\Pi_pA_p$ is a compression of the
success projection, not the projection itself and not its original trace.
The forgotten orthogonal sector remains specified in (S1).

\begin{theorem}[The order-of-composition correction]
Let $\bar A$ be an isometry onto a feature subspace and $P_A=\bar A\bar A^*$.
For $C=\bar A^*X\bar A$,
\[
 \boxed{\bar A^*X^2\bar A-C^2
 =\bar A^*X(I-P_A)X\bar A
 =R_A^*R_A,\quad R_A=(I-P_A)X\bar A.}\tag{U3}
\]
It vanishes exactly when the feature subspace is $X$-invariant. In general
one cannot replace the matrices in (U1) by $\Phi_j(C)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Insert $I=P_A+(I-P_A)$ between the two copies of $X$. The first summand is
$C^2$, the other is the displayed squared norm. Vanishing is equivalent to
$(I-P_A)X\bar A=0$, which is precisely invariance. Self-adjointness then
also makes the orthogonal complement invariant.
\end{proof}
The relation $R_A$ is retained with its source norm, as in the source's
minimum-section corrections; it is not discarded merely because its
projection to the feature quotient is zero. For the hard example the verifier
calculates the full nonzero positive semidefinite coordinate defect
$M_2-M_1K^{-1}M_1$, where $M_i=A^*X^iA$.

The danger has an actual ES countercheck. In the empty shell
$p=37,a=18,R=35$, the exterior states $u=1,12$ give
\[
 Q_1=(18,198/7,407/7),\qquad Q_2=(18,99/5,2442/5),
\]
with $D_1=7,D_2=5$. Compress their counting source onto its constant line.
Then $C=6/35$, so
\[
 \boxed{\bar A^*\Phi_3(X)\bar A=0,
 \qquad \Phi_3(C)=17/343000>0.}\tag{U4}
\]
The second expression would falsely report positivity. The quadratic relation
norm in (U3) is $1/1225$. Applying the filter first and then taking the mixed
Gram is therefore essential, even though each operation separately is defined.

\section{Evidence, scope and next arithmetic bound}
The default standard-library verification constructs all 9702 hard-shell
states and checks their rational identities, return denominators and ordered
inverses. It independently reconstitutes the four mixed gcd-moment rows by
bounded group multiplication, checks every two-coordinate negative form and
the full positive direction, and records all three integral witnesses.
It tests 729 symmetric integer forms including singular boundaries, 256
nonorthogonal support dictionaries at five filter degrees, the actual split
with 44550 occurrences, and six rejected false transformations.

The additional scan covers all 9531 first-half shells at the 54 primes
$p\equiv1\pmod{12}$, $p\le1500$: 412838 canonical states and 20177 choices
of the support $\beta_q>0$. There are 1693 certified choices covering the
685 occupied shells. There are no strictly ternary-only choices in this small
range; the fixed large example supplies the strict improvement. These counts
are not a new prime-coverage result, and the large example is not claimed least.
All inputs for the printed positive certificate are exact integers or rationals;
floating-point exploration is not used in its verification.

The new full three-support form, its explicit original arithmetic return, and
the uniform mixed-form transport are the contributions of this tranche.
Signed localizing forms, Schur congruences and compression identities have
classical antecedents. The scoped literature comparison did not establish
historical priority for the precise ES example; none is asserted.

For every hypothetical false prime, all such original mixed forms must be
negative semidefinite, including their ternary determinants and higher mixed
minors. The unresolved universal step is to force a positive direction in at
least one shell by an arithmetic estimate on the actual mixed moments.
Pairwise estimates omit a demonstrably relevant condition, but the correct
full condition is not yet proved positive for every prime. Uniform convergence
(U2) controls all retained observations without supplying that arithmetic
lower bound. No ES, RH, or global geometric conclusion is claimed.
