\begin{abstract}
On one complete marked divisor source, three explicitly overlapping observations
have a positive signed direction although each two-observation restriction is
negative definite. The certificate uses only mixed gcd moments through cubic
order and returns an original positive integral witness. We give the full
matrix, rational trial vector, support definitions and reconstruction.
The original counting metric and mixed cross terms are retained. This is a
strict sufficient-test improvement, not a universal Erd\H{o}s--Straus theorem.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Original states.}
Let $p\equiv1\pmod4$, $p/4<a<p/2$, $R=4a-p$, and $\gcd(pa,R)=1$.
For $a=\prod q_i^{e_i}$ retain every $c\in\{E,M\}$ and
$\beta_i\in[-e_i,e_i]_{\mathbb Z}$. Put
\[
 u=\prod_iq_i^{e_i+\beta_i},\quad g_E=4u+1,\quad g_M=u+a,\quad
 D=R/\gcd(R,g_c),\quad x=1/D.
\]
The positive rational triples are
\[
 E:\ \left(a,\frac{pa+a^2/u}{R},\frac{pa+p^2u}{R}\right),\qquad
 M:\ \left(a,\frac{p(a+a^2/u)}R,\frac{p(a+u)}R\right).
\]
They satisfy $(RY-pa)(RZ-pa)=p^2a^2$ and hence have reciprocal sum $4/p$.
Reduction modulo $R$ shows that both nonfirst reduced denominators equal $D$.
Thus a hit is exactly $D=1$; every failure has odd $D\ge3$.
These marked Type I/II coordinates are inherited [1].

\paragraph{Mixed observations, not independent sources.}
For original counting space $\mathbb R^\Omega$ and specified functions
$f_0,\ldots,f_{r-1}$ on the same states, define
\[
 (Av)_\alpha=\sum_i v_if_i(\alpha),\quad K=A^*A,\qquad
 J_{ij}=\sum_\alpha(x_\alpha-1/3)f_i(\alpha)f_j(\alpha).
 \tag{1}
\]
These are signed localizing forms [2]. Their cross terms record actual overlap.
The kernel of $K$ is exactly $\ker A$ and lies in $\ker J$. The original
norm on the coefficient quotient is $v^*Kv$, not an assigned identity matrix.
The source's joint Schur calculation [3, JSA12--JSA20] likewise keeps its
complete cross block; its analytic measure is not identified with (1).

\begin{theorem}[Positivity, count and a strict ternary test]
Let $H$ count original states with $D=1$. Then $n_+(J)\le H$.
If $|(Av)_\alpha|^2\le M(v)$ on every possible successful pattern, then
\[
 H\ge\left\lceil\frac{3v^*Jv}{2M(v)}\right\rceil\qquad(M(v)>0).
 \tag{2}
\]
For a symmetric $3\times3$ matrix with negative diagonal entries and positive
three two-coordinate principal determinants, a positive direction exists
exactly when its full determinant is positive. In that case its inertia is
$(1,2,0)$ while all two-coordinate restrictions are negative definite.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Failures contribute nonpositively to $v^*Jv$, while hits contribute
$(2/3)|(Av)_\alpha|^2$. This proves (2). Restricting $\Pi A$ to a positive
subspace of $J$ is injective: a vector with zero successful coordinates has
nonpositive form. Hence its dimension is at most $H$.
For the ternary assertion write $J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-a&b^*\\b&C\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
The invertible substitution $s=t-b^*z/a$ gives
\[
 (t,z)^*J(t,z)=-a s^2+z^*(C+bb^*/a)z.
 \tag{3}
\]
The latter $2\times2$ matrix has negative diagonal entries by the two-coordinate
conditions, and determinant $-\det J/a$. This proves all inertia claims.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Actual arithmetic certificate.}
Use the previously certified prime [4]
\[
\begin{split}
 p&=2671180768668258904496300170662649\\
 a&=(p+135)/4=2^5\,431^3\,457^3\,7229^4,\quad R=135.
\end{split}
\]
Its complete original source has $2\cdot11\cdot7\cdot7\cdot9=9702$ states.
Define supports without referring to successful-state labels:
\[
 \mathcal E=\{c=E\},\quad \mathcal B=\{\beta_{457}>0\},\qquad
 f_0=1,\quad f_1=x\mathbf1_{\mathcal E},\quad f_2=x\mathbf1_{\mathcal B}.
 \tag{4}
\]
The third predicate means $v_{457}(u)>3$. It is an original bounded-exponent
condition, not a successful-state label.
Let $S_k^{ij}$ denote the sum of $\gcd(R,g)^k$ in the four cells
$(\mathbf1_E,\mathbf1_B)=(i,j)$. Exact enumeration gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
(i,j)&S_0&S_1&S_2&S_3\\\hline
(0,0)&2772&19480&435596&14842144\\
(0,1)&2079&14819&332487&11329307\\
(1,0)&2772&19474&435524&14841442\\
(1,1)&2079&14805&368151&17899341
\end{array}\tag{5}
\]
These moments also follow from multiplying the four actual bounded exponent
polynomials modulo 135, retaining the $457$-half flag. This independent
calculation is included in the verifier.
The full signed matrix is
\[
 \boxed{3R^3J=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 -20121056100&-299246400&-256139010\\
 -299246400&-10273776&3997638\\
 -256139010&3997638&-6900186
 \end{pmatrix}.}\tag{6}
\]
The two-coordinate determinants are
\[
 117170815341873600,\quad73231837162654500,\quad54909855743292,
\]
whereas the full determinant is
\[
 799917306693954510200400>0.\tag{7}
\]
Thus every pair of the three \emph{specified} observations fails, and their
full mixture succeeds. This does not exclude a different reparametrization
that names an already combined function as one column.

An explicit rational positive trial is
\[
 F=\frac{-1+36x\mathbf1_E+36x\mathbf1_B}{71}.
\]
At $D=1$ it takes only the values $-1/71,35/71,1$, so $|F|\le1$ there.
Multiplying (6) by $(-1,36,36)$ gives
\[
 \boxed{\frac32\sum_\alpha(x_\alpha-1/3)F_\alpha^2
       =\frac{607462}{1890375}>0.}\tag{8}
\]
Theorem 1 proves a positive integral ES witness. Deleting just the mixed
entry would give the negative value $-1639754/17013375$ instead, losing the proof.
The older scalar first-degree test on $(1,x)$ fails on the combined source
and on each channel separately, as recalculated in the certificate [4].

\paragraph{An actual marked return.}
One reconstructed hit has
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \beta=(5,-2,3,-4),\quad u=4020447794899891321856,\quad c=E,\\
 h=431,\quad r=3054207776,\quad s=507303010196865101041,\\
 \kappa=60432156109394470448106286277616196397479.
\end{gathered}
\]
Directly, $a=hrs$, $u=hr^2$ and $135\kappa=pr+s$.
Therefore $(a,hs\kappa,phr\kappa)$ is a positive integer triple with
reciprocal sum $4/p$. The ordered inverse is $u=a^2/(RY-pa)$.
Every other returned coordinate follows by the stated gcd normalization;
none of the denominator order, prime exponents or channel is suppressed.

\paragraph{Original metric and all source information.}
The actual positive source Gram for (4) is
\[
 K=R^{-2}\begin{pmatrix}
176818950&4627665&3999240\\
4627665&803675&368151\\
3999240&368151&700638
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It is nonsingular: the actual rows $(M,1),(E,1),(M,457^4)$ give independent
coordinate readings. They recover the coefficients from the image by
$c_0=F(M,1)$, $c_1=(F(E,1)-c_0)/x(E,1)$ and
$c_2=(F(M,457^4)-c_0)/x(M,457^4)$.
The full inverse of an arbitrary source decomposition retains its orthogonal
remainder $(I-P_A)F$. It is not a claim that three observations encode all
9702 original states.

For a budget split $e=f+b$, the complete fibre over $\beta$ is
\[
 \max(-f_i,\beta_i-b_i)\le\xi_i\le\min(f_i,\beta_i+b_i),\quad
 \eta=\beta-\xi.
\]
Its size $n_\beta$ is the product of these interval lengths. Duplicating source
values into occurrences with weight $1/n_\beta$ is an isometry; its adjoint
averages each fibre. Thus it preserves $K,J$ and all their mixed entries.
The upper-half predicate is evaluated on $\beta=\xi+\eta$, not on $\xi$.
The tested split $f=(5,2,2,4),b=(0,1,1,0)$ has 44550 occurrences but original
mass 9702; seven raw hit occurrences have arithmetic mass three.

\paragraph{Uniform mixed filters, and a necessary correction.}
The inherited polynomials [4]
\[
 \Phi_j(x)=x\prod_{\nu=1}^j\frac{(2\nu+1)x-1}{2\nu},\qquad
 \varepsilon_j=\frac1{2^jj!(2j+3)}
\]
have one-sided factorial error on the odd reciprocal spectrum. Consequently,
for \emph{any} feature synthesis,
\[
 0\preceq(-1)^j\{A^*\Phi_j(X)A-A^*\Pi A\}
       \preceq\varepsilon_jK.\tag{9}
\]
This follows by evaluating on a coefficient vector and using the pointwise
bound with weight $|(Av)_\alpha|^2$. Passing to the quotient with its original
$K$ metric makes $A$ isometric. Direct sums over arbitrary growing prime-indexed
feature spaces therefore retain the same operator-norm error $\varepsilon_j$.
This bound controls mixed observations, not their unknown positive lower limit.

One must filter \emph{before} compression. If $\bar A$ is isometric,
$C=\bar A^*X\bar A$ and $P_A=\bar A\bar A^*$, then
\[
 \bar A^*X^2\bar A-C^2
 =\bar A^*X(I-P_A)X\bar A=R_A^*R_A,\quad R_A=(I-P_A)X\bar A.
\]
Insert $I=P_A+(I-P_A)$ to prove the identity. It vanishes precisely on an
$X$-invariant feature subspace. The actual empty shell $p=37,a=18,R=35$
has two exterior states $u=1,12$ with $D=7,5$. On their constant line,
\[
 C=6/35,\quad \bar A^*\Phi_3(X)\bar A=0,
 \quad \Phi_3(C)=17/343000>0.
\]
The latter ordering would give a false positivity test. Its discarded quadratic
relation norm is $1/1225$.

\paragraph{Scope and reproduction.}
The accompanying standard-library \texttt{verify.py} and
\texttt{prime\_certificate.json} reproduce (5)--(8), the full 9702-state
census, all three hits and their original inverse data. Primality is checked by
a recursive complete-$p-1$ order certificate (eight internal nodes, fifteen
trial-division leaves). The bounded scan through $p\le1500$ checks 9531 shells
and 20177 support choices without a false positive; it supplies no new prime
coverage. The example (6) is not claimed least. Localizing matrices and Schur
identities are classical; historical priority for this exact application is not
asserted. Universal positivity in at least one shell of every prime remains
unproved. All displayed sufficient implications have complete proofs above.
