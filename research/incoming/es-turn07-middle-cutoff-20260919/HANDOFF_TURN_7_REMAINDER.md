# Exact continuation after the Turn 7 atlas

The proven atlas reduces every oriented M state at a hard prime to a disjoint
finite union of the following searches:

1. `R<=B(p)`, `a=(p+R)/4`, and an original divisor `u|a^2` satisfying
   `u<a`, `R|u+a`, `R<=Q=(p+4u)/R`;
2. `j<=K(p)`, `Q=4j-1`, and `u|j^2` satisfying `Q|p+4u` and
   `R=(p+4u)/Q>Q`, followed by the exact inverse in equation (10).

The current theorem does not say that either search is occupied.  The exterior
E channel remains separate.  The next structural target is therefore:

> For every hard prime `p`, prove that at least one original E coefficient or
> one of the two exact M-chart coefficients is positive, and give the complete
> coordinate return to an ordered Erdős--Straus triple.

A proof may use a `p`-dependent inventory, the least nonresidue parameters, the
complete signed pair source from Turn 6, or a new exact morphism.  It may not
assume a fixed bound on `h` or `j`: the fixed-grade obstruction disproves that
route.  It may not identify a cofactor chart with the original shell having the
same integer, as the `p=2521` negative control disproves that identification.
Any successful replacement must state its domain, codomain, inverse or fibres,
exceptional locus and information loss.

The Turn 7 finite checker is useful for rejecting proposed inequalities.  A
numerical pattern becomes a theorem only after the corresponding coordinate
argument is supplied.  The equality family
`p=12r^2-40r+9`, `r=14+210n` is an exact integer family but no infinitude of
prime values has been proved or assumed.
