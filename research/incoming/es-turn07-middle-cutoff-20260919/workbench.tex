\title[Square-root middle atlas]{A sharp square-root atlas for middle Erd\H{o}s--Straus states\\
and an obstruction to fixed grades}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{September 19, 2026. Turn 7 of the cumulative programme.}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For every prime $p\equiv1\pmod8$, an oriented first-half middle state has
two odd cofactors $R,Q\equiv3\pmod4$.  We prove the sharp inequality
$4p\ge3\min(R,Q)^2+6\min(R,Q)-25$.  On the six hard residue classes modulo
$840$ this improves to $4p\ge3\min(R,Q)^2+14\min(R,Q)-81$, with a complete
equality classification and an attained example at $p=1801$.  The resulting
$O(\sqrt p)$ cutoff yields a disjoint, invertible two-chart atlas: a direct
chart in the original square-divisor coordinates and a cofactor chart with
$u\mid j^2$ and $4j-1\mid p+4u$.  Every coordinate map, inverse, ordering
condition and exceptional tie is retained.  We also prove unconditionally
that no fixed prime-independent cutoff on the original exterior grade $h$ and
middle grade $j$ can cover all hard primes, even though the constructed primes
have explicit endpoint solutions.  Two independent programs replay the finite
atlas through $3000$ and check a partial-factor order certificate.  Universal
Erd\H{o}s--Straus occupancy is not proved: the remaining problem is to force
at least one original E or M coefficient at every hard prime.
\end{abstract}
