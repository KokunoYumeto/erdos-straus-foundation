## Scope

Read and specialize the exact Zeta-workbench ES--Fabel inverse at original
Erdős--Straus reciprocal cubics. Prove the complete p-local fibre algebra,
its three rational points, the ramified/unramified channel distinction, and
its integral root-order index/conductor. Give an explicit labelled root map
with exactly one p-adic denominator, and its integral reverse.

Derive original negative-four-square E-to-M returns, including the unbounded
shape lines alpha=-4 and beta=-4. Retain the full source exponent bounds,
ordered denominator inverse, complete fibres and cross-branch collisions.

## Verification

Standard-library complete first-half replay through p3000; separate primitive
parameter implementation and exact factor-incidence verification; signed
inverse algebra, original polynomial Jacobian and Hensel digit checks. The
release receipt and JSON certificates record the actual totals and hashes.

## Nonclaims

This does not prove universal ES occupancy, turn an abstract inverse into an
initial arithmetic witness, or assert a Qp-model of a complex period matrix.
No historical-priority determination or Lean build. All additions are under
a new research directory; existing results are unchanged.
