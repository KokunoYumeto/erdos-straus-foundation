\begin{abstract}
The reciprocal cubic of every marked first-half Erd\H{o}s--Straus state is a
literal target of the four-dimensional polynomial used in the ES--Fabel and
Split-Zero conductor comparison. We determine its complete local fibre algebra
at the original prime. Exterior states yield two ramified quadratic factors;
middle states yield two unramified quadratic factors. Both have exactly three
rational local inverse points, with all seven geometric affine branches and
integral specializations accounted for. Separately, negative-four-square shape
coordinates give original same-prime middle states on an exact divisor domain,
covering entire unbounded shape lines. All arithmetic coordinates, fibres and
ordered denominator returns are explicit. Neither conclusion proves universal
Erd\H{o}s--Straus occupancy.
\end{abstract}
