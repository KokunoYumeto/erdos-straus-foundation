\begin{abstract}
We apply the latest SplitZero boundary comparison and original-metric secant
formulas to the complete labelled divisor states of Erd\H{o}s--Straus shells.
Independent prime-power nilpotents retain every local return defect. The
comparison of their joint socle with their ordinary augmentation is exactly
the original success projector. A singularity-safe pair of canonical moment
problems gives certified lower and upper bounds on its trace, preserving the
weights of overlapping split representations. Four original moments determine
all but one of the 9531 shell counts in the stated finite comparison; six
moments resolve the remaining case. No universal ES, RH, or priority claim is made.
\end{abstract}
