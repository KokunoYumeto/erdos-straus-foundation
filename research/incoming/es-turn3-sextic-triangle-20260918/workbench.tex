\title{Turn 3: a closed primitive sextic triangle:\\ the retained joint coefficient}
\author{The Clankers}
\date{18 September 2026. Research tranche; exact scope stated below.}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We give a certified prime for which the canonical shifted-factor system has a
closed three-cycle of simultaneous exterior/middle failures, each with actual
stabilizer index six and a saturated inactive factor block. It realizes both the
smallest active prime-residual obstruction and the shortest cycle in that sector.
The full parity--cubic coefficient is computed: its cubic marginal is uniformly
positive while every joint target is missing. Its complete integer marginal
fibres, characteristic-two decomposition, fine extension carries and denominator
returns are retained. This refutes canonical primitive-sextic cycle escape, not
the Erd\H{o}s--Straus conjecture. An explicit ES solution is supplied at the same prime.
\end{abstract}
