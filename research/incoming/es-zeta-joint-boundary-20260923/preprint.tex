\begin{abstract}
Full-support methods from the current Split-Zero programme suggest retaining
joint divisor directions and the boundary of their finite arithmetic action.
We construct, for every $n\ge2$, original $2\cdot3^n$-word ES trace packets at
infinitely many hard primes whose two full words use all $n$ directions.
Every proper coordinate face and both previously used capacity criteria miss
them. An explicit ordinary-radix inverse recovers the witnesses, with a
rank-one boundary term. A separate arithmetic example has uniform primary
marginals but no full middle word. This is a theorem about the stated
original packets, not universal ES occupancy or a new abstract radix theorem.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Original source.}
Let $p\equiv1\pmod4$ be prime, $p/4<a<p/2$, $R=4a-p$, and retain
$u\mid a^2$. With $g=\gcd(a,u)$, put
$(h,r,s)=(g^2/u,u/g,a/g)$. The classical Type-I/II maps are
\[
 E:(a,hs\kappa,phr\kappa),\quad\kappa=(pr+s)/R;
 \qquad M:(a,phs\lambda,phr\lambda),\quad\lambda=(r+s)/R.
\]
Their full gates are $R\mid4u+1$ and $R\mid p+4u$; their rational tail sums
are integral precisely at the squared gates. Define
$K=\prod_{q^e\parallel R}q^{\lceil e/2\rceil}$ and $D=R/K$.
Then $D\mid K$. On a trace,
\[
 p^\eta u/a=-1+Kc\pmod R,\quad c\in\mathbb Z/D,
 \qquad\eta=1\ (E),\ 0\ (M).
\]
The full gate is $c=0$ and the tail denominator is $D/\gcd(D,c)$.
These formulas follow directly from $a=hrs,u=hr^2$; the Type-I/II
antecedent is [1, \S2]. Every prime exponent of $u$ remains in its actual
interval $[0,2v_q(a)]$.

\begin{theorem}[Unbounded original mixed order]
For every $n\ge2$, infinitely many primes $p\equiv1\pmod{840}$ have a
specified original M trace packet with $2\cdot3^n$ words, fine modulus
$D=3^n-2$, and exactly two full words. Each requires all $n$ selected
centred-exponent directions to be nonzero. Fixing any one of them at zero
leaves no full word. Every fine residue nevertheless has at least two
preimages, with the explicit bounded inverse below.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $V=3^n$, $L=(V-1)/2$, $C=L-2$, $K=3D$ and $R=3D^2$. Fix a unit
$\ell\pmod D$. Choose distinct primes $q_0,\ldots,q_n>7$ in the reduced
classes
\[
 q_0\equiv-1+K\ell C\pmod R,
 \qquad q_{j+1}\equiv1+K\ell3^j\pmod R\quad(0\le j<n).
\]
Every class is $1$ or $-1$ at each prime of $R$, so Dirichlet applies [2].
Set $A=\prod q_i$ and use CRT to impose
\[
 H\equiv\frac{R+1}{4}A^{-1}\pmod{210},\qquad H\equiv1\pmod A,
 \qquad p=4AH-R.
\]
This prime progression has modulus $840A^2$ and is reduced: $p\equiv1$
modulo 840 and $p\equiv-R\ne0$ at each prime of $A$. There are infinitely
many prime members $p>R$. Their original shell is $a=AH$.

Take the actual divisors
\[
 u=Hq_0^{1+\sigma}\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}q_{j+1}^{1+\beta_j},
 \qquad\sigma=\pm1,\quad\beta_j\in\{-1,0,1\}.
\]
Since $K^2=0\pmod R$, including the negative powers with their inverses,
\[
 c=\ell\left(\sigma C-\sum_j3^j\beta_j\right)\pmod D.
\]
All words are therefore traces. Balanced ternary bijects the $3^n$ choices
onto $[-L,L]$. The unique representative of $C\pmod{2L-1}$ in that interval
is $C=L-2$, with digits $(-1,1,\ldots,1)$. The negative branch has the
opposite digits. This proves both full words and the proper-face claim.

For the complete inverse put $J=\sum_j3^j(\beta_j+1)$, so $0\le J<V=D+2$.
The two branches have respectively
$c=2\ell-\ell J$ and $c=-\ell-\ell J$. For any target $c$, solve the
single residue for the least $J_0\in[0,D)$; the entire fibre is
$J=J_0+kD<V$. Ternary integer division recovers every original digit.
Each branch has one or two preimages of each residue, proving the bound.
The gcd normalization then returns the ordered original M denominators,
with quotient $(r+s)/R$ integral at each full word.
\end{proof}

The preceding capacity tests genuinely fail here. In nonnegative
coordinates the lengths are $(2,3,\ldots,3)$ and the steps are
$(3\ell,\ell,3\ell,\ldots,3^{n-1}\ell)$. All steps are units modulo $D$,
but every length is less than $D$, so a subgroup-order chain cannot even
start. The total unit width is $2n+1<D-1$. The success comes from the
coherent consecutive powers, not larger one-coordinate capacity.

\paragraph{Exact support map.}
On the original word basis let $E_A$ keep the words whose active exponent
set is contained in $A\subseteq I=\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$. Then
$E_AE_B=E_{A\cap B}$. The zero-symbol join algebra of Split-Zero 032 FSJ1--4
acts by $[z_B]\mapsto E_{I\setminus B}$. Its original M\"obius idempotent
$\xi_B$ projects exactly onto active set $I\setminus B$. If $G$ is the
diagonal full-gate projection, then
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(GE_A)=0\ (A\ne I),\qquad\operatorname{Tr}(GE_I)=2.
\]
Thus the two actual witnesses occupy the highest mixed component. This is
an exact finite support representation, not an asserted extension of the
original analytic ST4 action.

\begin{proposition}[Retained boundary and integral image]
Let $T_se_x=e_{x+s}$ on $C_D$, with $s$ a unit. For any integer coefficient
vector $v$, let $V=\sum v_x$ and $b=(1-T_s)v$. Then
\[
 v_x=\frac{V-\sum_{j=1}^{D-1}(D-j)b_{x+js}}D.
\]
The image consists exactly of $\sum b_x=0$ and the divisibility by $D$ of
one displayed numerator. The map $v\mapsto(V,b)$ is injective and its
cokernel is $\mathbb Z/D$. If $B=\frac12\sum|b_x|$, then
\[
 v_x\ge\left\lceil\frac{V-(D-1)B}{D}\right\rceil.
\]
For an actual consecutive source $P e_J=e_{c_0+sJ}$, $0\le J<V$, and
$Ue_J=e_{J+1\bmod V}$,
\[
 PU-T_sP=(e_{c_0}-e_{c_0+sV})e_{V-1}^*.
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Integrate $b$ along the cycle: $v_{x+js}=v_x+\sum_{i=1}^jb_{x+is}$.
Summation gives the inverse and its one integral syndrome. Conversely the
inverse has the specified boundary and mass. The syndrome is onto modulo
$D$ by $(V,b)=(1,0)$. The range of $v$ is at most $B$, giving
$V\le D\min v+(D-1)B$; equality occurs at $(0,B,\ldots,B)$.
The last identity is equality on every source column except the last,
whose two images are precisely the displayed basis vectors.
\end{proof}
The boundary vanishes exactly when $D\mid V$. An equivariant bijection of
the whole source would require target fibres to have equal size, so it
cannot erase this defect otherwise. Counting norms and fibre multiplicities
are retained. The integer section picks one actual word in each occupied
bin; the minimum-norm section averages its words and need not be integral.

\paragraph{An affine two-channel consequence.}
If the actual prime satisfies $p\equiv1\pmod K$, put
$\omega=(p-1)/K\pmod D$. Multiplication by $p$ on the retained affine
principal part $-1/R+c/D\in\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ is
$c\mapsto c-\omega$, whereas its centred action $c\mapsto pc$ is the
identity. Thus the same original exponent packet has full count
$C_{c_0}+C_{c_0-\omega}$ in its two distinct E/M channels. For consecutive
unit step $\ell$, $V=qD+r$, and $d=\omega\ell^{-1}\pmod D$, its sharp
uniform minimum is $2q+\mathbf1_{r\ge D-\min(d,D-d)}$: the two remainder
intervals cover the cycle precisely at that threshold.
At $p=6841,a=1717,R=27$, the actual two-word part
$u\in\{101,29189\}$ has M coefficient vector $(1,0,1)$, target $c_0=1$
and $\omega=1$. Its M gate is empty but its E gate has one original word,
returning $(1717,436118,175499014)$. No new divisor or channel exponent is
inserted. The corrected affine origin, not a norm estimate, supplies this
same-shell return.

\paragraph{A full-shell example.}
For $n=3$, $D=25$, $K=75$, $R=1875$, take $\ell=3$ and
\[
 (q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3)=(599,5851,2551,151),\qquad H=1421=7^2\cdot29.
\]
The prime is $p=7673586546314641$ and $a=1918396636579129$. Of all 1215
original divisors of $a^2$, the full E channel is empty and the M channel
has exactly the two complementary words in the 54-word packet. One is
\[
 u=48646799621,\qquad(h,r,s,\lambda)=(1421,5851,230735399,123062),
\]
with ordered denominators
\[
 (1918396636579129,\quad309621186685901359565863932118418,
 \quad7851389822067175981176039082).
\]
All primality, exponent, reciprocal and inverse checks are exact in the
standard-library certificates. This isolated $H$ is coprime to the selected
primes; the stronger $H\equiv1\pmod A$ was only a convenient condition for
the infinite-progression proof.

\paragraph{The corresponding local-to-joint obstruction.}
At $p=34442321771655001$, $a=101\cdot179\cdot6121\cdot8821^2$ and
$R=675$, choose the original 15 M words
$u=101\cdot179^2\cdot6121^{1+\beta_1}\cdot8821^{2+\beta_2}$,
where $-1\le\beta_1\le1$ and $-2\le\beta_2\le2$.
Their defects modulo 15 are $c=4-\beta_1-\beta_2$, giving
\[
 v=(0,1,2,3,3,3,2,1,0,0,0,0,0,0,0).
\]
Both primary marginals are exactly uniform: $(5,5,5)$ modulo three and
$(3,3,3,3,3)$ modulo five. Yet $v_0=0$. In the orthogonal CRT support
splitting, the constant component is $1$, both first mixed components
vanish, and the full mixed component is $v-1$, with value $-1$ at zero.
The full 135-word surrounding box has three E states but no M states.
This tests the marginal inference, not the ES conjecture.

\paragraph{The finite arithmetic is not an unchanged complex jet.}
The exact maps corresponding to Zeta 031/032 are
\[
 \mathbb Z[C_D]/(T-1)^2\simeq\mathbb Z[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2,D\epsilon),
 \qquad c\epsilon\mapsto Kc\pmod R,
 \qquad c\epsilon\mapsto c/D\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
\]
They retain the finite torsion, unlike tensoring the jet with $\mathbb Q$.
For the actual degree operator $(A_mf)(x)=f(mx)$ on $\ell^2(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)$,
compressing to $C_D$ gives
\[
 A_{m,D}^*A_{m,D}=g\Pi_g,\qquad
 B_{m,D}^*B_{m,D}=mI-g\Pi_g,\qquad g=\gcd(m,D).
\]
Here $B$ is the escaped component, and $\Pi_g$ keeps residues divisible by
$g$. Both formulas follow by counting preimages. At the original ES prime
$g=1$: the finite action is a permutation and the escaped mass is $(p-1)I$.
The supported-zero distributional action and the absent label are not
identified. Neither the infinite norm gain nor the local marginals force
an initial ES word. Universal positivity of the complete original
all-shell coefficient sum remains the Stage 7 obligation.
