# Typed maps, inverses, fibres and scope — Turn 3

1. **Canonical source.** Input: a hard prime p and a prime q<p with (q/p)=-1.
   Output: (p,q,sigma,R,A), sigma=1 or 3 according to q mod4, R=sigma q,
   A=(p+R)/4. Retaining sigma gives inverse q=R/sigma. Both vertex types exist;
   the principal new theorems explicitly concern q=3 mod4.

2. **All-factor incidence.** The full factorization A=product r^e supplies every
   edge with (r/p)=-1 and weight e. An edge record retains all other factors,
   so A and the original source reconstruct. Selecting one edge loses the
   alternatives unless they remain separately recorded. The displayed triangle
   has exactly one eligible factor per vertex, not an arbitrarily selected edge.

3. **Centered exponent box to divisors.** beta_r in [-e_r,e_r] maps to
   u=product r^(e_r+beta_r). Inverse beta_r=v_r(u)-e_r; bijective. The fine
   observation is v=u/A modulo R, defined since gcd(A,R)=1.

4. **Original words to fine coefficients.** Group equal fine residues and sum
   their integer weights. The fibre is the entire original exponent list over
   that residue. The linear quotient kernel is generated by differences of
   those actual words. Counting support alone drops multiplicities.

5. **Actual stabilizer quotient.** K={g:gW=W}; Gamma=<-1,2,primes of A>.
   The triangle satisfies Gamma=G and K=G^6 by direct proof. All three labels
   -1,-p,-p^-1 remain; E_- uses beta -> -beta before E reconstruction.
   Coarse membership is valid because W is a FULL union of K-cosets. Coarse
   mass is the sum of fine integer coefficients, not a uniform distribution.

6. **Section plus kernel.** At vertex qi choose successor ri and section
   j -> ri^j (j=0,...,5). A fine g has unique coordinates (j,k),
   k=g ri^-j in K. Multiplication includes (ri^6)^floor((j+l)/6).
   This carry is 1,8,144 respectively. At 307 no homomorphic section exists.
   Changing section has its usual explicit coboundary correction; suppressing
   the kernel or its correction is not an inverse.

7. **Neighbour recurrence.** For the actual cyclic predecessor,
   qprev ri^2 in Ki, with residues 16,196,17 and sixth roots 3,38,20.
   The orientation pKi=riKi is retained. This is a congruence on original
   integers, not a residue-group homomorphism between qi and its successor.

8. **Eisenstein quotient.** pi=a+b*omega with norm qi maps
   Z[omega] -> F_qi by omega -> -a/b modulo qi. Inverse classes are the
   cosets of (pi). The primary factors are 1 modulo 3 in the strong convention.
   Reading only the norm loses the choice of pi versus its conjugate.

9. **Conjugation.** (a,b) -> (a-b,-b), an involution. The rational norm symbol
   equals the product of primary and conjugate cubic symbols. Their exponent
   tables are H and J. H is symmetric; J is antisymmetric; H+J need not be
   symmetric. The finite-field omega images are separately retained.

10. **Six-dimensional algebraic CRT.** F2[X]/(X^6-1) ->
    F2[epsilon]/epsilon^2 x F4[epsilon]/epsilon^2 evaluates at
    1+epsilon and omega(1+epsilon). Its inverse is
    (a+bE)e0+(c(Z)+E d(Z))e1, where e0=1+X^2+X^4,
    e1=X^2+X^4, E=1+X^3, Z=X^4. It is a full unital algebra isomorphism.

11. **Cubic body observation.** F4[epsilon]/epsilon^2 -> F4 drops its epsilon
    coefficient; kernel is epsilon F4. At the actual primitive packet the
    body is zero and the jet equals epsilon. This zero is supported, not tau.
    It does not mean any required original target is occupied.

12. **Squaring.** P=1+X+X^5 satisfies P^2=e0; the cubic jet is killed.
    At the original counting level multiplication takes a pair of source words.
    It uses two active occurrences and their fibres, not the original cap-one
    prime. No source-preserving selector map is asserted.

13. **Square-source creation.** (p,q,j) maps to full residual
    R=(2j+1)^2 q and A=(p+R)/4. Domain requires R<3p. The positive square
    root and q are retained, so the original multiplier returns. Square 9 is
    proved valid at every canonical all-3-mod-4 triangle. This changes the
    original exponent box rather than activating an absent old coefficient.

14. **Unchanged-divisor span.** Between ports i and j, common divisors are
    exactly divisors of gcd(A_i,A_j)^2. Their normalized exponent vectors
    change by v_r(A_i)-v_r(A_j); the residual and gate change too. The source
    gcd is gcd(A_i,(j-i)(i+j+1)). This is the actual intersection domain,
    not a homomorphism of the two full unit groups.

15. **Square-source factor escape.** Factor the new A and retain every
    nonresidue factor with its exponent. At a canonical triangle these factors
    avoid self, canonical successor (source gcd), and predecessor (reciprocity).
    Existence is proved, and factors are below p. No canonical-target hit is
    inferred from departure alone.

16. **Prime-power residual observation.** A divisor at modulus 9q projects
    to a candidate modulo q. Its full inverse also requires the SAME divisor's
    mod9 gate. At q=223 both projected channels have two candidates and no
    candidate returns at 9q. Chinese-remainder compatibility is not permission
    to choose a different beta for its 9-part.

17. **Cycle coefficient inverse.** For the directed triangle with cofactors
    B_i, D=64 product B_i, T_i=1+4B_i+16B_i B_(i+1), h=gcd(T_i).
    Then p=(D-1)/h and qi=T_i/h. All types, ordered cycle marks, factorizations,
    ranges and primality remain tests on the inverse. A compatible integer
    coefficient tuple does not by itself establish a prime cycle.

18. **Original E/M return.** With d=gcd(A,u), h=d^2/u, r=u/d, s=A/d,
    E uses kappa=(pr+s)/R and (A,hs*kappa,phr*kappa); M uses
    lambda=(r+s)/R and (A,phs*lambda,phr*lambda). Inverses are
    u=A^2/(Ry-pA) and u=pA^2/(Ry-pA), with ordered denominators retained.

19. **Finite construction line.** A recorded integer t maps to four linear
    forms for p and the three remaining prime factors. The inverse on the
    displayed image is t=(p-p0)/step. Only t=622 is certified. Primality is
    not transported to all t, and no discovery leastness is asserted.

20. **Certificate DAG.** Exact n-1 factorizations and primitive-order congruences
    return proven prime roots by a finite acyclic recursion. Trial leaves have
    complete explicit bounds. The arithmetic certificate is distinct from the
    preliminary probable-prime/factor-discovery software.
