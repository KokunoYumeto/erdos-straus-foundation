\begin{abstract}
We give an exact closed all-failure triangle in the canonical external-nonresidue
factor graph at a certified prime. All three full and effective stabilizer quotients
are cyclic of order six, and every inactive factor box is saturated. We retain the
original counts, a genuine cubic extension carry, and the primary/conjugate cubic
reciprocity tables. The cubic coefficient packet is $(1,\varepsilon)$ in an explicit
dual-number decomposition; its nonzero jet does not imply target occupancy.
A separately proved square-source theorem forces escape from every canonical
all-$3\bmod4$ triangle; the multipliers $1,9,25$ cannot preserve a closed primitive
component of at most six vertices. These are local counterexamples and escape
results, not a proof or counterexample to the Erd\H{o}s--Straus conjecture.
\end{abstract}
