# Route record for the channel-coupling continuation

The goal was to convert the preceding factor-supply theorems into occupancy of
an original exterior or middle coefficient.

1. **Unchanged-word and square-ratio transport.** Quadratic reciprocity proves
   that the two channels require opposite characters on every negative source.
   Such transports preserve character and therefore cannot cross the channels.
   The successful replacement is the exact involution (u\mapsto a/(4u)) on
   (\operatorname{Div}(a/4)), together with its joined exponent windows.

2. **Immediate occupancy after six produced vertices.** The complete
   (p=315361) six-cycle has empty (E/M) gates at all 17 same-vertex square
   sources.  An expanded edge nevertheless exits to an occupied source.  Thus
   six-vertex factor production is not the same theorem as selector occupancy.

3. **Reciprocal-source repair.** The reciprocal source has an exact return to
   first-half exterior states, but all 22 reciprocal gates in the (p=12889)
   control are empty.  Requiring a common nonresidue factor of the direct and
   reciprocal sources reduces exactly to the already known (3\bmod4) factor
   of (p+1) endpoint construction.

4. **Combined character positivity.** The common pair source couples the two
   channels with complete (2\tau(h)) fibres, but the Jacobi character that
   forced new factors is killed by (|1+\chi(p)|^2).  The remaining exact target
   is positivity of the full first-half opposite-pair sum; no sign is assumed
   for its other character terms.

The continuation therefore ends with a stronger positive structure and three
proved obstructions to proposed shortcuts.  Its next problem is the complete
pair sum at an arbitrary prescribed prime, not another restricted factor family.
