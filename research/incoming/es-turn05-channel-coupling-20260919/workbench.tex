\begin{abstract}
An original divisor involution interchanges the exterior and middle channels.
For a source $a=2^eA$ the associated windows join to length $3e+3$; with one simple
nonresidue prime this gives complete channel counts and a sharp one-unit comparison.
A common $p$-coloured pair source has exact fibres $2\tau(h)$ and gives the complete
first-half count after M\"obius inversion. Its factor-source Jacobi term is annihilated.
Reciprocal-source and six-vertex examples retain empty original coefficients despite
actual factor supply; neither is an ES counterexample. All maps and finite checks
retain their original exponent bounds, channel labels and ordered returns.
\end{abstract}\maketitle
