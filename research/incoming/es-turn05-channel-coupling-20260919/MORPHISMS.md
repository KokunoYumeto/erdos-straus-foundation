# Exact maps, fibres and information loss

1. Original raw exponent box f_l in [0,2e_l] -> u=prod l^f_l. Inverse valuations. Centred beta=f-e. No quotient of prime labels.
2. Original u -> (h,r,s)=(gcd(a,u)^2/u,u/gcd(a,u),a/gcd(a,u)). Inverse u=hr² with a=hrs and gcd(r,s)=1.
3. E/M hit -> ordered denominators as in core (3). Inverses a²/(Ry-pa) and pa²/(Ry-pa); channel is required.
4. Character observation u -> chi_p(u). Its fibre consists of the actual available exponent vectors, not all integers of that character. E/M sign separation applies to negative sources.
5. A bounded same-source rational ratio rho=prod l^delta maps f -> f+delta on the exact interval intersection in (6). Inverse subtracts delta. Gates are preserved across channels only at the specified residue rho=p or p^-1. Squares cannot do this on negative sources.
6. p-coloured divisor map (d,epsilon)->p^epsilon d for d|N, epsilon=0,1. Inverse reads v_p. Assumes p not dividing N.
7. Ordered pair (b,c) -> gcd and coprime pair. Equal p-colours return M with common p-bit; unequal colours return E with p-side bit. Full inverse is (8)-(9). Fibre size 2*tau(h).
8. Fibre counting -> original state mass uses weight 1/(2*tau(h)) on each actual occurrence. No duplicated or independently chosen states.
9. T_R(N)/2 -> F_R(N) via Möbius inversion on the exact divisor poset. Inverse is the positive divisor sum. Auxiliary N retain R and p and are not claimed to satisfy 4N=p+R.
10. Original positive divisor counts -> four-colour 2x2 matrix. Coarse B0/B1 sums lose original pair identities and gcd factors unless the stored fibres are retained. It is not assumed PSD.
11. Integer group-ring to finite character coordinates: complete character transform with inverse orthogonality. Signed local factors and the |1+chi(p)|² kernel retained. The Jacobi term can vanish; that does not imply absence of its original source.
12. Reciprocal w|B_D², D|(w+B_D) -> normalized coprime pair -> recorded swap -> first-half original E state. Inverse D=(4u+1)/R, B=h r kappa and two w orientations.
13. Reciprocal cofactor prime -> original nonresidue graph vertex only when that prime is actually less than p and has character -1. Larger primes remain in source data but outside graph domain.
14. Direct/reciprocal common divisor domain is Div(gcd(A_D,B_D)²). A common nonresidue prime maps to a 3-mod-4 divisor of p+1 and the original endpoint E state. The converse uses D=2ell+1 after the proved size bound.
15. Original source family -> complete first-half tagged count C_p. Existence equivalence uses the inherited first-half completeness theorem; numerical value is not asserted positive for every p.
16. Metadata/character quotient zero retains the original nonempty fibre as supported zero; it is not tau. No inverse from a coarse sign or sum alone is asserted.

17. On the original residue counting space, S_p is translation by p, J_- is translation by -1, and C_p=I+S_p duplicates then sums the two p-colours. T=<C_p f,J_- C_p f>. J_- is an involutive signed target form, not a positive metric. ker(C_p) consists exactly of f(pg)=-f(g); retaining the colour source avoids silently losing those relation coordinates.

return domain among original square divisors. Inverse identical; its ratio changes
character by chi_p(a). Numerical fixed points a=4u² retain both distinct E/M tags.
19. The odd-divisor packet of a=2^e A uses E:(w,j)->2^(-j-2)w and

The shared exponent window pairs original words with producta/4. Overlap counts

20. For a=Br with a unique simple nonresidue prime, E usesu=rv and M usesu=v or
a²/v withv|B². This is a complete original factor-source reduction. The two M
orientations are distinct; the E/M fine targets are -p^-1 and-r on mu_B.
21. For B=ell^e, the quotient log is only taken inside the actual cyclic subgroup.
Its full fibres are the bounded integer intervals, with each representative kept.
If4 is not in the subgroup, its nontrivial coset is not given a fictitious log.
22. Reduction e -> e mod ord2 preserves only the corresponding period formula;
when e varies at fixedR,A, p_e varies. It is not same-p witness transport.
