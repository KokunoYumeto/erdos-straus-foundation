# What we tried, and why

The aim is to resolve Erdős–Straus while developing the exact structural
connections that motivated the programme. The owner's broader motivation is
interdisciplinary: arithmetic obstructions suggested connections to geometry,
coloured structures and modular mathematics. A resemblance motivates a calculation;
the calculation must determine the actual map and its arithmetic return.

These short accounts cover five routes in the published 72-statement supplement,
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22678971](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22678971).
They are a reading aid alongside the [full research map](WORKBENCH.md) and proofs.
The route-specific heuristic explanations below are editorial reconstructions
from those constructions, not quotations of an original thought process.

[Recovered human directions](MOTIVATIONS.md) now provide a separate, directly
sourced record from the earlier web conversations. They restore historical
intent without inventing a theorem-by-theorem lineage for these five routes.

## 1. Full-shell occupancy and factor sieves

The later [12 September attempt account](research/continuation-2026-09-12/README.md#what-the-approaches-achieve)
records the new joint Boolean, character-energy and geometric work, its
motivations, complete proofs and exact failed strengthenings. The five
published-supplement routes below retain their original edition scope.

**Aim:** understand exactly what an ES counterexample would have to evade.
**Reason for trying:** factor exponents retain more arithmetic information than
a coarse prime congruence. We constructed full divisor packets, ordered witness
maps and factor tests for residuals three and seven. The tests work exactly for
those shells; the full packet gives a primewise biconditional. **Unfinished:**
this does not show that some shell is occupied at every prime. The per-shell
small-selector failures are retained, not mistaken for ES counterexamples.
[Full source and proof](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/counterexample_sieve/all_shell_no_hit.tex)
· [factor tests](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/first_two_shell_sieve.tex).

## 2. Simultaneous constraints and bounded CRT

**Aim:** turn the colleague's linked conditions into one exact common-tuple
problem. **Reason for trying:** CRT organizes simultaneous congruences, but the
same exponent vector must satisfy them inside its original bounds. We calculated
lattice obstructions, bounded fibres, Boolean coordinates and input/prime-output
incidence. These give complete finite algorithms and explicit witness return.
**Unfinished:** neither integral compatibility alone nor the input construction
forces an occupied bounded fibre or a suitable prime output. Both kinds of
failed inference have explicit examples.
[Full source and proof](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/shared_variable_crt.tex)
· [incidence](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/input_output_incidence.tex).

## 3. Witness propagation and exact first codes

**Aim:** propagate solutions structurally, retaining their order, factor scale
and original codes. **Reason for trying:** a proved transport law could connect
many witnesses without a fresh search each time. We classified ratio maps,
positive shears, fixed-denominator successors, ray returns and four swap/tag
candidates. Their exact integer domains and failed returns are known. An occupied
residual-three shell yields a proved global first code. **Unfinished:** these
domains do not yet supply universal occupancy; passing adjacent positive shears
cannot form a two-step path.
[Full source and proof](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/boundary_swap/boundary_swap_completion.tex)
· [raw-scale transport](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/raw_scale_hit_incidence.tex).

## 4. Divisor categories and cyclic coordinates

**Aim:** connect the same arithmetic to Connes–Consani constructions by explicit
maps. **Reason for trying:** embedded ordered groups retain finite divisor data,
while cyclic characters can detect the integral gluing obstruction. We proved
the intersections, reciprocal returns, primitive marks, affine shear correction
and integral cyclic character correspondence. These are exact interfaces with
recoverable arithmetic data. **Unfinished:** detecting the integral obstruction
does not force the original bounded intersection to be occupied. The uncorrected
map's failure led to a stronger corrected map, not a claim of unrelatedness.
[Full source and proof](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/connes_reading/connes_primitive_intersections.tex)
· [cyclic coordinates](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/integral_cyclic_bridge.tex).

## 5. Auxiliary torus, sampling and directional inverse

**Aim:** transport arithmetic character packets through the cited auxiliary torus
and return the calculation to its operators. **Reason for trying:** finite
covering fibres make precise what averaging preserves. We found the extra terms
from unchanged-grid sampling, repaired them by complete preimages and correction
cosets, and proved continuous transfer and sharp directional inverse estimates.
The corrected packets preserve the original counts exactly. **Unfinished:** this
does not construct a fluid solution from a prime, force ES occupancy, or verify
the complete announced fluid construction.
[Full source and proof](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/ns_operator_bridge/torus_cover_lemma.tex)
· [inverse estimates](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/ns_operator_bridge/reverse_smooth_inverse.tex)
· [human-source attribution](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/ns_operator_bridge/ATTRIBUTION.md).

## Keeping future attempts intelligible

For each task or substantial route, retain a short account with these same fields:
aim, broader motivation, heuristic reason, what was tried, what happened, exact
successful scope, and unfinished scope. Link its complete dated source. Mark
inferred motivation as inferred. Record an unfinished attempt as unfinished rather
than quietly dropping it. The account describes the work; it need not prescribe
what another researcher should try next.

The [machine records](polyclank/attempts.json) expose these fields separately.
This first pass covers the five routes above; it does not claim to have recovered
every attempt in every historical task or neighbouring workbench.
