# Human ideas behind the research

The programme's questions did not begin with the latest supplement. Earlier
human–AI discussions contain the reasons for studying positivity, residual
propagation, coloured algebraic structures and geometric correspondences.
Six passages have now been recovered from the human contributor's actual
messages in two web exports, not reconstructed from later assistant summaries.
Attribution is collective: **The Clankers, human research contribution**.

## Directions recovered from May 2026

In *Mathematical Framework Construction*, the human contributor asked for
finer sign information: “Either see if you can find any of the tables or forms
where some sneaky positivity condition is hiding, or adjoin a sort of completed
Tesserine structure to this” (line 13235). The continuation of that same message
specifies four-way rather than two-way symmetry. This was a request to preserve
more distinctions while searching for positivity, not to discard the original
arithmetic in a new notation.

A more localized suggestion was: “consider blade 1 - that's the only
non-symmetric one so any positivity should live there” (line 15455).
That records a proposed place to investigate the positivity mechanism; it does
not certify the claim inside the suggestion.

The target was explicitly not limited to isolated examples: “generalize to all R
by induction the proof already given for the isolated late one” (line 17675).
The same message also requested an explicit lift of the root-system and
Hauptmodul calculations through the quadratic tessarine structure. Establishing
that induction, rather than merely collecting more residual examples, was the
research aim. This historical request is not evidence that it was achieved.

The colour extension was described as “just colour + parity type grading as
oposed to just parity” (line 30756). At line 33106 the contributor explained
the origin: a Y-junction picture from string theory and QCD centre symmetry
had motivated the star/snowflake construction. They explicitly rejected replacing
that direction with any available three-way symmetry: “So it is not the same
as just the word Z3.” The requested work was to calculate the actual finer
distinctions and their maps. Neither the physical heuristic nor the shared
number three alone establishes the proposed correspondence.

## A later question about information lost in projection

In *Mathematical Functor Construction*, July 2026, the contributor asked whether
the proposed third direction could occur with either sign:

> and then I suppose asking - I'd guess "are tere any negaivve ones?" i.e. "(0,0,1) or (0,0,-1) as well?

This is line 9432, within the question at lines 9428–9432. The preceding message
at line 9420 links the concern to an earlier false ES proof using projection.
The direction was to retain the missing coordinate and test it, not accept a
projected positivity argument as sufficient. The coordinates in this historical
proposal still need their definitions and calculations from the original work;
the quotation is not an established counterexample.

## How these ideas relate to the present workbench

These records restore why structural positivity, propagation across residuals
and information-preserving maps mattered to the project. The present
[shell and transport work](RESEARCH_STATE.md) pursues related objectives.
A specific theorem-by-theorem derivation from the old tessarine/colour programme
to every current result is **not yet reconstructed**. That historical connection
must be traced through the actual intervening calculations, not inferred from
matching vocabulary. The [attempt accounts](ATTEMPTS.md) retain their separately
labelled editorial motivations until those source-specific connections are read.

The recovery is partial. It covers six human passages, not all messages, export
versions or historical programmes. The full 488-page reader and its source
archives remain [available separately](README.md#start-reading). Raw web exports
and private conversation archives have not been dumped into this public repository.
