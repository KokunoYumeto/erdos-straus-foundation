# Licences and reuse

This workbench contains material from different editions and contributors.
There is no blanket relicensing of its contents.

## Existing material

The complete expanded supplement retains its
[CC BY 4.0 licence](research/expanded-2026-09-08/exact_bounded_transport_72/LICENSE.md).
Earlier archives, third-party sources, imported tools and formal libraries retain
their own notices. A new contribution cannot waive somebody else's rights.
Private transcripts are not licensed for public redistribution merely because
the project uses them to recover its history.

## New contributions

The default offer for new original research text and metadata is
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode),
to the extent the contributor holds the relevant rights and chooses that
dedication. This applies to the newly authored RESEARCH_STATE.md and this
LICENSING.md, not retrospectively to the imported corpus. AI assistance does
not automatically relicense human or third-party expression.

The default for new standalone software is
[MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit), with its notice attached to the
particular tool. Existing code and dependencies keep their licences.
The new [orientation checker](tools/verify_orientation.py) carries its
[own MIT notice](tools/VERIFY_ORIENTATION_LICENSE.txt).

Contributors may choose another explicit licence. A peer's CC BY, CC BY-SA or
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
work can be linked with that choice recorded; linking it does not convert it
to CC0 or grant incompatible reuse rights. When copying or adapting such work,
retain and follow the actual terms. If no permission to redistribute is known,
link and cite the work rather than copying it. Peer discovery does not require
every linked artifact to have the same licence.

## Credit is preserved

CC0 itself does not impose a copyright-based attribution condition. This
workbench nevertheless keeps source and contribution credit as a research
practice: who suggested a direction, supplied a source, derived a result or
found a correction. The contributor may select a name, pseudonym or collective
credit. Neither licence choice nor credit represents endorsement of every
downstream claim. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
